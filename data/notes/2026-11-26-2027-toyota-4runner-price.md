Toyota [announced the 2027 4Runner](https://pressroom.toyota.com/the-2027-toyota-4runner-legendary-capability-refined-for-the-road-ahead) on November 25. The headline number is the price. The SR5 starts at $43,490 before destination. With the $1,595 destination charge it's $45,085, which [CarBuzz](https://carbuzz.com/2027-toyota-4runner-pricing-revealed/) says is the first time a 4Runner has started above $45,000. CarBuzz puts the SR5's increase at $1,220 over the 2026.

Here's what that money buys. Every grade now gets heated front seats and 8-way power seats for the driver and passenger. Per CarBuzz, the heated seats are new on the SR5, and the power seats are new on the SR5, TRD Sport and TRD Off-Road. The TRD Pro gets a new color called Hot Honey (a $695 option, per CarBuzz) and red front recovery hooks. The Platinum gets puddle lamps with the 4Runner logo. "Inked" replaces Black.

That's the list. The engines are the same: the 2.4-liter turbo four with 278 hp, and the i-FORCE MAX hybrid with 326 hp and 465 lb-ft. So is the TNGA-F frame, and so is the 6,000 lb tow rating. CarBuzz's trim table shows the grades that gained no equipment going up about $200 or less, and the hybrid TRD Pro now listing at $70,465 with destination.

## Why it matters
The 4Runner used to be the sensible one, the Toyota you bought because the Land Cruiser was too much money. A $70,000 TRD Pro isn't sensible anymore. At that price you're shopping alongside the [[land-cruiser/250-series|Land Cruiser 250]] and the [[lexus/gx550|Lexus GX 550]], which ride on the same platform.

## My take
Heated seats on an SR5 are welcome. Nobody should have to pay extra for a warm seat in a truck built for snow and trailheads. But this is a model-year bump, not a new truck. If you were happy with a 2026 last month, a 2026 is still the same 4Runner, minus heated seats on the SR5 and power seats on a few grades.

I own a 2024 4Runner, the last year of the fifth gen, and I've owned three before it (2002, 2007 and 2016). Toyota knows the 4Runner sells whatever it costs. That's exactly why you should make the dealer work for it.
