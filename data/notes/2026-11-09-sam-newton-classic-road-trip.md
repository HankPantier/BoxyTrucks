Filmmaker Sam Newton just put out the best argument for buying an old 4x4 I've seen this year, and it isn't a buyer's guide. [**We Bought Classic Cars and Road Tripped America**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=im58tofq75s) follows five friends and three old trucks on a 10-day, 2,000-mile run from LA through Utah and on to a cliffside camp in Arizona, while they shoot a campaign for Canon USA along the way. The trucks are two 60 Series Land Cruisers and an early Bronco.

It goes wrong immediately. On day zero, before they've left LA, one of the trucks has transmission trouble. Later a truck gets stuck for two hours in 100-degree heat, and a Land Cruiser breaks down on the road. They keep going anyway.

## Why it matters
Nobody in the film talks about horsepower, resale value or towing capacity. It's about what these trucks are for: getting somewhere slowly, with friends, and fixing things when they break. That's the feeling that makes people search for an FJ60 or an early Bronco in the first place.

## My take
This video is 100% the kind of vibe Boxy Trucks is about. I owned a 60 Series, and I still regret selling it.

The breakdowns are the honest part. A 40-year-old truck will break on a 2,000-mile trip. The difference between a good trip and a bad one is buying a truck that's been sorted, carrying the right spares, and travelling with people who laugh when it happens.

## What it means if you're shopping
- **Want the 60?** Start with rust: the rear frame rails first, then the floor under the rear seat. Our [[land-cruiser/fj60|FJ60 guide]] covers the 2F six, the smog equipment and which four-speed you have. If you want fuel injection and square headlights, look at the [[land-cruiser/fj62|FJ62]].
- **Want the Bronco?** Rust is everywhere, even on dry-state trucks. Check whether the rear fenders are cut, check the frame at the steering box, and find out who built it. The [[bronco/early-bronco|early Bronco guide]] has the full checklist.
- **Not sure which year?** [Every early Bronco year](/models/bronco/early-bronco/years/) from 1966 to 1977, and what changed.
- **Planning the trip?** Do the [inspection checklist](/models/land-cruiser/fj60/problems/) before you go, not in a parking lot in Utah.
