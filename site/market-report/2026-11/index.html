Boxy market report · November 2026

What boxy 4x4s actually sold for: November 2026

431 Bring a Trailer completed auctions that ended October 25 – November 24, 2026, sorted by generation. 312 sold and 119 didn't meet reserve.

What the numbers say

  • The 80 and 100 Series are the easiest trucks to sell. 17 of 18 80 Series sold (median $26,500), and all 15 100 Series sold (median $22,250). A 25k-mile 2004 100 Series brought $65,000.
  • Half the classic Defenders didn't sell. 33 Defender 90/110/130s were listed but only 16 met reserve. The median sale was $43,250, so sellers are asking for more than buyers will pay.
  • Early Broncos are a restomod market. 19 of 33 sold at a $72,000 median. A Coyote-swapped Velocity build made $240,000, the top price this month.
  • The cheapest way into a G-Class is still an early W463. Three G 500s sold for $20,250–32,100, against a $66,750 median for 2009–18 G 550s and $144,000 for the 4×4².
  • Wrangler JK sellers are optimistic. 10 of 17 didn't sell. The ones that did went for $5,600–20,000.
