Nissan Patrol Y62 / Armada problems to check before you buy

  1. Shift qualityCarSwitch says the RE7R01A seven-speed in early Y62s (2010–2014) has a reputation for valve-body problems, costing AED 5,000–12,000 to fix in the UAE. Test for harsh shifts, delayed engagement and shudder between second and third. One Australian owner reported his Y62 holding gears under hard acceleration while towing, even after a warranty transmission.
  2. Signs of a hard desert lifeCarSwitch's desert checklist: gouged skid plates and crossmembers, compacted sand in the frame rails and behind the inner guard liners, fine sand in the air filter, and ABS, stability or TPMS warnings on a cold start.
  3. Patrol or Armada?A US Armada has no rear locker, no disconnecting sway bars and no HBMC (AutoGuide). If you want a locker, you're shopping for a Patrol, and a Y62 Patrol isn't old enough to import into the US under the 25-year rule.
  4. HBMC and lift kitsIf the truck has HBMC, ask what's been done to it. One owner found HBMC front springs only come as complete shock-and-spring assemblies, and HBMC-specific kits such as Ontrack's are designed to fit without disturbing the system. Look for wet hydraulic lines and fittings at each corner and a truck that sits level.
  5. Modifications and warrantyCars.co.za advises avoiding heavily modified examples. One NissanPatrol.com.au member was told by his dealer that drivetrain problems wouldn't be covered after a lift.
  6. Towing historyOwners report the rear of an HBMC truck sagging under heavy ball weights. Many towing rigs have airbag kits designed for HBMC. A transmission cooler or deep pan is a good sign on a truck that has towed.
  7. Armada recallsCheck the VIN with NHTSA. The 2020–2021 Armada fuel-pump recall (21V373) replaces a pump whose impeller can swell and stall the engine. The 2026 campaign 26V455 corrects the axle-weight label on Armadas with 22-inch wheels.
  8. Brakes and tyresIt's a heavy truck (a Warrior weighs 2,884 kg). Cars.co.za notes the weight wears brakes and tyres quickly, so check pad and rotor thickness and tyre wear before you price the truck.

Nissan Patrol Y62 / Armada recalls and service bulletins

  • Rear-view camera image can be turned off ↗ 2018–2019 Armada · NHTSA 19V-654Camera display settings could hide the rear-view image, failing FMVSS 111. Dealers updated the software.
  • Fuel pump impeller can swell and bind ↗ 2020–2021 Armada (V8) · NHTSA 21V-373The fuel pump can fail and stall the engine. Dealers replace the fuel pump module (Nissan R21A2).
  • Incorrect axle weight rating on certification label ↗ 2020–2026 Armada with 22-in wheels · NHTSA 26V-455The label's gross axle weight rating is wrong, which could lead to overloading. Dealers fit new labels.

Check any VIN for open recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls ↗, or see the recall tracker.

Owner questions about Nissan Patrol Y62 / Armada reliability

Which years should I buy or avoid?

CarSwitch, a UAE used-car marketplace, rates 2015–2017 trucks as the value sweet spot and the 2020–2021 facelifts as the best recent buys. Perth dealer Westside Auto calls the 2020-on facelift Patrols the most desirable on the used market. CarSwitch suggests caution with 2010–2012 trucks because of transmission valve-body problems and early fit-and-finish issues. In the US, run any 2020–2021 Armada's VIN for the fuel-pump recall.
