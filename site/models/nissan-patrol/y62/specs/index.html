Fluids, capacities & service intervals

A used Y62 has usually done one of three jobs: school runs, desert weekends or towing a caravan across Australia. Baseline everything, and pay special attention to the transmission and transfer-case fluids, which are model-specific. The oil, plug, diff and brake-fluid specs below are from Nissan USA's owner's manuals for the Armada, the US-market Y62. Patrols sold elsewhere may specify different grades, so check your own manual.
