Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing has been done unless there are receipts. On a GQ, the jobs that matter most are the cooling system, TD42 tappets, a timing belt on RD28T and RB30 engines, gear oils (with LSD oil in the rear), and the front swivels, which run in grease and quietly fill with diff oil when the inner seals fail. Capacities below are owner-reported figures from forum threads (grades quoted from Gregory's), so confirm them in a workshop manual for your engine.
