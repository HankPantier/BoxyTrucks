Maintenance schedule · 1987–1997

Nissan Patrol Y60 (GQ) maintenance schedule

Nissan's GQ service schedule isn't published online, so this page gives you the baseline to do on a truck that's now 29 to 39 years old, the intervals owners and specialists report, and the checks that catch the GQ's known faults early.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

We couldn't find Nissan's factory GQ schedule online, so nothing here is a factory interval. The first row is a baseline to do at purchase; the rest are intervals owners and specialists report. Check the service book if you have it.

WHENWHAT TO DO
At purchase (no records)
  • Engine oil and filter, fuel filter, air filter
  • Gearbox, transfer case, both diffs (rear is an LSD on many trucks: use LSD oil)
  • Coolant flush, hoses and belts if original or soft
  • Brake fluid flush; inspect pads, shoes and hoses
  • Inspect the swivel hubs and front wheel bearings; repack and reseal if there's oil or play
  • Automatic (RE4R03A): drain and fill if there's no record
  • Petrol: spark plugs, coil lead and distributor cap (Carsales says the coil leads corrode and crack the cap)
About every 20,000 km
  • TD42: check and set the tappets (one forum member's interval; he sets 0.35 mm at ambient)
About 100,000 km
  • RD28T and RB30: timing belt, tensioner and idler (ExplorOz owners' figure; TD42 and TB42 have gear-driven cams, no belt)
After every deep water crossing
  • Front and rear diffs, transfer case and gearbox: check for water
  • Swivel hubs and front wheel bearings: repack

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Towing, dust and sand work the TD42's cooling hardest: Carsales notes the TD42 ran hot even naturally aspirated, especially in soft beach sand. Keep the radiator, fan clutch and coolant in top condition, and change oil and filters more often than you would on a road car.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover

ITEMWHENWHY
Rear axle sealsCheck at every serviceThe GQ's most common fault per Carsales; oil on the backing plate or brakes means seals.
Panhard rod and radius arm bushesWhen the steering shimmies or wandersWorn bushes are a common cause of the 70–80 km/h shimmy.
Rear coil springs and body mountsInspect, especially on towing trucksOwners report sagging springs and worn body mounts on hard-worked GQs.
Fuel tankInspect around the drain plugCarsales reports cracked tanks around the drain plug on hard-worked Patrols.
SeatsCheck recliners and runnersCarsales says broken recliner mechanisms and floor runners are common on worked trucks.
