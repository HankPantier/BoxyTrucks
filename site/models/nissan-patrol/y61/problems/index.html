Exercise your lockers

Engage the lockers every six months or so, on almost any boxy truck. Both of my locker problems, on the 80 Series and the G500, came from systems that weren’t used often enough.

Nissan Patrol GU (Y61) problems to check before you buy

  1. Identify the engine and series firstMake sure the engine and gearbox match the build date and series. A 'Series III ZD30' and a 'Series IV TB48' are different buys. A ZD30 sold as 'overhauled' should come with an itemised list: head, bores, pistons, bearings and rings, not just new pistons and a turbo.
  2. ZD30: demand oil history and check the dipstickLook for regular oil changes and the right grade: a September 2004 Nissan bulletin, quoted by owners, calls for ACEA B3 or JASO DH-1 oil with 10W-40 strongly recommended. Nissan Europe's Y61 service manual schedules ZD30 oil and filter every 15,000 km or 12 months, or every 7,500 km or 6 months in severe use such as towing, dust or short trips. Check your own market's service book; many owners change it more often. Fuel in the oil is bad news. One forum veteran says heavy soot within 5,000 km points to an open EGR, and another warns that factory intercoolers eventually leak, so check for oil staining. Listen for knocks on a cold start.
  3. Cooling under loadEvery GU engine can overheat when towing, and the TD42 has a particular reputation for it. Check the radiator core, the foam seals between condenser and radiator, and the fan clutch (it should drag and turn only about half a revolution when cold). Watch the temperature gauge on a long climb.
  4. Manual gearboxDrive through every gear. Owners report fifth-gear spline and hub problems and worn 2nd-to-3rd synchros on the five-speed.
  5. Rear axle seals, diffs and underbodyPut it on a hoist. Look for wet rear backing plates from leaking axle seals, bash plates pushed up into what they're meant to protect, and damage to sills and bumpers. Find out whether the rear diff is an H233B or an H260 if you plan to fit a locker.
  6. Cab-chassis: look behind the cabExplorOz members report cracks in GU cab-chassis frames just behind the cab and near the rear axle on heavily loaded trays, repaired by plating. Check under any big service body.
  7. Rear barn door and dashOwners report cracks in the big rear door that carries the spare, down the side of the window on the left-hand side. Dashboard tops can blister; Nissan Japan extended the warranty to seven years on specified wagons built August 2004 to June 2007.
  8. Previous lift workA lifted GU that wanders or steers vaguely probably needs caster correction. Lifts of about 3 in or more also need longer panhard rods, drag-link changes and longer brake lines. Ask what was fitted and whether it was certified.

Nissan Patrol GU (Y61) recalls and service bulletins

  • ZD30 oil capacity and dipstick change ↗ After piston failures on the 3.0 L ZD30, Nissan raised the oil fill from 6 L to 8.3 L and had dealers shorten and recalibrate the dipsticks (CarsGuide).
  • ZD30 oil specification revised (Sept 2004) ↗ Owners on ExplorOz quote Nissan service bulletin MA-04-001 (30 September 2004): ZD30 oil must meet ACEA B3 or JASO DH-1, with 10W-40 strongly recommended, retrospectively for all ZD30 engines.
  • Common-rail ZD30 injection components ↗ Nissan's 2007 manual lists a warranty-repair bulletin for Bosch diesel-injection components on the common-rail engine.
  • Dashboard blistering (Japan) ↗ Nissan Japan extended the warranty to seven years for blistering dashboards on specified wagons built August 2004 to June 2007.

Check any VIN for open recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls ↗, or see the recall tracker.
