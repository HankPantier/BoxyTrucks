Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Every 160 is at least 37 years old and most have worked hard, so assume every oil is old and every grease point is dry. We couldn't find factory capacities published for the 160 online. Treat the specs below as types, and check quantities in the Nissan 160/61-series workshop manual.
