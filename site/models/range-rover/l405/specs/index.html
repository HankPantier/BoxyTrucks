Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Baseline everything, and before anything else run the VIN through Land Rover's recall lookup (the 2014–17 knuckle recall alone is worth it). Land Rover calls the ZF eight-speed 'filled for life' and gives the diffs and transfer box exact wet-fill volumes, so treat the official capacities below as the target and measure what goes in.
