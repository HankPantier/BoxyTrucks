Maintenance schedule · 2002–2012

Land Rover Range Rover L322 maintenance schedule

The L322 had several official schedules over its life. Land Rover North America's 2006–08 schedule is the clearest: oil every 7,500 miles or six months, with long intervals for the driveline. Land Rover's 2012 European plan stretched oil to 15,000 miles. Specialist Atlantic British recommends changing the transmission, transfer case and diffs every 60,000 miles, far sooner than Land Rover's 150,000.

Service schedule

Land Rover North America 2006–08 schedule and Land Rover owner guides, as quoted in our buyer's baseline; Atlantic British's specialist intervals noted. Trucks with the service indicator follow it. Check the book for your year and engine.

WHENWHAT TO DO
Every 7,500 mi / 6 months (LRNA 2006–08)
  • Engine oil and filter (2012 European plan: 15,000 mi / 12 months; owners often do 10,000 or sooner)
Every 15,000 mi / 12 months
  • Cabin (pollen) filter
Every 3 years
  • Brake fluid, regardless of distance
Every 60,000 mi (Atlantic British)
  • Automatic transmission fluid and filter
  • Transfer case
  • Front and rear differentials
Every 60,000 mi / 48 months (LRNA)
  • Engine air filter (far sooner on dusty roads)
105,000 mi (LRNA 2006–08)
  • Spark plugs
  • Supercharger belt (supercharged trucks)
150,000 mi or 10 years (LRNA)
  • Transmission fluid and filter (Land Rover's own interval)
  • Drive belts and the petrol fuel filter
  • Electronic rear diff (also at 75,000)
Every 10 years (petrols)
  • Coolant (the older owner guide gives 4 years for diesels)

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Land Rover says arduous use (dust, towing, off-road) needs more frequent servicing; change the air filter far sooner than 60,000 miles on dirt roads.
