Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing was done. The L322 spans five petrol and diesel engines and three transfer boxes, so confirm exactly what you have (engine, model year, open or locking rear diff, NV225 or DD295 transfer box) before ordering a single fluid. Land Rover's differentials and DD295 transfer box take an exact measured fill, not 'fill until it drips', and the friction-modified fluids are not interchangeable.
