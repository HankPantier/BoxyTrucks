Range Rover L460

The story

On 26 October 2021, Gerry McGovern pulled the covers off the fifth Range Rover at the Royal Opera House in London — a suitably theatrical venue for a truck that is mostly about refinement. The L460 keeps the family silhouette and the two-speed transfer box, then piles on things no Range Rover has had before: a seven-seat option, four-wheel steering, plug-in hybrids with a real electric range, a BMW-sourced V8 and, from the 2027 model year in America, a fully electric version. It is the most technically ambitious Range Rover yet, and its first few years brought a long list of recalls to match.

MLA-FLEX AND THE BIG STRETCH

The L460 rides on JLR's MLA-Flex architecture, shared with the third-generation Range Rover Sport. It grew in every direction over the L405: 53 mm longer, 63 mm wider and 35 mm taller, on a wheelbase 22 mm longer at 2,997 mm. The long-wheelbase version adds another 200 mm between the axles, and it is the only body that offers seven seats. JLR claims torsional rigidity is up 50 percent to 50,000 Nm per degree, and a steel closed-section front bulkhead cuts road noise entering the cabin by about 24 percent.

Off-road hardware survived the luxury push. US-spec trucks still list a twin-speed transfer box with high and low range, Terrain Response 2, All Terrain Progress Control and hill descent control. Upper trims get an Electronic Active Differential with torque vectoring, while the SE makes do with an open rear diff and torque vectoring by braking. New tricks include all-wheel steering, the first on a Land Rover, which gives a 10.95 m turning circle. An intelligent AWD system can also disconnect the front axle between 35 and 160 km/h to cut drag losses by 30 percent.

SIX, EIGHT, PLUG, BATTERY

Engines are all electrified to some degree. The core is JLR's 3.0-litre Ingenium straight-six, as a 400 PS mild-hybrid petrol, as 249–350 PS mild-hybrid diesels outside the US, or as a plug-in hybrid with a 38.2 kWh battery and a 105 kW motor. The P440e and P510e launched the PHEVs, and the P550e replaced the P440e for the 2024 model year. The V8 is BMW's twin-turbo 4.4. Early P530s used the N63, and from the 2024 model year JLR switched to the newer S68, which also powers the 615 PS SV. In EPA testing a 2023 P530 managed 16 mpg city and 21 highway, the P400 18 and 26, and the P440e 51 MPGe on electricity.

Then the big one. Range Rover Electric is available to order in the US as a 2027 model, with twin 260 kW motors, 660 lb-ft, an 800-volt battery architecture and an EPA range of 333 miles. Land Rover is careful to say it is still a Range Rover. It quotes a 35-inch wading depth and 5,511 lb of towing, and the battery sits behind a flat, re-engineered underbody meant to cope with sliding over obstacles.

GROWING PAINS

The launch was rough. Owners on FullFatRR report early cars delivered with downgraded audio and other options because JLR couldn't get the parts. Then came the recalls. NHTSA lists, among others, a blocked cam-carrier oil channel (2020–2023 trucks), badly welded second-row seat frames on 2022–2023 seven-seaters, damaged tail-light links, and 2024 trucks with body panels that may not have been bonded properly. UK owners also reported a rear-subframe cracking recall in 2023. The headline problem now is the 48-volt to 12-volt DC-DC converter on mild hybrids: when it fails, the 12-volt battery stops charging. NHTSA recall 26V248000 covers 2020–2024 Range Rovers with an interim software fix.

None of that changes what it is on a trail: a very wide, very heavy luxury car with low range, clever traction electronics and air suspension that does its job well. What it lacks is an aftermarket. A 2025 FullFatRR thread asking for sliders and underbody protection was told to budget €3,500–4,000 for a fabrication shop. Owners weighing one for African dirt roads were pointed at a Defender or a Land Cruiser 300. Treat the L460 as a superb gravel-and-green-lane machine, and keep it away from rock gardens.

SIDEBAR · RANGE ROVER ELECTRIC

From −40 °F to the Arabian Desert

Before putting the electric L460 on sale, Land Rover ran it through what it calls one of its most arduous testing programmes. Product engineering director Thomas Müller took it into −40 °F conditions in the Arctic Circle, and test driver Yakub Dino drove it into the heat of the Arabian Desert.

The result: 344 nickel-manganese-cobalt cells stacked in two layers under a flat underbody, twin in-house 260 kW motors, a 35-inch wading depth, and single-pedal driving that works with Terrain Response to bring the truck to a stop on any surface. It's a 333-mile EPA-rated Range Rover that can still wade a ford. Spen King would probably want to know where the V8 went.

How it’s built

The L460 is a mixed-material monocoque on JLR's MLA-Flex platform, built for electrification from the start: mild-hybrid, plug-in and battery-electric versions share the same body. It keeps the Range Rover essentials, with air suspension, permanent AWD and a two-speed transfer case on combustion models, then adds rear-wheel steering, a disconnecting front axle and a lot of software. For a hands-on owner, that means fewer bolt-on problems and more electrical ones. A capable scan tool is no longer optional.

Buying one: what to check

  1. 01
    Run the VIN against every recallNHTSA lists a stack of campaigns: a cam-carrier oil channel (2020–2023), 7-seat second-row frame welds (2022–2023), tail-light links (2022–2023), rear-camera water ingress (2022–2023), body panels not bonded properly (2024), transmission oil pressure (2024), a cracking oil filter housing (2024), the driver's airbag clockspring connector (2022–2026), and the DC-DC converter (2020–2024). Get written confirmation that they're closed.
  2. 02
    Ask about the DC-DC converterOn mild hybrids, a failing 48-volt to 12-volt converter shows 'Stop – electrical fault' with a red battery light and stops charging the 12-volt battery. Owners say it may log no fault and clear itself, only to return. Dealers have had cars waiting weeks for back-ordered modules. NHTSA recall 26V248000 is an interim software update, with a final remedy to follow.
  3. 03
    Check that it goes to sleepFlat 12-volt batteries after a few days parked are a recurring forum topic. Dealers have fixed some with door-module and other software updates, and one owner traced the same symptom to a brake-pedal security lock that kept the car from sleeping. Ask whether it has needed jump-starts, and whether any aftermarket immobiliser or tracker is fitted.
  4. 04
    Check the build spec on early trucksOwners say many early (2022) trucks were delivered with downgraded audio and other options during the parts shortage. Check the car against its original order or a parts catalogue before paying for options it doesn't have.
  5. 05
    Know which V8 you're gettingP530s built for 2022–2023 use BMW's N63 twin-turbo V8, while 2024-on trucks got the newer S68. Forum owners rate the S68 as significantly more refined. It's also the engine in the SV P615.
  6. 06
    Look at the service history, not just the stampsService intervals were up to 21,000 miles or 24 months, and owners report the 2024 model year D350 dropped to 10,500 miles or 12 months. Many owners change the oil more often than the schedule asks. A truck run to the maximum on short urban trips deserves extra scrutiny.
  7. 07
    Check software and connectivityUpdates arrive over the air, but one 2022 owner whose truck was stuck on an old version found reports of a telematics (TCU) problem on some 2022–23 trucks. Check the software version against Land Rover's published releases, and confirm the remote app works.
  8. 08
    Mind the wheel bolts and wheel sizeThe L460 uses wheel bolts, not the studs and nuts of the L405, so older Range Rover wheels need the correct flat-seat bolts. Owners say 22- and 23-inch wheels ride harshly on broken roads. For trail use, a smaller wheel with more sidewall is the better bet.
Post-purchase baseline

After you buy it

The L460 is new enough that most trucks will have dealer history. Still, the first job is the recall and software status, because several known faults (DC-DC converter, battery drain) are fixed by updates. Fluid specs differ by powertrain (Ingenium I6 diesel or petrol, BMW V8, PHEV) and the BorgWarner transfer box takes its own fluid, so read the owner's handbook for your VIN rather than reusing L405 figures.

For the hands-on owner

Common repairs

The jobs owners actually end up doing, rated for a competent home mechanic with a decent tool kit. Always confirm torque figures and procedures against the factory service manual for your VIN.

Rear brake pads (electric parking brake service mode)
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · [TO RESEARCH]SHOP COST · Parts plus labor
SYMPTOMSA pad-wear warning (one owner's P440e needed rear pads at 31,000 miles, another owner's at 16,000), or grinding at the rear.
THE FIXThe rear calipers use an electric parking brake, so put the brake into service (maintenance) mode before you push the pistons back. Shops do this with a scan tool. With the truck supported and the wheels off, remove the caliper, swap the pads (one owner fitted genuine pads and found a single wear sensor), refit, then take the brake out of service mode and check it applies properly.

DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · [TO RESEARCH]SHOP COST · Auxiliary stop/start battery about $288 (Lucky8); parts plus labor
SYMPTOMS'Low battery, please start engine' messages, a truck that is electrically dead after a few days parked, or alarm and keyless-entry glitches.
THE FIXFirst rule out software (door-module and RFA updates have fixed some drains) and anything keeping the car awake. Then load-test the 12-volt battery. On a 2024 D350, one owner found the lithium-ion starter battery under the driver's seat, only removable with the truck on a ramp. A label under the bonnet says to jump-start at no more than 13.7 V, so don't use a random jump pack. Fit the correct battery type and register it with a diagnostic tool so the battery-management system charges it properly. Some trucks also carry an AGM auxiliary stop/start battery. 48 V to 12 V DC-DC converter
DIFFICULTY · 4 / 5TIME · [TO RESEARCH]SHOP COST · Specialist roughly £700–800 for the part plus about £400 to fit and program; dealers quoted £1,800–2,000 (owner reports)
SYMPTOMS'Stop – electrical fault detected' with a red battery light, the 12-volt battery not charging, and possibly loss of drive. Often no stored faults, and it may clear itself before coming back.
THE FIXCheck first whether NHTSA recall 26V248000 (US) has been done. It adds a central software update to stop the converter overloading, and it's free. If the converter has already failed, it has to be replaced and then programmed to the vehicle, so use a dealer or a JLR specialist with the right software. Parts have been back-ordered, so plan on the truck being off the road for a while.

Asked on every forum

Range Rover L460 questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

There is no forum consensus yet. Owners haven't published L460 all-terrain fitment the way L405 owners have, and the off-the-shelf sensor-link lift kits sold for the L405 don't list the L460. Factory wheels run from 21 to 23 inches, and one owner fitted L460 21s with 275/50R21 tyres to an L405. If you want sidewall, 20-inch wheels are the realistic floor: Lucky8 lists 20×9.5 ET30 wheels for the L460, and L405 20s fit with the correct flat-seat bolts. Test any oversize tyre at full lock and in every height mode before you trust it.

SOURCE · FULLFATRR · LUCKY8
What fuel economy should I expect?

For 2023, the EPA rated the P400 at 18 mpg city, 26 highway and 21 combined, and the 4.4 V8 at 16/21/18. The P440e plug-in hybrid was rated 51 MPGe on electricity and 21 mpg on petrol alone.

SOURCE · FUELECONOMY.GOV
How much can it tow?

Land Rover USA quotes up to 7,716 lb for the P400 and the V8s, 6,614 lb for the current plug-in hybrid, and 5,511 lb for Range Rover Electric. Earlier P440e trucks were listed at 5,511 lb. Check the figure for your exact model year and powertrain.

SOURCE · RANGEROVER.COM · AUTOPADRE
Does it still have low range and a locking diff?

Combustion and hybrid models keep a twin-speed transfer box with high and low range. Upper trims get an Electronic Active Differential with torque vectoring, while the base SE has an open rear differential with torque vectoring by braking. Terrain Response 2, All Terrain Progress Control and hill descent control are standard.

SOURCE · RANGEROVER.COM (US SPEC)
N63 or S68 V8?

2022–2023 P530s use BMW's N63 twin-turbo 4.4. From the 2024 model year, JLR fitted BMW's S68, also used in the 615 PS SV. Owners on FullFatRR call the S68 'significantly more refined.' It's too early for long-term reliability data on either in this truck.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · FULLFATRR
How far does the plug-in hybrid go on electricity?

PHEVs use a 38.2 kWh battery (31.8 kWh usable) and a 105 kW motor. Land Rover estimates about 50 miles of real-world electric range. A 7 kW home charger fills it in about 5 hours, and a 50 kW DC charger gets it to 80% in under an hour.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · RANGEROVER.COM
Is there an electric one?

Yes. Range Rover Electric is on order books in the US for the 2027 model year. It has an EPA-estimated range of 333 miles, twin 260 kW motors, 660 lb-ft, 800 V charging (10–80% in about 22 minutes on a 350 kW charger), a 35-inch wading depth and 5,511 lb of towing.

SOURCE · RANGEROVER.COM
Can I get seven seats?

Only in the long-wheelbase body, and Land Rover USA says LWB seven-seaters aren't available as plug-in hybrids or EVs. Four- and five-seat layouts are also offered. Check recall 23V252000 on 2022–2023 seven-seaters for the second-row seat frame welds.

SOURCE · RANGEROVER.COM · NHTSA
Is it reliable?

The early record is mixed. NHTSA lists more than a dozen recalls across the 2023 and 2024 model years, and the DC-DC converter, 12-volt battery drain and software glitches dominate the FullFatRR technical board. Buy one with a warranty, a clean recall record and connected software that actually updates.

SOURCE · NHTSA · FULLFATRR
Can I use a GAP tool on it?

Yes. GAP's IID G4 lists 2022–2025 L460s on the standard tool, for fault codes, live data, service resets, calibrations, air-suspension height adjustment and CCF edits. FullFatRR owners use it for proximity-locking and other CCF tweaks.

SOURCE · LUCKY8 · FULLFATRR
Can I get sliders or a bash plate?

Not easily. A 2025 FullFatRR thread found no off-the-shelf sliders or underbody protection, and members suggested a fabrication shop would charge €3,500–4,000 for tree sliders and plates. It's a gap the aftermarket hasn't filled the way it has for the L405.

SOURCE · FULLFATRR
