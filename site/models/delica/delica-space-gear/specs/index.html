By year

YEARSPLATFORMENGINEOUTPUTNOTES
1994–1997PE8W / PD8W (Series 1)4M40 2.8 L TD I4125 PS / 30 kgf·mMechanical injection pump. Five-speed manual or four-speed automatic, 4WD.
1997–2004PE8W / PD8W / PF8W (Series 2)4M40 2.8 L TD I4140 PS / 32 kgf·mElectronically controlled pump from the 1997 facelift. Column-shift INVECS-II automatic; the manual leaves the catalogue in 1998.
1994–2007PD6W / PF6W6G72 3.0 L V6185 PS / 27 kgf·mFour-speed automatic only. The sole engine from late 2004 to the end.
1994–1997PD4W4G64 2.4 L I4145 PS / 21 kgf·m4WD petrol four; rear-drive PA4W versions lasted longer.
1994–2002PA4W / PA5W / PB6W and others2.4 petrol, 2.5 4D56 diesel, 3.0 V64D56: 105 PSRear-wheel-drive versions. Check the transfer lever before you buy anything sold as a 4x4.
1994–2005L400 / Space Gear / Starwagon (export)Various—Europe, Australia and other markets. Australia's Starwagon got 2.0 and 2.4 fours and a 3.0 V6.

Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Most Space Gears arrive with Japanese service history that's hard to read or missing, so change everything. The figures below are from one owner's L400 owner's manual, posted on Delica Canada. Mitsubishi also put a maintenance label on the air-intake duct under the hood. Work out whether you have the 4M40 diesel or the 6G72 V6 before you order anything.
