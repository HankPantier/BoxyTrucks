Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Treat any imported Star Wagon as unserviced until you see receipts, and don't take an importer's 'fully serviced' at face value. GearJunkie notes that good importers do the timing belt, water pump, hoses, glow plugs and fluids. The capacities below come from Liqui Moly's lookup, which uses European data for the L300 2.5 TD 4WD (1987–94). Confirm them in the workshop manual for your frame number, because automatics, twin-tank vans and Japanese-market specs differ.
