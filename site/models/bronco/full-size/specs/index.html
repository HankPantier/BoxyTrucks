Fluids, capacities & service intervals

These are thirty-year-old trucks with simple pushrod engines, so assume nothing has been done and baseline every fluid. Treat the front axle as a service item too. The TTB's spindle bearings, U-joints, ball joints and radius-arm bushings decide how the truck drives, and on most Broncos for sale they're original.
