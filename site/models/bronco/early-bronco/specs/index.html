Ford Bronco (1966–1977) specs, fluids and service intervals

The numbers you need in the garage, on one page: spec sheet, capacities and service intervals, wheels and tires, and the parts to order. Sourced from owners, specialists and the factory; tags marked confirm in manual haven't been checked against a factory source yet.

Spec sheet

BUILTMichigan Truck Plant, Wayne, Michigan · 1965–1977
PRODUCTION≈ 225,600 (Autotrader Classics)
BODIESU13 roadster (to 1968) · U14 half-cab (to 1972) · U15 wagon
CHASSISBox-section ladder frame, unique to the Bronco
WHEELBASE92 in · length 151.5 in
ENGINES170 & 200 I6 · 289 & 302 V8
TRANSMISSIONS3-speed manual (column shift) · C4 3-speed auto from 1973
TRANSFER CASEDana 20 · 2.46:1 low (T-handle) / 2.34:1 (J-shift)
FRONT AXLEDana 30 to mid-1971 · Dana 44 after; coil springs, radius arms
REAR AXLEFord 9-inch on leaf springs
BRAKESDrums · power front discs 1976–77
TURNING CIRCLE34 ft curb to curb (Ford, 1965)

YEARSPLATFORMENGINEOUTPUTNOTES
1966–1968U13 roadster · U14 half-cab · U15 wagon170 I6 · 289 V8 (from March 1966)170: 105 hp · 289: 200 hpColumn-shift three-speed only. Sport package from 1967. The roadster was dropped after 1968.
1969–1972U14 half-cab · U15 wagon170 I6 · 302 V8—302 replaces the 289; electric wipers from mid-1969. Sport a separate model from 1970. Dana 44 front from mid-1971. Half-cab ends in 1972; Ranger trim arrives.
1971 on (≈ 4 years)Baja Bronco (Stroppe)302 V8, automatic—Quick-ratio power steering, flares, Gates Commando tires, roll bar, tricolor paint. $5,566 new; 650 sold.
1973–1975U15 wagon200 I6 · 302 V8302 (1975): 125 hp netC4 automatic and power steering optional from 1973; J-shift Dana 20 with 2.34:1 low.
1976–1977U15 wagon200 I6 · 302 V8—Power front disc brakes, front anti-roll bar and Y-type steering linkage. 1977: heavy-duty 9-inch housing, fuel doors, plastic tanks.

Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing has been done, and assume plenty has been changed: most early Broncos have been modified, so check what engine, axles and transfer case you actually have before buying parts. The data plate on the glovebox door gives the factory engine, transmission and axle codes.
