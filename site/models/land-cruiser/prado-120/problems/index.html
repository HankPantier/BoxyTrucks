Land Cruiser Prado 120 problems to check before you buy

  1. 1KD-FTV: injector seats and oil pickupAsk for injector-seat (washer) receipts. Listen for a rattle and look for white smoke on a cold start, then shine a light through the sump drain-plug hole. CarsGuide and Unsealed 4X4 both say carbon on the pickup screen means a suspect engine. Trucks built from August 2007 got aluminum seats.
  2. 1KD-FTV: injector ageAustralianCar.Reviews says the injectors typically fail at 120,000–140,000 km. Signs are a loud knock when cold, rough idle, poor running under load and worse fuel economy. Both it and 4X4 Australia suggest testing them at every service and replacing them at about 100,000 km, so ask whether they've been done.
  3. Timing belt or chain?The 1GR-FE V6 and the 2.7 fours use chains. The 1KD-FTV, 1KZ-TE and 5VZ-FE use belts, and HD Automotive quotes Toyota's 1KD interval as 150,000 km or five years. No receipt means budget for the belt and water pump.
  4. Cracked dash and split rear doorCarsGuide reports dashboards cracking near the passenger airbag on early 120s, and rear-door skins splitting along the hinge side under the weight of the spare. Check both, and look closely at the door if a heavier spare tire has been fitted.
  5. Rear air suspension (Grande and top grades)If it has air springs, check that it levels and holds height overnight. 4X4 Australia warns that self-leveling rear air makes aftermarket suspension 'a much bigger (and more expensive) task' than on coil-sprung trucks.
  6. V6 transmission4X4 Australia suggests skipping the early four-speed automatic V6 in favor of the five-speed auto (late 2005 on) or the rarer six-speed manual.
  7. Test the center diff lockEngage the center lock off pavement and make sure the light comes on and goes off. IH8MUD's GX 470 pre-buy advice applies to the Prado's shared system: a seized actuator is an expensive surprise on a truck that's never left the road.
  8. Import eligibilityA J120 can't be imported into the US until it's 25 years old by date of manufacture. The first ones qualify from September 2027. A '2003 Prado' advertised as US-legal today should set off alarms.

Land Cruiser Prado 120 recalls and service bulletins

  • Fuel pulsation damper assembly (Australia) ↗ GRJ120 built 29 October 2002 – 22 March 2003 · PRA 2003/6123April 2003: the pulsation damper, which smooths fuel-pressure pulses from the injectors, may have been assembled incorrectly.
  • 1KD-FTV injector seats changed from copper to aluminum ↗ Production change from August 2007 to stop blow-by leaking past the injector seats (AustralianCar.Reviews).

Check any VIN for open recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls ↗, or see the recall tracker.

Owner questions about Land Cruiser Prado 120 reliability

What's the 1KD-FTV injector problem?

The original injector seats were copper and leak with age. Blow-by then mixes with the oil, forms carbon and can block the sump pickup, starving the engine. Aluminum seats replaced copper from August 2007. Separately, injectors often fail at 120,000–140,000 km. Check the seat history, look for white smoke or a rattle on a cold start, and inspect the pickup through the drain-plug hole.
