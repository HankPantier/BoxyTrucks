Fluids, capacities & service intervals

On a 1KD-FTV, 'baseline' starts with the injectors: get the seats and injectors tested or replaced and the oil pickup inspected before you worry about anything else. Specs below are from Roughtrax's KDJ120 (1KD-FTV) data sheet unless noted. 1GR-FE and 2.7 petrol oil capacities are from Penrite's lookup for Australian Prados. 1GR-FE, 1KZ-TE and petrol-four trucks differ, so confirm in the manual for your engine.
