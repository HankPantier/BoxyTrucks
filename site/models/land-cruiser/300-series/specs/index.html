Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Toyota never sold the 300 in the US, so the specs below are for its US twin, the Lexus LX 600 (same V35A-FTS twin-turbo V6 and 10-speed). They come from AMSOIL's LX 600 lookup and service-data figures posted by IH8MUD owners. Diesel (F33A-FTV) and hybrid trucks differ, and service intervals vary by market, so check your owner's manual. Baselining matters more than usual here because of the V35A machining-debris recall.
