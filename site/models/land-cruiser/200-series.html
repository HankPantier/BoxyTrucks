TOYOTA LAND CRUISER · J200 · 2007–2021

Land Cruiser 200 Series

“The 200 is a nearly three-ton bank vault with a 5.7-liter V8 and a crawl-control button, and it is far better in the dirt than anything with a heated steering wheel has any right to be. It was also the last of its kind in America: when it left after 2021, the big V8 Land Cruiser went with it.”

The story

Toyota launched the 200 Series in late 2007 with a new frame and platform, bigger brakes, a strengthened front suspension, skid plates and roof pillars redesigned for rollover protection. In the US it came with one engine, the 5.7-liter 3UR-FE V8 at 381 hp, in a single trim level. Sadayoshi Koyari, the lead engineer known as 'Mr Land Cruiser', called it 'the strongest Land Cruiser ever produced by Toyota.' Fourteen years later Toyota pulled the Land Cruiser from North America, and the 200 became the last full-size Land Cruiser wagon sold there.

A FIVE-YEAR PLAN

Work on a successor to the 100 Series began in 2002 under Koyari and Tetsuya Tada. A production design was settled in 2004, ten years after the 100's, and prototypes were tested for more than two years, from 2004 to early 2007. Production started in September 2007 at Toyota's Yoshiwara and Tahara plants. In Venezuela it went on sale that November under the nickname 'Roraima', after Mount Roraima.

Underneath, it shared its platform with the Lexus LX 570 and was related to the Tundra and Sequoia of the same era. The global range was huge: a 4.0 V6, three petrol V8s and the 4.5-liter 1VD-FTV twin-turbo diesel V8, a twin-turbo version of the engine in the 70 Series. Unsealed 4X4 says the diesel made up about 90 percent of 200 sales in Australia.

ELECTRONICS THAT EARN THEIR KEEP

The 200 brought a set of off-road aids no previous Land Cruiser had. CRAWL works like off-road cruise control, holding a slow, steady speed with throttle and brakes. There was also Downhill Assist Control, a multi-terrain ABS calibration, and KDSS, the Kinetic Dynamic Suspension System, which frees up wheel articulation off-road while keeping body roll in check on the highway. US trucks used an AB60F six-speed automatic, a JF2A full-time transfer case and 3.90 gears.

There was no 2012 model year in the US. Slee notes that 2011 production ran late and the 2013s started early. The 2013 facelift added Multi-Terrain Select with five modes, Turn Assist for tight trails, and front, side and rear cameras. For 2016 came the biggest change: an AE80F eight-speed automatic, taller 3.30 gears, larger front brakes, Toyota Safety Sense and new styling front and rear.

HOW IT ENDED

To mark 60 years since the FJ40, Toyota built the Heritage Edition for 2020 and 2021. It was a two-row truck in black or white with bronze BBS forged wheels, a Yakima MegaWarrior basket, no running boards and a vintage Land Cruiser badge on the rear pillars. Slee counts 1,202 built for 2020. In December 2020 Toyota told Road & Track the Land Cruiser would leave North America after 2021, and production ended in March 2021.

Elsewhere the 300 Series took over. Americans waited until 2024, when the name came back on the smaller, Prado-based 250 Series. The LX 570 also ended in 2021, and Lexus replaced it with the LX 600. For American buyers who want a big V8 Land Cruiser wagon with a frame under it, the 200 is the last one.

SIDEBAR · NINE IN A ROW

The 200 Series at the Dakar Rally

Team Land Cruiser Toyota Auto Body raced the 200 in the Dakar Rally's Group T2 production class from 2009 to 2022. T2 is for production-based vehicles, so these were recognizably showroom Land Cruisers, the diesel VDJ200 in recent years, not purpose-built prototypes.

From 2014 to 2022 the team won the class nine years in a row. For a truck that often ended up in suburban school lines, that's a remarkable record.

How it’s built

The 200 is built on a new, stronger frame with independent front suspension and a coil-sprung live rear axle. The clever part is KDSS, a system that frees up articulation off-road and stiffens the truck on the road. US trucks use the 5.7-liter 3UR-FE, a 32-valve DOHC, chain-driven V8 with dual VVT-i, and full-time four-wheel drive with a lockable center differential.

Buying one: what to check

  1. 01
    Look for pink crust at the water pumpDried pink coolant running down the front of the engine from the water-pump weep hole is the classic 3UR sign. One owner's build thread notes the engine is known for water-pump weeps around 100,000 miles, and that the pump usually gives plenty of warning.
  2. 02
    Scan for air-injection codesA check-engine light with VSC and traction-control lights on together often turns out to be the secondary air-injection system, such as code P2438 for the pressure sensor. It's emissions-related and the truck drives normally, but a seller who has cleared codes is hiding a repair.
  3. 03
    If the suspension has been touched, check the KDSS valvesAfter a lift or shock swap, the two KDSS valves must be closed again. One IH8MUD owner found a shop had left them fully open and the truck rolled alarmingly on uneven roads. Check them with a 5 mm hex key, and test-drive on a winding road.
  4. 04
    Listen for a driveline clunkA thunk when stopping and pulling away usually isn't the transmission. Unsealed 4X4 traces it to dry universal joints, especially on trucks that cross water. Ask when the driveshafts were last greased.
  5. 05
    Check the oil filter capThe 3UR has a plastic oil filter housing cap that wrenches round off and that can warp or crack with age. Look for leaks and chewed edges.
  6. 06
    Know which phase you're buying2008–2011: six-speed, 3.90 gears. 2013–2015: facelift with Multi-Terrain Select, Turn Assist and cameras. 2016–2021: eight-speed, 3.30 gears, bigger brakes, Toyota Safety Sense. There's no 2012 in the US.
  7. 07
    Ask about towingKBB lists 8,500 lb for 2008 and 8,100 lb for 2016–2021. A truck that pulled a heavy trailer for years deserves a closer look at the transmission fluid, the brakes and the rear springs.
  8. 08
    Diesel (non-US) 200s: injectors, DPF and airboxOn the 1VD-FTV, Unsealed's big three are injector problems (rough idle, smoke, poor economy), DPF and EGR trouble on 2015-on trucks, and dust getting past the airbox seal.

For the hands-on owner

Common repairs

The jobs owners actually end up doing, rated for a competent home mechanic with a decent tool kit. Always confirm torque figures and procedures against the factory service manual for your VIN.

Replace a weeping water pump
DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · ≈ A few hoursSHOP COST · Pump and gasket plus coolant; one owner paid for 'a few hours of shop time'
SYMPTOMSDried pink coolant trailing down the front of the engine from the water-pump weep hole, and a slowly dropping coolant level.
THE FIXFollowing the factory manual, drain the coolant, remove the fan, drive belt and anything else in front of the pump, then fit the new pump with a new gasket. Refill and bleed the cooling system. The build-thread owner also replaced the fan bracket, because its bearing had been sitting in leaking coolant.

Diagnose secondary air-injection faults
DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · ≈ 1–3 hours to diagnoseSHOP COST · Parts plus labor; sensor first, pumps and valves cost more
SYMPTOMSA check-engine light, often with the slip light flashing and 'Check VSC' on, that may come and go, especially in cold or damp weather. Codes such as P2438 (pressure sensor circuit high, bank 2). Owners on Tundra sites also report P2440–P2443.
THE FIXRead the code before you buy parts. IH8MUD members point to the factory description for P2438: pressure in the air-injection system is above its threshold, so suspects are the pressure sensor, its wiring or the ECM. If the light clears on its own, start with the sensor and wiring. Because the 3UR also powers the Tundra and Sequoia, their forums are a good source of fixes. Toyota extended warranty coverage on this system for 2007–2010 Tundras and 2008–2010 Sequoias; the Land Cruiser wasn't named.

Close the KDSS valves after suspension work
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 15 minutesSHOP COST · Free
SYMPTOMSAlarming body roll on uneven roads, or a truck that feels wrong right after a lift or shock install.
THE FIXChock the wheels and get under the truck. The two KDSS valves are opened during suspension work and must be closed again afterward. Find both valves and confirm they're closed with a 5 mm hex key. If anyone has worked on the suspension, check them before your next drive.

Grease the driveshaft U-joints
DIFFICULTY · 1 / 5TIME · ≈ 30 minutesSHOP COST · A tube of grease
SYMPTOMSA clunk when you come to a stop, as if you'd nudged the car ahead, and another clunk when you pull away.
THE FIXUnsealed 4X4 says the clunk is usually dry universal joints, not the transmission or the differentials. Grease washes out in water crossings and breaks down under heavy crawling. Grease every U-joint and slip joint on both driveshafts about every six months, and again after deep water. If the clunk is still there, then start looking at the drivetrain.

Swap to an aluminum oil filter cap
DIFFICULTY · 1 / 5TIME · ≈ 20 minutes at an oil changeSHOP COST · About half the $60+ retail price of the plastic cap, per IH8MUD
SYMPTOMSA plastic cap that rounds off, lets the wrench slip, warps or cracks after many heat cycles.
THE FIXAn IH8MUD member found the aluminum cap from the Venza 3.5 V6 fits once you move over the internal parts. Bend up the two small tabs on the Land Cruiser cap's perforated support tube, push down, turn it about 45 degrees and lift it out. Watch for the spring and the drain-valve disc underneath. Fit the tube into the aluminum cap the same way and bend the tabs flat. Refill the oil, start the engine and check for weeps.

Add rear air helper springs for towing
DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · ≈ 2 hours on a liftSHOP COST · $200–250 for one IH8MUD owner
SYMPTOMSAlmost 4 inches of sag at the hitch with a 5,000 lb trailer and about 600 lb of tongue weight, even with weight distribution set up.
THE FIXRaise and support the rear, then disconnect the rear shocks so the axle can drop. Fit the air bags inside the rear coil springs, route the air lines away from heat, and add an onboard compressor with a union tee if you want to adjust them from the cab. Reconnect the shocks; the owner needed a long steel bar to compress the suspension enough to line them up. Run about 35 psi when towing.

DIFFICULTY · 1 / 5TIME · ≈ 15 minutes to checkSHOP COST · Free to check; an aftermarket airbox costs more

Replace leaking injectors (1VD diesel)
DIFFICULTY · 5 / 5TIME · ≈ A long day or moreSHOP COST · About A$4,000 in parts for a DIY job, plus a few thousand more for labor, per Unsealed
SYMPTOMSRough idle, smoke, poor fuel economy, sludge in the intake and DPF trouble, often caused by leaking injector seat washers.
THE FIXUnsealed's advice is to replace all eight injectors with updated seat washers rather than one at a time, because single replacements often failed again within months. Do it under warranty where possible. Out of warranty, many specialists will do it for less than a dealer.

Asked on every forum

Land Cruiser 200 Series questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

On IH8MUD, 285/65R18 'will fit fine with zero rubbing' on a stock truck, and 285/70R18 'will rub a bit but should be manageable', One owner running 285/70R17s on aftermarket wheels reported light rubbing on the plastic liner, which he fixed with a heat gun. 275/70s are described as 'tried and true'. For anything bigger, check the forum's 200 Series Tire and Wheel Size Database.

SOURCE · IH8MUD
How much can it tow?

KBB lists 8,500 lb for 2008, 8,200 lb for 2015 and 8,100 lb for 2016–2021. An IH8MUD owner with a 2013 quotes 8,500 lb with an 850 lb maximum tongue weight. Weight distribution helps the short 112-inch wheelbase, and rear air helpers fix sag.

SOURCE · KBB · IH8MUD
Six-speed or eight-speed?

2008–2015 trucks have the AB60F six-speed with 3.90 gears. 2016–2021 trucks have the AE80F eight-speed with 3.30 gears and bigger front brakes. If you plan big tires on an eight-speed truck, re-gearing (Nitro's 4.88s are a common choice) restores the lost low-end response.

SOURCE · SLEE · IH8MUD
What fuel economy should I expect?

KBB lists 13 mpg city and 18 highway (15 combined) for most years, and 13/17 for 2021, on a 24.6-gallon tank. It needs regular unleaded, not premium.

SOURCE · KBB
Does it have lockers?

US trucks have full-time four-wheel drive with a lockable center differential, plus A-TRAC, CRAWL and (from 2013) Multi-Terrain Select. Front and rear lockers are aftermarket; ARB Air Lockers and Harrop E-lockers are both common in IH8MUD build signatures.

SOURCE · SLEE · IH8MUD
What is KDSS, and does it complicate a lift?

The Kinetic Dynamic Suspension System lets the wheels articulate more off-road while controlling body roll on the road. It complicates lifts a little: the KDSS valves are opened during suspension work and must be closed afterward. IH8MUD has a warning thread from an owner whose shop left them open.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · IH8MUD
Was there a diesel in the US?

No. North America only ever got the 5.7-liter 3UR-FE petrol V8. The 4.5-liter twin-turbo 1VD-FTV diesel was sold in Australia, Europe, the Middle East and elsewhere.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA
What breaks?

On US 3UR trucks: water pumps around 100,000 miles, secondary air-injection faults, plastic oil filter caps and dry U-joints. On diesels: injectors, the DPF and EGR on 2015-on trucks, and dust past the airbox. Unsealed also notes some 200s have a vibration that owners argue about.

SOURCE · EXPO · IH8MUD · UNSEALED 4X4
What's special about the Heritage Edition?

Built for 2020 and 2021 to mark 60 years since the FJ40: two rows of seats, black or white paint, bronze BBS forged wheels, a Yakima MegaWarrior basket, no running boards or side moldings, and a vintage Land Cruiser badge. Slee counts 1,202 for 2020.

SOURCE · SLEE · WIKIPEDIA
Why did Toyota stop selling it in the US?

In December 2020 Toyota told Road & Track that the Land Cruiser would end in North America after 2021. The name came back in 2024 on the smaller 250 Series, and the 300 Series flagship isn't sold in the US.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA
Is the Lexus LX 570 the same truck?

It shares the platform and the 3UR-FE (Lexus rates it at 383 hp and 403 lb-ft), but the LX 570 adds standard AHC height control and adaptive damping, 20-inch wheels and a lot more leather.

SOURCE · SLEE
