Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Any FJ25 is now more than 65 years old, so assume nothing has been done and service everything. Toyota's capacities below are for the F engine as fitted to the 40 and 55 Series (compiled on IH8MUD from Toyota publications). The FJ25's own gearbox and transfer case aren't in that table, so check its figures in an FJ25 manual.
