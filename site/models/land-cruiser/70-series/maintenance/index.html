Maintenance schedule · 1984–present

Toyota Land Cruiser 70 Series maintenance schedule

Toyota Australia services the 70 Series every 10,000 km or six months: its capped-price program covered six services over three years or 60,000 km. 70seriesstore's interval guide fills in the longer items. Severe use (towing, dust, sustained off-road work) halves everything, and on a 70 that's most of its life.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

Oil interval from Toyota's capped-price servicing program and 70seriesstore; longer intervals from 70seriesstore's guide, which doesn't separate Toyota's figures from its own. Older 1HZ trucks run shorter oil intervals. Check your service book.

WHENWHAT TO DO
Every 10,000 km / 6 months (V8 and 2.8)
  • Engine oil and filter (5,000 km in severe service; 1HZ every 5,000 km)
  • Fuel filter (70seriesstore: the most-skipped item)
  • Inspect the air filter and the clean side of the airbox
  • Check the rear spring hangers and grease the chassis
Every 20,000–40,000 km
  • Replace the air filter, sooner in dust
Every 40,000 km (20,000 severe)
  • Front and rear differentials and transfer case (GL-5 75W-90 or 80W-90)
Every 80,000 km
  • Gearbox oil: GL-4 75W-90 in the manual (not GL-5); Toyota WS in the automatic
Every 2 years
  • Brake and clutch fluid
Every 5 years
  • Flush the coolant (Toyota red long-life) regardless of kilometres
Every 1,000–2,000 km (late-2016-on V8 with DPF)
  • A spirited highway run so the DPF can regenerate (Unsealed)

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

70seriesstore says to halve every interval for heavy towing, sustained off-road use, dust, frequent short trips or lots of idling. After water crossings, check the diff breathers and look for milky oil.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover
