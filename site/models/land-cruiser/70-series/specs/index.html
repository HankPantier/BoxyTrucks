Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Most 70s in the US are 25-year-old grey imports (1HZ, 1PZ, 1HD-FTE or petrol 3F/1FZ), while newer markets got the 1VD-FTV V8, so first confirm the exact model code and engine on the VIN plate. Specs below are given for the 4.5 V8 (VDJ79, Roughtrax) and the 1HZ (Toyota publications via IH8MUD); anything else, check the manual. Unsealed's rule applies to all of them: service history beats odometer.
