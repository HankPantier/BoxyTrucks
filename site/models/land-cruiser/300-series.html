“This is the Land Cruiser the rest of the world gets and America has to admire from a distance. It's lighter, stiffer and quicker than the 200, and its engineers used a 30-year-old 80 Series as the benchmark. The only way to buy one in the US is with a Lexus badge on the front.”

PART 01

Overview

Where it came from, why it matters, and how it changed year to year.

The story

Toyota unveiled the J300 on 9 June 2021 to replace the 200 Series, which had been on sale since 2007. The V8s are gone. In their place is a pair of twin-turbo V6s: a 409-hp 3.4-liter petrol and a 304-hp 3.3-liter diesel. The truck rides on a new body-on-frame platform and weighs about 200 kg less than the 200. It went on sale in the UAE and Kuwait within weeks, then Japan, Australia, South Africa and much of Asia. It has never been sold in the US or Canada. Wikipedia cites slow sales of the 200 and limited production capacity. North America gets the truck as the LX 600 instead.

BENCHMARKING THE 80

Chief engineer Takami Yokoo made an unusual choice for a flagship launch: he compared the new truck to a 30-year-old one. 'We actually used the Land Cruiser 80 as a guidepost,' he told reporters, adding that 'even 30 years after it first appeared, it has the best rough road performance of any Land Cruiser.' The team kept the wheelbase that started with the 80 Series, and Yokoo summed up the brief as being 'the partner that will get you home safely, no matter where you go.'

To find what needed fixing, Toyota drove the outgoing 200 around Australia. The trip showed that drivers got tired, so the engineers changed the suspension geometry to make it smoother on pavement. Laser welding that joins two types of metal increased torsional rigidity and helped take out about 200 kg (440 lb). The engine sits lower and further back, and a V6 replaces the V8, so the nose is lighter. Dakar Rally driver Akira Miura did test driving and reported much better control and balance.

WHAT'S UNDER IT

The platform is GA-F, which the 250 Series and the Lexus GX later shared. Most markets get the V35A-FTS 3.4-liter twin-turbo petrol V6 (VJA300) or the F33A-FTV 3.3-liter twin-turbo diesel (FJA300), both with a 10-speed automatic. Some markets also keep the old 4.0-liter 1GR-FE V6 with a six-speed (GRJ300). The chassis gets an upgraded Electronic Kinetic Dynamic Suspension System (E-KDSS), adaptive variable suspension, Multi-Terrain Select with new Deep Snow and Auto modes, and an underbody camera.

For the first time there's a GR Sport. Auto Bild described it as inspired by the Dakar Rally, and it is the version to have off-road: it has front and rear locking differentials, while the standard truck has only a center lock. Ground clearance is 230 mm and the approach angle is 32 degrees, and overall length, width and wheelbase are largely the same as the 200's. ANCAP's 2022 rating scored it 89 percent for adult occupants and 88 percent for children.

THE WAITING GAME

Demand overwhelmed supply. In January 2022 Auto Bild reported that Toyota's Japanese website was telling buyers to expect waits of up to four years, a backlog it compared to the Mercedes G-Class's. Germany wasn't expected to get the 300 at all, and kept the older J150 Prado instead. A hybrid, the VJH300, arrived in 2025, launching first in the UAE with a new front end. Japan got an updated range with the hybrid on 1 October 2026, according to Wikipedia.

THE AMERICAN WORKAROUND

For Americans, importing a 300 isn't a realistic option, because of the 25-year rule; one IH8MUD member worked out that 2047 is the first year a 2022 truck becomes legal. The practical answer is the LX 600, unveiled on 13 October 2021 on the same platform with the 409-hp V35A and a 10-speed. The 457-hp LX 700h hybrid followed, and the LX 700h Overtrail, announced in October 2024, has front, center and rear lockers like a GR Sport.

Being the US version has a downside: the US recalls. NHTSA campaigns 24V381 and 25V767 cover V35A engines in the Lexus LX, Tundra and some GX 550s in which machining debris can damage the main bearings, causing knocking, rough running or a stall. 25V767 alone lists 9,895 LX models built between February 2022 and April 2024. Separately, Lexus is updating transmission software on about 4,400 2025–26 LX 600s. The sources here don't say whether overseas 300s with the same engine are covered, so owners outside the US should check with their local Toyota distributor.

SIDEBAR · THE 300 YOU CAN BUY HERE

LX 600 and LX 700h: a Land Cruiser with a Lexus badge

Mechanically, the LX 600 is the 300 Series with a Lexus body and interior: same GA-F frame, same 3.4-liter twin-turbo V6 (409 hp, 650 Nm), same 10-speed. Lexus adds trims Toyota doesn't offer, including an F Sport, a four-seat Ultra Luxury and, since late 2024, the LX 700h Overtrail with three lockers. Wikipedia counts about 7,000 US LX sales a year since 2023.

Many of the owners in IH8MUD's 300-series section drive LXs, and they've taken it on the trail with Slee skid plates and sliders, 35-inch tires, and even 74Weld portal axles. If you live in North America and want a 300, this is how you get one.

PART 02

Specs & capability

How it’s built, what it can do, and what fits under it.

How it’s built

The 300 keeps the formula that made the name: a separate ladder frame, full-time four-wheel drive with low range, and stabilizer bars that are hydraulically linked by E-KDSS. Toyota rebuilt everything around that formula. The frame is lighter and stiffer, the twin-turbo V6 sits lower and further back, and there's a 10-speed automatic, adaptive dampers and a camera that shows what's under the truck.

PART 03

Buying one

What to pay, what to check, and how to tell a good one from a clone.

What to check before you buy

  1. 01
    Accept that it's an LX in the USThe 300 isn't sold in the US or Canada and can't be legally imported under the 25-year rule for a long time. For North American buyers, the LX 600 or LX 700h is the 300 Series you can actually buy.
  2. 02
    Run the VIN against the V35A recallsNHTSA campaigns 24V381 and 25V767 cover V35A engines in which machining debris can damage the main bearings, causing knocking, rough running, a no-start or a stall. 25V767 lists 9,895 2022–2024 LX models built from 26 February 2022 to 2 April 2024; 24V381 covered earlier engines. Ask whether the engine has already been replaced, which several IH8MUD members' trucks have had.
  3. 03
    Check the transmission software recallNHTSA 26V094000 (Lexus 26LA01) covers about 4,400 2025–26 LX 600s: if a transmission solenoid fails, the transmission and engine ECUs can lose communication and the transmission can damage itself. Dealers update the transmission ECU software for free.
  4. 04
    Test-drive in traffic, not just on the highwayOwners of 2022–24 LX 600s describe a clunk into first gear and hesitation when accelerating from a rolling stop. Some blame the pre-collision system intervening. Drive one through a few stop-and-go junctions before you buy. Owners say the LX 700h hybrid doesn't do it.
  5. 05
    Work the rear hatchOne IH8MUD owner's power hatch beeped and refused to close. The cause was a sticking left-side pinch sensor, which was replaced. Open and close it several times.
  6. 06
    AHC or not, and which locker count?Many LX trims have height-adjustable AHC suspension, and armor is specific to it: Slee sells separate non-AHC LX 600 sliders, for example. Overtrail and GR Sport trucks have front and rear lockers; standard trucks have only the center lock. Know which one you're buying.
  7. 07
    Ask about fluid changesOwners on IH8MUD do early differential and transfer-case changes (around 20,000–30,000 miles) after reports of debris in Tundra differential oil. Records of early oil and gear-oil changes, or oil-analysis reports, are a good sign.

Spotting the real thing

No 300 was ever sold new in the US, so any 'Land Cruiser 300' advertised there is either a Lexus LX or an import with a registration problem.

  1. ID
    Model codesVJA300 = 3.4 V35A-FTS twin-turbo petrol V6. FJA300 = 3.3 F33A-FTV twin-turbo diesel V6. GRJ300 = 4.0 1GR-FE V6 with a 6-speed automatic. VJH300 = V35A hybrid.
  2. ID
    GR Sport lockersThe GR Sport has front and rear diff locks. Standard 300s lock only the centre diff, so check the switches if lockers matter to you.
  3. ID
    US siblingThe Lexus LX 600 / LX 700h (J310) is the 300's US-market twin, on the same frame and powertrains. It's the only legal way to own this platform new in the US.

Importing & legality

Toyota has never sold the J300 in the United States or Canada, citing slow 200 Series sales and limited production capacity. The Lexus LX is the US version of this platform.

Under the NHTSA 25-year exemption, the earliest 300s (production began in 2021) won't be eligible for US import until the mid-2040s. Until then the only route is a registered importer converting and certifying the vehicle, which in practice is almost never feasible.

Rules change and vary by state and country. Confirm with your DMV and a licensed importer before you buy.

Asked on every forum

Land Cruiser 300 Series questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

IH8MUD owners report 275/70R18 (about 33.3 inches) and 285/70R18 KO3s (about 33.7 inches) with no rubbing on stock suspension. One LX owner with 275/70R18s had no rub at any AHC height, at full lock, in drive or reverse. 295/70R18s on stock wheels have been reported on an LX 700h Overtrail, and Dissent Off-Road told a member that 35s on a +40 mm offset wheel fit with minimal to zero trimming.

SOURCE · IH8MUD
Can I buy a 300 Series in the US?

Not new. It isn't sold in the US or Canada, and the 25-year import rule rules out bringing one in for decades. The Lexus LX 600 and LX 700h are the same truck under the skin and are sold in North America.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · IH8MUD
Why didn't Toyota sell it in America?

Wikipedia cites slow sales of the 200 Series and limited production capacity. The smaller 250 took over the Land Cruiser name in the US for 2024.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA
Which engines are there?

It depends on the market: a 3.4-liter twin-turbo petrol V6 (409 hp), a 3.3-liter twin-turbo diesel V6 (304 hp), a carried-over 4.0-liter V6 in some places, and since 2025 a 457-hp V6 hybrid. All but the 4.0 use a 10-speed automatic.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA
Does it have lockers?

Every 300 has a lockable center differential. The GR Sport adds front and rear lockers. On the Lexus side, the LX 700h Overtrail has all three, and Wikipedia also lists a Japan-only LX 'Offroad' trim with three.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA
How much lighter is it than the 200?

About 200 kg (440 lb), thanks to the redesigned GA-F frame and laser-welded mixed-metal construction. The V6s are also lighter than the V8s they replaced and sit further back.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · JAPANESE NOSTALGIC CAR
Is the twin-turbo V6 reliable?

The US LX 600 shares the V35A with the Tundra, and both are covered by recalls 24V381 and 25V767 for machining debris that can ruin the main bearings. Later engines were built with improved processes or an improved main bearing, and some are still under investigation. Owners routinely do early oil changes and oil analysis.

SOURCE · NHTSA · IH8MUD
What did Toyota use as the benchmark?

The 80 Series. Chief engineer Takami Yokoo said the 80 still had 'the best rough road performance of any Land Cruiser,' and the 300 keeps the wheelbase the 80 introduced.

SOURCE · JAPANESE NOSTALGIC CAR
How long was the waiting list?

In January 2022 Auto Bild reported Toyota Japan quoting waits of up to four years for new orders.

SOURCE · AUTO BILD
How safe is it?

ANCAP's 2022 rating, aligned with Euro NCAP, scored 89% for adult occupants, 88% for child occupants, 81% for vulnerable road users and 77% for safety assist.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · ANCAP
What fluids and capacities does it take?

From service data posted by an IH8MUD owner: transfer case 1.89 qt of Toyota LF 75W, front diff 1.23 qt. Owners also report using 0W-20 engine oil. Check your own service manual, since markets and drivetrains differ.

SOURCE · IH8MUD
