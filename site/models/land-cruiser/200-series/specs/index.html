Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing was done. The 200 is easy to keep alive, but 'lifetime' fluids, dry U-joints and a weeping water pump are common on high-mileage trucks. Specs below are for the US 3UR-FE V8. Intervals come from Toyota's 2021 Warranty & Maintenance Guide; capacities come from AMSOIL's lookup for a 2020 truck (8-speed). Trucks from 2008–2015 have the 6-speed AB60F, and the 1VD diesel is different again, so check capacities against your manual.
