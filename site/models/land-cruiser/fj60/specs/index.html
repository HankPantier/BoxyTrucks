Fluids, capacities & service intervals

An FJ60 is now 40-plus years old and most have passed 200,000 miles, so treat every fluid and grease point as unknown. Fluid types and capacities below are from Toyota publications as compiled on IH8MUD for the 60 and 70 series (US FJ60: 2F engine and H41F/H42F four-speed; the H55F five-speed was never fitted to US trucks at the factory).
