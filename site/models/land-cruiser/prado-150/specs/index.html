Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Toyota Australia's owner's-manual site was offline while we researched this, so the fluid specs below come from Toyota Japan's own owner's manuals for the Japanese-market 150 (the 2011 petrol edition and the 2020 1GD-FTV edition), plus Penrite's lookup for Australian trucks. Japan never got the 1KD-FTV, and other markets can specify different grades, so check your own manual. The jobs that matter on a 150 depend on the engine: the 1KD-FTV needs its timing belt and injectors looked after, and the 1GD-FTV needs its DPF and intake kept healthy.
