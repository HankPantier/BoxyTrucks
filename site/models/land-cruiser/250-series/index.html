PART 01

Overview

Where it came from, why it matters, and how it changed year to year.

The story

In December 2020 Toyota confirmed that the Land Cruiser would leave North America after the 2021 model year, ending the run of the V8 200 Series. The gap lasted two model years. On 2 August 2023 Toyota unveiled the J250. Most of the world knows it as the fifth-generation Land Cruiser Prado, but in the US it is sold simply as the Land Cruiser. It is a two-row midsize SUV rather than a three-row full-size one, it is boxier, and it starts at a much lower price than the 200 did. In North America it comes only as a hybrid. The full-size flagship, the 300 Series, still isn't sold here.

A PRADO BY ANY OTHER NAME

Americans have owned Prados for years without being told. The previous J150 Prado was sold in the US as the Lexus GX 460, and the J250 has a Lexus twin of its own in the GX 550, which uses a 349-hp twin-turbo V6. This time the Toyota-badged truck came too. It sits on the GA-F body-on-frame platform shared with the 300 Series. Toyota claims frame rigidity is up 50 percent and overall rigidity 30 percent, and the 250 is the first 'light-duty' Land Cruiser with electric power steering, which is what makes lane-tracing assist possible.

Outside the US the engine list is long. Depending on the market, the 250 is offered with a 2.4-liter turbo petrol, a 2.7-liter petrol four, the familiar 2.8-liter 1GD-FTV turbo-diesel, or that diesel with a 48-volt mild-hybrid system, which is what Australia and Western Europe get. Europe mostly calls it plain 'Land Cruiser' and keeps the Prado name only where the 300 is also sold. The US and China get the gasoline hybrid only. Every version is a four-cylinder.

BACK TO THE BOX

Wikipedia credits project chief designer Yoshito Watanabe's team with returning to practicality, durability and dependability, following a basic direction set by former Toyota CEO Akio Toyoda. They also made it look like a 1980s Land Cruiser again. The square body draws on the 60 Series. The base '1958' grade and the launch First Edition get round LED headlamps behind a TOYOTA heritage grille, and the mid-grade 'Land Cruiser' uses rectangular lamps that Toyota says were modeled on the FJ62. Toyota also says it optimized the A-pillar position so you can see more of the trail.

The US launched with three grades: the 1958 at $55,950 plus $1,395 destination, the Land Cruiser, and a First Edition limited to 5,000 trucks for North America. The First Edition came with a roof rack, rock rails and a front skid plate. Car and Driver's First Edition test car reached 60 mph in 7.7 seconds. The magazine's staff liked the slimmer size and the 40- and 80-series design cues. They complained about the thin sheet metal and doors you have to slam.

THE HYBRID IN THE ROOM

The US powertrain is the i-FORCE MAX: a 2.4-liter turbocharged four with a 48-hp electric motor in the bell housing of an eight-speed automatic, for 326 hp and 465 lb-ft. The hybrid battery is a 1.87 kWh nickel-metal-hydride pack. The EPA rates it at 22 city, 25 highway and 23 combined, compared with 14 combined for the last V8 200, and Car and Driver averaged 20 mpg over its test. Full-time four-wheel drive with a two-speed transfer case is standard, and so are locking center and rear differentials on every US grade.

The catch is the fuel tank. It holds 17.9 gallons, and with the mileage above, overlanders start looking at the fuel gauge sooner than they would like. Slee Off-Road's Arctic Trucks build swaps in a 29-gallon tank, which is more than 50 percent more. Owners also note that the battery's location gives the cargo area a sloped floor. On long, steep descents, several owners on LandCruiserForum.com report brake fade from relying on Downhill Assist alone. At the Pikes Peak brake check, a ranger measured one owner's rotors at 575°F.

SO FAR, SO TOYOTA

Americans bought it. Wikipedia counts 29,113 US sales in 2024 and 43,946 in 2025. With the First Edition gone, Toyota's 2027 line-up is two grades: the 1958 at $58,180 and the Land Cruiser at $64,255 (base MSRP, per Toyota). New options are an 'Inked' paint color and a $980 raised air intake, which Motor1 is careful to point out is not a watertight snorkel.

The recall list so far is mostly software and paperwork: an instrument-cluster update for displays that can go blank at start-up, a corrected load-capacity label on some distributor-accessorized 2024 trucks, a rear-camera fix, and an interim notice in 2026 for a fuel-pipe connector that can come loose. None of it is the kind of mechanical horror story that gets a truck a reputation, though it also isn't the 'never needs anything' legend some buyers expect. Notably, the US 250 doesn't use the V35A twin-turbo V6 whose bearing recalls have affected the Lexus LX, Tundra and some 2024 GX 550s.

SIDEBAR · THE WALRUS

The 250 on 37s, done properly

Slee Off-Road and Arctic Trucks North America built what they describe as the first US-spec Land Cruiser 250 AT37, nicknamed 'The Walrus'. It isn't just a lift. Arctic Trucks enlarges the wheel wells and moves the rear axle, which extends the wheelbase, so 37-inch BFGoodrich KO3s fit at full compression on a minimal lift.

Slee lists 11 inches of front wheel travel (2 more than stock) and 12 inches at the rear (3 more), from three-way-adjustable R53/AT dampers. The truck also gets 4.56 gears, an ARB front Air Locker to go with the factory rear locker, Slee aluminum skids and SliderSteps, and a 29-gallon fuel tank in place of the stock 17.9. It's the most complete answer anyone has given to the 250's two weak points: tire clearance and range.

How it’s built

Under the retro bodywork is a modern Toyota truck: a ladder frame on the GA-F platform it shares with the 300 Series and the Lexus GX 550, an independent front end with a coil-sprung solid rear axle, and a full-time four-wheel-drive system. In US trucks that's paired with a 2.4-liter turbo-hybrid and lockers as standard. The wheelbase is 112.2 inches, the same figure the 80 Series introduced.

What to check before you buy

  1. 01
    Know which grade you're looking atThe 1958 has round lamps, cloth seats, 245/70R18 tires on 18x7 wheels and no stabilizer disconnect. The Land Cruiser grade has rectangular lamps, 265/70R18 tires, and SDM availability. The 2024 First Edition (5,000 built for North America) keeps the round lamps and adds rock rails, a front skid and a roof rack. Premium Package trucks ride on 20-inch wheels with 265/60R20 tires.
  2. 02
    Run the VIN for recallsCampaigns so far include an instrument-cluster software update for displays that can go blank (about 82,000 Toyota and Lexus vehicles, announced May 2026), a corrected load-capacity label on certain 2024 trucks accessorized by Gulf States Toyota, a rear-camera software fix, and a 2024–2027 recall (Toyota 26TA23, interim notice 26TB23; NHTSA 26V673000) for a fuel-tube connector that can disconnect and leak. Check NHTSA or the Toyota app.
  3. 03
    Listen for squeal in reverseFront-brake squeal when backing up is covered by TSB T-SB-0006-26, which supersedes T-SB-0046-25 and replaces pads and shims after a technician confirms the problem. Owners say you can tell it's been done from the newer anti-rattle springs on the caliper.
  4. 04
    Check the hybrid-battery ventsThe cooling intakes look like speaker grilles in the lower side trim at the rear door opening. Make sure nothing is blocking them. Owners have found cargo and even a plastic bag over them, and some owners got battery cooling-fan maintenance warnings at low mileage; in one case the dealer found nothing and cleared a reminder it thought had been set at the wrong interval.
  5. 05
    Test the 12 V batteryOwners on LandCruiserForum.com report no-starts after leaving doors or the hatch open for a few minutes at camp. Have the 12 V battery load-tested and ask whether it has been replaced.
  6. 06
    Look underneathOn used trucks, pull the front skid plate. Owners report stripped skid-plate bolt threads from repeated oil changes. Look for trail damage, and check whether any lift kit came with an alignment.
  7. 07
    Ask for diff and transfer-case service recordsThe owner's manual calls for 75W-85 GL-5 in the differentials, and owners report the dealer parts counter doesn't always stock it. Ask what was used.
  8. 08
    Do the range mathA 17.9-gallon tank at the EPA's 23 mpg combined is about 410 miles in theory, and less with bigger tires, a roof rack or a trailer. If you travel remote, budget for jerry cans or an aftermarket long-range tank.

Spotting the real thing

Three US grades share one powertrain, and the differences are mostly in kit. The badge to check is First Edition, which is easy to copy.

  1. ID
    1958Round headlights, unpainted plastic body panels, cloth seats and 245/70R18 tires on 18x7 wheels. Toyota lists rear and center locking differentials as standard on the 1958; the Stabilizer Disconnect Mechanism is a Land Cruiser-grade item.
  2. ID
    Land Cruiser gradeRectangular headlights, painted panels, heated and ventilated front seats, wider 265/70R18 tires, rear locker and stabilizer disconnect. The Premium Package brings 20-inch wheels.
  3. ID
    First Edition2024 only. Limited to 5,000 for North America, it combines the round headlights with a factory roof rack, rock rails and front skid plate. Check the window sticker or build sheet.
  4. ID
    Name confusionIn the US and much of Europe it's just 'Land Cruiser'. Japan calls it the Land Cruiser 250, and Australia and other markets keep the Prado name. The Lexus GX 550 is the same platform.

SYMPTOMSRubbing at full lock or compression after fitting tires over about 33 inches.
THE FIXThreepiece's fitment guide: remove the mud flaps, crush brackets and canards (they bolt or clip on and can go back later). Invert and heat-form the plastic fender liners. If you still need room, radius the back of the front fenders or trim a few inches from the front bumper. Offset matters: the guide's 35-inch example on a stock-height 1958 used 17x8 +25 wheels, and it warns that near-zero offsets add a lot of poke. Get an alignment afterwards.
TOOLS & PARTSSocket set · trim tools · heat gun · cutting tool · 17-inch or 18-inch wheels with the right offset (6x139.7, 95.1 mm bore, M14x1.5 lugs).
WHERE TO BUY PARTSWheel & tire package builder (6x139.7) · ThreePiece.us ↗Amazon ↗LC250 / GX550 wheel & tire fitment guide (offsets that work) · ThreePiece.us ↗Amazon ↗
Sunroof leak and a damp headliner →
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 1 hour to clean the drains; a dealer visit if the seal is at faultSHOP COST · Free to clean; seal work under warranty
SYMPTOMSWater dripping through the overhead console with the roof shut, a damp headliner or passenger footwell, or a musty smell after rain. NHTSA complaints include a rear section of the sunroof that doesn't seal and a cabin that smells of mildew every time it rains.
THE FIXOpen the roof and clean out the housing, especially the corners where the drains are. One LandCruiserForum owner found his corner drains plugged with leaves and plant matter, so water overflowed into the headliner. He cleared it with rags, a shop vac and compressed air. Keep the seals clean and the drains clear. If water still gets in once the drains are clear, it's a warranty job: UK owners report dealers removing the glass and fitting a new seal under Toyota's instructions, and one says his dealer found a TSB involving new sealant (no number was given). Some trucks needed more than one visit.
TOOLS & PARTSShop vac · compressed air · rags · flashlight
WHERE TO BUY PARTSSunroof drain cleaning tool (search) · Amazon ↗
Quiet a vibrating driver's mirror →
DIFFICULTY · 1 / 5TIME · ≈ 30 minutesSHOP COST · A few dollars in thin plastic and felt
SYMPTOMSAt highway speed the driver's mirror glass shakes enough to blur the view. Owners say the passenger side is solid. One NHTSA complainant says the dealer confirmed it and called it common to all Land Cruisers.
THE FIXReport it to the dealer first, while it's under warranty. On LandCruiserForum, dealers have tightened the mirror or replaced the whole assembly, sometimes twice, without curing it. Owners think the driver-side assembly simply has too much play, and their fix is a shim. One member cut a C-shaped piece of thin plastic lid with peel-and-stick felt on both faces and reported about a 90% reduction, with a little shake left on rough roads. Another used a roughly 1.6 mm Rubbermaid lid and says pivoting the mirror forward makes it easier to fit. A bare plastic shim rubbed the paint when the mirror folded, so use the felt.
TOOLS & PARTSScissors or a craft knife · thin plastic lid · adhesive felt

Asked on every forum

Land Cruiser 250 Series questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

Factory tires are about 31.5 inches on the 1958 and 32.6 inches on the other grades. Threepiece's fitment guide shows 275/70R18s (about 33 inches) on stock wheels and suspension with plenty of clearance and no trimming. Owners go a bit bigger: 285/70R18s (about 33.7 inches) on stock suspension, including one reported on 18x9 +40 wheels with no rubbing on gentle trails. An LT275/70R18 KO3 (33.2 inches) just fits in the spare well. 35s are possible on stock suspension, but only after removing the mud flaps, crush brackets and canards, heat-forming the fender liners and choosing the right wheel offset.

SOURCE · THREEPIECE · LANDCRUISERFORUM.COM
How much can it tow?

6,000 lb, according to Toyota, and the 1958 grade comes with a trailer brake controller. Owners note that a small engine doesn't give much engine braking, so on long grades use the transmission and low range rather than the service brakes alone.

SOURCE · TOYOTA · LANDCRUISERFORUM.COM
What fuel economy and range should I expect?

The EPA rates it at 22 city, 25 highway and 23 combined. Car and Driver averaged 20 mpg, and Flexmotor saw about 21 off-road and 24 on the highway. The tank holds 17.9 gallons, which is why long-range tanks and jerry cans are popular.

SOURCE · KBB · CAR AND DRIVER · FLEXMOTOR
Does it have lockers?

Every US grade has a locking center differential and an electronic locking rear differential. There's no factory front locker. Slee's AT37 build added an ARB Air Locker up front.

SOURCE · TOYOTA · SLEE
Why only a four-cylinder hybrid? Can I get the diesel?

North America and China only get the 2.4-liter i-FORCE MAX hybrid. The 2.8-liter diesel, the 2.7 petrol and the non-hybrid 2.4 turbo are sold elsewhere. If you want a V6 on this platform in the US, the Lexus GX 550 has a 349-hp 3.4-liter twin-turbo.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA
Is it a 'real' Land Cruiser or just a Prado?

It's a Prado, and the Prado has always been a real Land Cruiser: ladder frame, live rear axle, full-time 4WD with low range. It shares the GA-F platform with the 300 Series, and its 112.2-inch wheelbase is the same figure the 80 Series introduced. It's smaller than the 200 Series it replaced in the US and seats two rows, not three.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · KBB
Which grade should I buy for off-road use?

The 1958 is the cheapest way into center and rear lockers, but it doesn't offer the Stabilizer Disconnect Mechanism, which gives the front axle more articulation. The Land Cruiser grade (and the 2024 First Edition) can have SDM and comes on wider 265/70R18 tires. For 2027 the 1958 starts at $58,180 and the Land Cruiser at $64,255 (base MSRP).

SOURCE · TOYOTA · THREEPIECE
What oil and fluids does it take?

0W-20 engine oil (owners report about 5.9 quarts with the filter), 75W-85 GL-5 in the differentials according to the manual, and Toyota 75W 'LF' in the transfer case. Check the owner's manual for your truck's exact figures.

SOURCE · LANDCRUISERFORUM.COM
What are the lift options?

ICON sells 1.25–3 inch systems for the 250, Dobinsons offers a 2-inch kit, and Slee's dealer build used an ADS 2.5-inch system. Threepiece tested a 1.5-inch Tuff Country leveling kit with 285/70R17s and reported no rubbing.

SOURCE · ICON · DOBINSONS · SLEE · THREEPIECE
Is the new raised air intake a snorkel?

No. The $980 factory raised intake offered for 2027 isn't watertight, so Motor1 notes it doesn't improve wading the way a sealed snorkel would. It helps with dust.

SOURCE · MOTOR1
Is it reliable? Any recalls?

It's early days. Recalls so far are an instrument-cluster software update, a corrected load-capacity label, a rear-camera fix, and a 2024–2027 fuel-tube connector recall. Common owner complaints are front-brake squeal in reverse (fixed by a TSB), 12 V battery drain, and hybrid-battery fan warnings. The US 250 doesn't use the V35A V6 involved in the LX and Tundra bearing recalls.

SOURCE · TOYOTA · PICKUPTRUCKTALK · NHTSA
