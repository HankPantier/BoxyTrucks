Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Every FJ45 or FJ55 is now 40 to 60 years old, so start from zero: change every fluid, grease every fitting and set the valves. Fluid types and capacities below are from Toyota publications as compiled on IH8MUD for the 40 and 55 series; the FJ55 shares the FJ40's F/2F engine and driveline lubricants.
