Toyota Land Cruiser 100 Series maintenance schedule

Toyota's 2005 Scheduled Maintenance Guide covers the 100 Series Land Cruiser on a 5,000-mile / 6-month oil service. Every 30,000 miles adds filters and greasing the drive shaft bushing, propeller shafts and wheel bearings. The 2UZ-FE V8's timing belt is due at 90,000 miles. Toyota's guide doesn't schedule the AHC fluid; the LX 470's Lexus schedule replaces it at 60,000 and 120,000.

Service schedule

From Toyota's 2005 Scheduled Maintenance Guide (Land Cruiser items). Earlier and later years are similar; check your book.

WHENWHAT TO DO
Every 5,000 mi / 6 months
  • Engine oil and filter
  • Rotate tires and inspect brakes
Every 15,000 mi / 18 months
  • Lubricate and re-torque the propeller shafts
  • Inspect ball joints, boots, coolant, exhaust, diff oil, radiator and steering
Every 30,000 mi / 36 months
  • Engine air filter and cabin filter
  • Grease the drive shaft bushing, propeller shafts and wheel bearings
  • Limited-slip diff oil (if fitted)
  • Inspect transmission fluid and transfer case oil
60,000 mi
  • Everything in the 30,000-mile service
  • First drive belt inspection (then every 15,000 mi); inspect valve clearance
90,000 mi
  • Timing belt (do the water pump and thermostat with it)
100,000 mi, then every 50,000 mi / 60 months
  • Engine coolant
120,000 mi
  • Spark plugs
  • Inspect valve clearance

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Desert or dirt roads: inspect ball joints, boots, air filter and steering linkage and lubricate the propshafts every 5,000 miles. Salted roads: lubricate the propshafts every 5,000. Towing: front and rear diff oil every 15,000, and transmission fluid and transfer case oil at 60,000 and 120,000.
