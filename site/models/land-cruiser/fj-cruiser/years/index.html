Toyota FJ Cruiser years to avoid, and the best years to buy

Every Toyota FJ Cruiser model year: which ones owners and specialists recommend, which have documented problems, which carry recalls, and what they sell for now.

Read the full Toyota FJ Cruiser guideCommon problemsBiggest tireSpecs & fluids
The short answer

2010–14 if you can. Those trucks have the dual VVT-i engine on regular fuel, and CarGurus says the inner-fender bulge mostly shows up on 2007–09 trucks, while 2007 draws far more owner complaints than any other year. IH8MUD owners add that some pre-February 2007 builds had a brittle rear ring and pinion, so check the door-jamb build date. They also point out that an early truck you plan to re-gear anyway isn't much of a loss. Rust history and how the truck was used matter more than the year.

Toyota FJ Cruiser year by year

YEARVERDICTWHAT CHANGED
2003—FJ Cruiser concept shown in Voodoo Blue at the Detroit auto show
2005—Production version shown at the Chicago auto show in February; Hino starts building it at Hamura in January 2006
2007BewareLaunch: 239-hp 1GR-FE on premium, six-speed manual (full-time 4WD) or five-speed automatic; 3,200 TRD Special Editions. CarGurus says owners report far more problems with 2007s than any other year, and inner fender aprons on early trucks can bulge and crack. IH8MUD owners add that some builds before February 2007 had a brittle rear ring and pinion
2008—Side curtain and torso airbags standard; first Trail Teams Special Edition (Iceberg, 3,200 built)
2009—Optional backup camera; no Trail Teams edition, a TRD package instead. US sales fall to 11,941
2010Sweet spotDual VVT-i 1GR-FE: 259 hp on regular fuel, slightly better economy, and a slightly larger rear diff. CarGurus ties the fender-apron bulge mainly to 2007–09 trucks, so 2010–14 are the years owners usually steer buyers toward
2011—260 hp; new front-seat hinges for easier rear-seat access; RHD sales begin in Australia and New Zealand
2013—CRAWL control on Trail Teams automatics; access-door panel recall covers 2007–2013 trucks
2014—Final US model year; 2,500 Trail Teams Ultimate Editions in Heritage Blue
2016–2018—Australian exports end in August 2016; Japan Final Edition in September 2017, Japanese sales end January 2018
2022—Middle East Final Edition (1,000 units); production ends in December

“Sweet spot” means owners and specialists recommend those years; “Beware” means documented problems; “Recall” means check the recall was done rather than walk away.
