Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing has been done, and assume the driveshafts have never seen grease. Intervals below come from Toyota's 2011 FJ Cruiser Warranty & Maintenance Guide; capacities come from AMSOIL's lookups for 2011 and 2007 trucks. Specs changed in 2010 (0W-20 oil, larger oil capacity, 75W-85 diff oil), so check your owner's manual for 2007–2009 trucks.
