Toyota FJ Cruiser maintenance schedule

Toyota's 2009 FJ Cruiser Scheduled Maintenance Guide runs on a 5,000-mile / 6-month oil service. The 4x4's propeller shafts get lubricated and re-torqued every 15,000 miles, and the diffs, transfer case and transmission are inspected rather than changed unless you tow. On a truck this age, the frame and the front axle actuator matter as much as the schedule.

Service schedule

From Toyota's 2009 FJ Cruiser Scheduled Maintenance Guide (4WD items apply to 4WD trucks only). Other model years are similar; check your guide.

WHENWHAT TO DO
Every 5,000 mi / 6 months
  • Replace engine oil and filter
  • Clean the cabin filter
  • Rotate tires and inspect the brakes
Every 15,000 mi / 18 months
  • Replace the cabin filter
  • Lubricate the propeller shafts (4WD)
  • Inspect coolant, front and rear diff oil and the steering box
Every 30,000 mi / 36 months
  • Replace the engine air filter
  • Measure brake thickness and runout
  • Inspect transfer case oil, transmission fluid or oil, fuel lines and the fuel cap gasket
60,000 and 120,000 mi
  • Everything in the 30,000-mile service
  • Inspect drive belts and valve clearance
100,000 mi / 120 months, then every 50,000 mi / 60 months
  • Replace engine coolant

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Dirt or dusty roads: inspect the ball joints, boots, steering linkage and air filter, lubricate and re-torque the propshafts and tighten chassis and body bolts; Toyota asks for daily checks after sand, mud or water. Towing: replace the front and rear diff oil at 15,000, 30,000, 60,000, 90,000 and 120,000 miles, the transfer case at each 30,000, and the manual gearbox oil at 30,000 and 90,000 or automatic fluid at 60,000 and 120,000.
