PART 01

Overview

Where it came from, why it matters, and how it changed year to year.

The story

The FJ Cruiser began as a show car. Toyota unveiled the concept, painted Voodoo Blue, at the Detroit auto show in January 2003, showed the production version at Chicago in February 2005, and had its subsidiary Hino Motors start building it at Hamura, Japan, in January 2006. Underneath was a ladder frame closely related to the Land Cruiser Prado's; on top sat a cartoon of the FJ40, white roof and all. America got it for model years 2007 through 2014. Other markets kept it until a 1,000-unit Final Edition for the Middle East closed out production in December 2022.

A FORTY FOR A NEW CENTURY

By the time FJ40 production ended in 1984, the Land Cruiser had grown bigger and plusher. According to Larry Edsall's book on the truck, the idea of a new FJ with the old one's toughness came from product planner Dave Danzer and sales vice-president Yoshi Inaba in the mid-1990s. Danzer and Akio Toyoda quietly set up a shop at the NUMMI plant to test it, putting the bodies of Brazilian Toyota Bandeirantes, which descended from the FJ40, on Tacoma underpinnings. Toyota's Calty design studio in California then took over.

Inside Toyota the project was called the Rugged Youth Utility, aimed at young male buyers the company felt it was losing. Several concepts were tried before a retro design by Jin Won Kim, a 24-year-old Calty designer, won, with William Chergosky drawing the interior. The FJ40 cues are everywhere: a near-vertical windshield with three wipers, TOYOTA spelled out across the grille instead of the corporate badge, round headlights and a white roof. Rear-hinged access doors replaced the B-pillars, and high-strength steel took over their side-impact job.

DEVELOPED ON THE RUBICON

From mid-2004 Toyota ran development mules over the hardest trails it could find, including Moab, the Angeles National Forest, the Mojave Desert and the Rubicon, and retuned the A-TRAC traction control and the suspension as a result. Underneath, the FJ shares hardware with the 120-series Prado, the 4th-gen 4Runner and the GX 470: double-wishbone front suspension, a four-link coil-sprung rear axle with a lateral rod, and the 4.0-liter 1GR-FE V6. A 4x4 FJ has 9.6 inches of ground clearance and, Toyota says, can wade 28 inches of water.

The drivetrain choice is the interesting part. The six-speed manual came only with full-time 4WD: a Torsen center differential splits torque 40:60, locks at 50:50, and 3.909 axle gears give a 41.84:1 crawl ratio. The five-speed automatic got part-time 4WD, 3.727 gears and a 33.76:1 crawl ratio, and was also sold as a 4x2. Toyota's 2014 specifications list the rear electric locker as standard with the manual and optional with the automatic, and A-TRAC as part of an option package on 4x4s.

THE PRICE OF STYLE

The styling that sold the truck also held it back. Reviewers praised its off-road ability and criticized its huge blind spots, small cargo area and cramped, awkward rear seat. Toyota added an optional backup camera for 2009 and new front-seat hinges for easier rear access in 2011. US sales fell from 56,225 in 2006 to 11,941 in 2009, and critics blamed rising fuel prices, more competition in the segment and the Jeep Wrangler.

Toyota's answer for 2010 was a dual VVT-i version of the 1GR-FE, with 259 hp on 87-octane fuel instead of 239 hp on 91, and slightly better economy. Early trucks also had a structural problem. The inner fender aprons in the engine bay of 2007 and some early 2008 trucks could bulge and crack, and Toyota changed the design. Owners still report cracked aprons on early trucks, so that's the first thing to look for when you open the hood.

HOW IT ENDED

From 2008, Toyota built short runs of Trail Teams Special Editions, each in one color with blacked-out trim, TRD wheels and Bilstein shocks. On 5 November 2013 it announced that 2014 would be the last US model year, and the final Trail Teams truck became the Ultimate Edition: 2,500 built in Heritage Blue with a white grille surround, remote-reservoir rear Bilsteins, an aluminum front skid plate and, on automatics, CRAWL control. By the end of 2013, US sales alone had passed 200,000.

Production continued for other markets. Exports to Australia ended in August 2016, Japan got a Final Edition in September 2017 and stopped sales in January 2018, and the Middle East Final Edition closed production in December 2022. Toyota reused the name, though not the formula, for the 2026 Land Cruiser FJ, a smaller Hilux-based truck with a 2.7-liter four that isn't planned for North America. The original is now a collectible: on Bring a Trailer in late 2026, prices ran from $12,500 to more than $56,000.

SIDEBAR · VOLUNTEERS WITH TOW STRAPS

The FJ Cruiser Trail Teams

Before customers had their trucks, Toyota recruited volunteer Trail Teams from Toyota and Land Cruiser clubs. Paul Williamsen, Toyota's product education manager at the time, wrote that truck marketing manager Paul Czaplicki proposed the program in 2005, and that Bill Burke ran a week-long boot camp for the volunteers in Grand Junction, Colorado, in March 2006. Two of the volunteers he names drove FJ80s.

Each team drove two pre-production FJ Cruisers, towing enclosed trailers, to public trail events including Moab, Tellico and the Rubicon. They marked trails, gave first aid, recovered stuck vehicles, picked up trash and cooked camp meals until the passes snowed shut in November 2006. Toyota funded the program again for 2007 and 2008, and its success led to the Trail Teams special editions.

PART 02

Specs & capability

How it’s built, what it can do, and what fits under it.

How it’s built

The FJ Cruiser is a body-on-frame truck built from Toyota's mid-size Prado family of parts: independent double-wishbone front suspension, a four-link coil-sprung live rear axle and the 4.0-liter 1GR-FE V6. What's underneath depends on the gearbox. Manual trucks have full-time 4WD with a lockable Torsen center differential. Automatics have part-time 4WD, with an electric actuator that connects the front axle when you shift into four-wheel drive.

What to check before you buy

  1. 01
    Get under it with a hammer and a lightFrame rust is the FJ's big weakness in salt-belt states. An IH8MUD member who shopped the Northeast said every truck he looked at had rot around the rear control-arm brackets or had already been patched. Another warned about rust hidden under thick fresh undercoating. Rust Buster's list of FJ repair sections (rail ends, center rails, crossmembers, coil buckets, body mounts) shows where to probe.
  2. 02
    Open the hood and look at the inner fendersThe engine-bay inner fender aprons on 2007 and some early 2008 trucks could bulge and crack, and Toyota changed the design. Owners on IH8MUD report cracks that never grew and others that went all the way through. Look on both sides behind the headlights and price in a repair if they're split.
  3. 03
    Check the rear access doorsThe front seat-belt retractors are mounted in the rear-hinged access doors, and the door panels can crack after years of being slammed. Toyota recalled 2007–2013 trucks for it (NHTSA 13V098000). Run the VIN for open recalls and look at the inner door panels.
  4. 04
    Feel for a clunk when it downshiftsA harsh jolt into first after sitting in Drive, as if you'd been rear-ended, is covered by Toyota bulletin T-SB-0046-12 for 2007–2013 FJ Cruisers and Tacomas. Greasing a dry rear driveshaft slip yoke often fixes it. Ask whether the driveshafts have been greased; Toyota calls for it every 5,000 miles on dirt, salt or when towing.
  5. 05
    Manual trucks: listen at the clutchA squeak or rattle from the gearbox that stops when you press the clutch is T-SB-0365-10 on 2007–2011 six-speed FJs: updated release bearing, fork support and release cylinder, with the transmission out. Manuals are much rarer than automatics, so expect to search harder.
  6. 06
    Test every 4WD modeShift into 4H and 4L, try the rear locker and A-TRAC, and watch for warning lights that stay on. On automatic trucks the front axle is engaged by an electric disconnect actuator on the front differential; IH8MUD owners say they fail mostly from unsoldered internal contacts or water getting in.
  7. 07
    Know which engine you're buying2007–2009 trucks make 239 hp and are rated on premium fuel; the dual VVT-i engine from 2010 makes 259–260 hp on regular. 4x2 automatics exist, so check the transfer-case lever. Trail Teams editions are monotone with blacked-out trim; confirm a claimed edition with the window sticker or a dealer build record.
  8. 08
    Clear the smaller recalls too2014 trucks built 7–20 August 2013 need a new intermediate steering shaft (15V081000). Ultimate Editions had front lower ball-joint and lower-arm bolts checked for torque (14V539000). Trucks with the TRD brake kit or Toyota auxiliary driving lamps have their own campaigns.

Spotting the real thing

There was essentially one US trim, so what you're checking is the drivetrain, the engine generation and whether a claimed special edition is real.

  1. ID
    VIN and plantUS FJs start with JT and were built by Hino at Hamura, Japan. The 10th character is the model year (7 = 2007, A = 2010, E = 2014). 4x4s are GSJ15 and 4x2s GSJ10. On Bring a Trailer, 2007 VINs begin JTEBU11F and 2010–2014 VINs begin JTEBU4BF.
  2. ID
    Manual or automatic 4WDManual trucks have full-time 4WD with a center-differential lock and no 2WD position. Automatic 4x4s have a part-time transfer case with 2H, and automatics were also sold as 4x2s with no transfer-case lever.
  3. ID
    Locker and A-TRACLook for the rear-locker and A-TRAC switches on the dash. The locker was standard with the manual and optional with the automatic (2014 spec), so don't assume every automatic has one.
  4. ID
    Trail Teams editionsTrail Teams trucks are monotone in that year's color, with blacked-out mirrors, handles and bumper caps, Trail Teams badges and TRD wheels. The 2014 Ultimate Edition is Heritage Blue with a white grille surround and a 'One of 2500' dash plaque. Badges are easy to copy, so ask for the window sticker.
  5. ID
    TRD Special EditionThe 2007 TRD Special Edition is Black Diamond with a black roof (other FJs have a white roof), with TRD Bilsteins, rock rails, a cat-back exhaust and gunmetal TRD wheels; 3,200 were built.
  6. ID
    Engine generation2007–2009 trucks have the single VVT-i engine (239 hp, premium fuel); 2010–2014 trucks have dual VVT-i (259–260 hp, regular).

Importing & legality

Every US-market FJ Cruiser (2007–2014) is federally legal. The right-hand-drive Japanese and Australian trucks (sold from late 2010 and 2011) and the FJs built for the Middle East and other markets until 2022 were never certified for the US, so under NHTSA's 25-year rule they can't be imported until each is 25 years old, which for the earliest RHD trucks means about 2035.

Repair rotted rear frame sections →

Cure the downshift clunk at the rear driveshaft →
DIFFICULTY · 1 / 5TIME · ≈ 30 minutesSHOP COST · A tube of grease; a replacement rear driveshaft under T-SB-0046-12 was quoted at roughly $350–550
SYMPTOMSA harsh jolt, as if you'd been rear-ended, when the transmission drops into first after the truck has been sitting in Drive. Toyota's bulletin covers 2007–2013 FJ Cruisers and Tacomas.
THE FIXStart with grease. A dry slip yoke binds and then lets go. Toyota's schedule says to lubricate the propeller-shaft spiders and slide yokes with lithium chassis grease, use molybdenum lithium grease on the double-cardan joint, and re-torque the shaft bolts. Do it every 5,000 miles if you drive on dirt or salted roads or tow. If the clunk survives a proper greasing and the driveshaft is confirmed as the cause, T-SB-0046-12 calls for a new rear driveshaft.
TOOLS & PARTSGrease gun with a needle or angled coupler · lithium chassis grease · moly grease for the double-cardan · torque wrench.

Fix a failed front-axle disconnect actuator (automatic 4x4s) →
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 1 hourSHOP COST · Free if you can solder; a replacement actuator costs more
SYMPTOMSThe transfer case goes into 4WD but the front wheels don't drive. The actuator may have water inside.
THE FIXOn part-time trucks an electric actuator on the front differential slides a collar to connect the passenger-side axle when you shift into 4WD. IH8MUD member BMThiker says most failures come from internal contacts that only rest on their terminals; he opened the housing and soldered the motor and switch contacts. Water can also get in through a failed seal, a cracked housing or a damaged breather tube. On the trail, remove the actuator (a few 12 mm bolts), move the shift fork and slide the collar over by hand, then refit it to get 4WD back.
TOOLS & PARTS12 mm socket · soldering iron and solder · dielectric grease · tools to drop the skid plate if fitted.

Replace a front wheel bearing hub →
DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · ≈ 2 hours per sideSHOP COST · One IH8MUD owner paid about $85 for a Beck/Arnley assembly
SYMPTOMSA growl or vibration from one front corner. One owner's bearing failed at 64,000 miles.
THE FIXPull the dust cap and break the axle nut with a 35 mm socket and a long bar. Remove the caliper and rotor, then the four 17 mm bolts that hold the hub to the knuckle. Once they're cracked loose, switch to an open-end wrench, because the bolts can't back fully out with the hub in place. Knock the hub free and keep or replace the large O-ring behind it. A complete hub-and-bearing assembly bolts straight in, which saves you pressing a bearing without a 20-ton press.
TOOLS & PARTS35 mm socket · breaker bar · 17 mm open-end wrench · big hammer · torque wrench · new axle nut.

Chop the front body mounts for bigger tires →
DIFFICULTY · 4 / 5TIME · ≈ Half a day, with weldingSHOP COST · $34.95 for Metal Tech's plates, plus welding
SYMPTOMSOversize or wide tires rubbing at full lock or under compression, usually the inside of the tire hitting the front body mount.
THE FIXIH8MUD owners say rubbing on FJs is as much about tire width and wheel backspacing as diameter, and that the front body mount is the usual contact point at full lock. The fix is to cut the mount back and weld a cap over the opening. Metal Tech's laser-cut 3/16-inch plates show what to cut and come with instructions a welding shop can follow. Also remove or heat and reshape the plastic mud flap, which one owner found was the only thing his 285/70R17s touched on stock suspension.

Check, and live with or fix, cracked inner fender walls →
DIFFICULTY · 4 / 5TIME · ≈ 15 minutes to inspect · body-shop work to repairSHOP COST · Free to inspect. A proper repair is body-shop labor, and Toyota's own fix meant pulling the dash and front end.
SYMPTOMSA bulge, then a crack or tear, in the engine-bay inner fender aprons at the crumple zones, usually on both sides. 2007 and early-2008 trucks are the usual victims. Owners have filed dozens of NHTSA complaints. In the worst ones, the fenders dropped and bound on the doors or changed the door gaps.
THE FIXOpen the hood and look along both aprons behind the headlights. If you see a bulge, scrape back the undercoating: one IH8MUD member's 'small bulge' turned out to be a crack once a Toyota factory rep cleaned it off. Plenty of cracks stop growing. One 2007 owner cracked his at 58,000 miles and traded the truck at 107,000 with little change, and another's grew slowly after a hard landing in a ditch. Toyota's warranty fix used redesigned aprons with support brackets welded inside, which meant removing the dash and front end. An IH8MUD member warned that welding sparks under the dash started a fire that destroyed one FJ, so any welder needs to strip or shield the dash first. For a truck with open cracks, have a body shop weld and reinforce the apron, then seal and paint it. With a heavy steel bumper and winch, avoid dropping the nose hard.

Repair a chip before the windshield cracks →
DIFFICULTY · 1 / 5TIME · ≈ 30 minutes for a chip · replacement is a glass-shop jobSHOP COST · One IH8MUD member paid $50 for a chip repair. Early replacement quotes on IH8MUD ran about $1,600–1,800 with dealer glass.
SYMPTOMSChips from road debris that grow into long cracks, often from the bottom center of the glass. More than 60 NHTSA complaints across the 2007–2014 model years describe cracked FJ windshields, some after a pothole or cold weather and some from owners on their third or fourth windshield.
THE FIXThe nearly upright glass takes the full force of every rock, so fix chips while they're small. A resin repair kept one IH8MUD member's small crack from spreading. If it has to be replaced, have the shop order a new top molding too. An IH8MUD member said it often gets ruined coming off; his dealer molding was about $278. Another member listed PPG FW02652 as the replacement glass. After a replacement, check for leaks at the top molding and A-pillars in the first rain or car wash.
TOOLS & PARTSWindshield chip repair kit · glass cleaner · razor blade · UV light or sunshine to cure the resin

Asked on every forum

Toyota FJ Cruiser questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

About a 285/70R17 (roughly 33 x 11 inches). One owner on stock suspension found his only rubbed the driver's-side mud flap under compression and fixed it by heating and flattening the flap. A long-time IH8MUD member's stricter view is that only a 31–32 inch tire clears in every situation; above that you need a body mount chop. Width and wheel backspacing matter as much as diameter, because the inside of the tire hits the front body mount at full lock.

SOURCE · IH8MUD
What fits with a 3-inch lift?

With a 3-inch Old Man Emu lift, Slee Off-Road told one owner the limit is 285/70R17 unless you trim the body, and ARB told another 285/75R17. Several owners run 285/70R17s on factory wheels that way without rubbing. With the front body mounts chopped, owners run 305/70R17s, though one still rubs at full compression. One club's two FJs on 35s have trimmed body mounts and touch only at extreme flex and full lock.

SOURCE · IH8MUD
Manual or automatic?

They're different trucks underneath. The six-speed manual has full-time 4WD with a lockable Torsen center differential, 3.909 gears and a 41.84:1 crawl ratio. The five-speed automatic has part-time 4WD, 3.727 gears and a 33.76:1 crawl ratio. Toyota's 2014 specifications list the rear locker as standard on the manual and optional on the automatic. Manuals are much less common.

SOURCE · TOYOTA · IH8MUD
Does it have lockers?

A rear electric locker, plus A-TRAC brake-based traction control in an option package on 4x4s. There's no factory front locker; one IH8MUD member fitted a Harrop E-Locker to his front diff. On very early trucks the rear locker could override A-TRAC. Toyota synchronized the two on the 2007 TRD Special Edition and in all production from November 2006.

SOURCE · TOYOTA · WIKIPEDIA · IH8MUD
How much can it tow?

Toyota originally rated the FJ at 5,000 lb for both 4x4 and 4x2. Under the SAE J2807 standard, the 2014 rating with the Class IV hitch was 4,700 lb.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · TOYOTA
What fuel economy should I expect, and does it need premium?

EPA ratings for 4x4s range from 14 city / 19 highway (2009 manual) to 17/21 (2010 automatic); a 2014 automatic is 17/20 and a manual 15/18. 2007–2009 trucks were rated on premium. The dual VVT-i engine from 2010 is rated on regular, although Toyota recommends premium for towing, off-roading and high altitude.

SOURCE · FUELECONOMY.GOV · WIKIPEDIA
Which years are the best to buy?

2010–14 if you can. Those trucks have the dual VVT-i engine on regular fuel, and CarGurus says the inner-fender bulge mostly shows up on 2007–09 trucks, while 2007 draws far more owner complaints than any other year. IH8MUD owners add that some pre-February 2007 builds had a brittle rear ring and pinion, so check the door-jamb build date. They also point out that an early truck you plan to re-gear anyway isn't much of a loss. Rust history and how the truck was used matter more than the year.

SOURCE · CARGURUS · IH8MUD
What is the inner fender problem?

The engine-bay inner fender aprons on 2007 and some early 2008 trucks could bulge and crack, and Toyota changed the design. Owners report everything from cracks that never grew to splits that went right through. Check both sides before you buy.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · IH8MUD
How long do they last?

On IH8MUD's high-mileage thread, one Ontario owner passed 450,000 km (about 280,000 miles) on the original engine and transmission, and a Toronto owner reported 300k on a 2007 with one clutch replacement. Another member reports 250,000 miles of hard use without burning or leaking oil between oil changes.

SOURCE · IH8MUD
What were the Trail Teams editions?

Monotone, black-trimmed, TRD-equipped FJs built in one color per year: Iceberg 2008 (3,200), Sandstorm 2010 (1,500), Army Green 2011, Radiant Red 2012 and Cement Gray 2013 (2,500 each), and the 2014 Heritage Blue Ultimate Edition (2,500). There wasn't one for 2009; a white TRD package filled the gap.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA
Is the 2026 Land Cruiser FJ the same truck?

No. The Land Cruiser FJ (J240) is a smaller Hilux-based truck with a 2.7-liter four-cylinder and a six-speed automatic, built in Thailand from 2026. Toyota hasn't planned it for North America or Europe.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA
