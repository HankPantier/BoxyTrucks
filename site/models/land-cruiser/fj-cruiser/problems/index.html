Exercise your lockers

Engage the lockers every six months or so, on almost any boxy truck. Both of my locker problems, on the 80 Series and the G500, came from systems that weren’t used often enough.

Toyota FJ Cruiser problems to check before you buy

  1. Get under it with a hammer and a lightFrame rust is the FJ's big weakness in salt-belt states. An IH8MUD member who shopped the Northeast said every truck he looked at had rot around the rear control-arm brackets or had already been patched. Another warned about rust hidden under thick fresh undercoating. Rust Buster's list of FJ repair sections (rail ends, center rails, crossmembers, coil buckets, body mounts) shows where to probe.
  2. Open the hood and look at the inner fendersThe engine-bay inner fender aprons on 2007 and some early 2008 trucks could bulge and crack, and Toyota changed the design. Owners on IH8MUD report cracks that never grew and others that went all the way through. Look on both sides behind the headlights and price in a repair if they're split.
  3. Check the rear access doorsThe front seat-belt retractors are mounted in the rear-hinged access doors, and the door panels can crack after years of being slammed. Toyota recalled 2007–2013 trucks for it (NHTSA 13V098000). Run the VIN for open recalls and look at the inner door panels.
  4. Feel for a clunk when it downshiftsA harsh jolt into first after sitting in Drive, as if you'd been rear-ended, is covered by Toyota bulletin T-SB-0046-12 for 2007–2013 FJ Cruisers and Tacomas. Greasing a dry rear driveshaft slip yoke often fixes it. Ask whether the driveshafts have been greased; Toyota calls for it every 5,000 miles on dirt, salt or when towing.
  5. Manual trucks: listen at the clutchA squeak or rattle from the gearbox that stops when you press the clutch is T-SB-0365-10 on 2007–2011 six-speed FJs: updated release bearing, fork support and release cylinder, with the transmission out. Manuals are much rarer than automatics, so expect to search harder.
  6. Test every 4WD modeShift into 4H and 4L, try the rear locker and A-TRAC, and watch for warning lights that stay on. On automatic trucks the front axle is engaged by an electric disconnect actuator on the front differential; IH8MUD owners say they fail mostly from unsoldered internal contacts or water getting in.
  7. Know which engine you're buying2007–2009 trucks make 239 hp and are rated on premium fuel; the dual VVT-i engine from 2010 makes 259–260 hp on regular. 4x2 automatics exist, so check the transfer-case lever. Trail Teams editions are monotone with blacked-out trim; confirm a claimed edition with the window sticker or a dealer build record.
  8. Clear the smaller recalls too2014 trucks built 7–20 August 2013 need a new intermediate steering shaft (15V081000). Ultimate Editions had front lower ball-joint and lower-arm bolts checked for torque (14V539000). Trucks with the TRD brake kit or Toyota auxiliary driving lamps have their own campaigns.

Toyota FJ Cruiser recalls and service bulletins

  • Rear access-door panels can crack at the seat-belt retractor ↗ 2007–2013 · NHTSA 13V098000The front seat-belt retractors mount in the rear-hinged access doors; repeated hard slamming can crack the door panel.
  • Intermediate steering shaft weld ↗ 2014 (built 7–20 August 2013) · NHTSA 15V081000The shaft linking the steering wheel to the steering gear may have an inadequate weld; dealers replace it.
  • Trail Teams Ultimate Edition front suspension bolts ↗ 2014 · NHTSA 14V539000Lower ball-joint and lower-arm bolts may have been under-torqued when the TRD shocks and springs were fitted; dealers check and retighten them.
  • TRD brake kit outer brake tube ↗ 2007–2012 (accessory) · NHTSA 12E046000On trucks with dealer-fitted TRD front brake kits, a balance weight can damage the outer brake tube and leak fluid; the tube is replaced.
  • Toyota auxiliary driving lamps too bright ↗ 2007–2013 (accessory) · NHTSA 13V163000Bumper-mounted accessory lamps with 55 W bulbs exceed high-beam limits; dealers fit 35 W bulbs.
  • TPMS not recalibrated after accessory wheels ↗ 2008–2011 · NHTSA 11V148000Trucks given accessory wheels and LT tires before sale may not warn at the required pressure; FJs can't be recalibrated, so dealers replace the TPMS control unit.
  • Denso low-pressure fuel pump ↗ 2014 (some) · NHTSA 20V012000Added to Toyota's fuel-pump recall in March 2020; the pump can fail and stall the engine. Dealers replace it.
  • 6-speed manual clutch release bearing squeak or rattle ↗ · T-SB-0365-102007–2011 FJ Cruiser 6MT: replace the release bearing, fork support and release cylinder with updated parts.
  • Harsh downshift into first after sitting in Drive ↗ · T-SB-0046-122007–2013 FJ Cruiser and Tacoma 4x4s: replacement rear driveshaft once the shaft is confirmed as the cause; greasing a dry slip yoke often cures it.

Check any VIN for open recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls ↗, or see the recall tracker.

Owner questions about Toyota FJ Cruiser reliability

What is the inner fender problem?

The engine-bay inner fender aprons on 2007 and some early 2008 trucks could bulge and crack, and Toyota changed the design. Owners report everything from cracks that never grew to splits that went right through. Check both sides before you buy.
