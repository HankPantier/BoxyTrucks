Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume a 25-year-old import has had nothing done, whatever the auction sheet says. On a diesel the priorities are the timing belt and the cooling system, because heat is what kills a 1KZ-TE head. Specs below are from Roughtrax's KZJ90/95 data sheet unless noted. Petrol oil capacities are from Penrite's lookup for Australian Prados. V6 and 2.7 petrols differ in other ways too, so confirm in the manual for your engine.
