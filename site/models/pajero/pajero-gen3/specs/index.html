Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing has been done without receipts. On a US Montero the big jobs are the timing belt and water pump on the interference V6, a brake-fluid flush for the hydraulic brake booster, and Diaqueen SP-III in the automatic. Capacities below are AMSOIL's figures for a 2004 Montero 3.8; confirm them for 3.5 trucks and diesel imports.
