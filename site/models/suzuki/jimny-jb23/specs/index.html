Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Specs below are from the Suzuki owner's-manual tables BigJimny reproduces for UK-market M13A VVT trucks, with earlier-engine differences noted. A US import may be a kei JB23 with the K6A turbo, which has its own specs, so confirm your model code first. BigJimny's advice for any secondhand Jimny without history: change every fluid and filter, and the belts, straight away.
