Suzuki SJ / Samurai maintenance schedule

Suzuki's own Samurai maintenance book isn't published online that we could find. The one published interval we can attribute is the oil change in Olyslager's data (via Liqui Moly) for the 1990–98 Samurai. Everything else here is a purchase baseline: a 25-to-40-year-old Samurai should get every fluid, the timing belt and the ignition parts at once unless there are receipts.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

Not a Suzuki schedule. Oil interval from Olyslager / Liqui Moly; the rest is a purchase baseline from our buyer's guide. Check a factory service manual for capacities and specs.

WHENWHAT TO DO
Every 10,000 km (about 6,000 mi) / 6 months
  • Engine oil and filter (5,000 km / 4 months in severe use)
  • Grease the U-joints and check the steering joint and spring bushings
At purchase
  • Timing belt and tensioner unless documented (the G13A is non-interference, but a failure strands you)
  • Spark plugs, wires, cap and rotor; check the distributor O-ring for leaks
  • Air and fuel filters
  • Gearbox, transfer case, front and rear diff oil; check the knuckles for weeping
  • Brake fluid flush
Every 2 years
  • Coolant flush
  • Brake fluid flush

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Olyslager halves the oil interval to 5,000 km or 4 months for severe use. After water crossings, check the knuckle and diff oil for water.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover

ITEMWHENWHY
Transfer case mountsAt purchase and every serviceThey crack; reinforcing them is a common fix.
Transfer case shift sheetIf the lever gets sloppy or sticks in neutralThe plastic sheet breaks up with age.
Front knucklesEvery serviceWeeping gear oil means kingpin bearings and seals are due.
Front locking hubsAt purchaseThey must lock for 4WD to work.
