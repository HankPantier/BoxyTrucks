Fluids, capacities & service intervals

No US dealer will ever service one, so the owner's handbook is your bible. The grades and intervals below are Suzuki's handbook figures as transcribed by maintenanceschedule.com (2021 JB64/JB74 handbook) and jimny.joker.art.pl (2023 handbook for the five-door JC74, which shares the K15B and gearboxes). Capacities come from Liqui Moly's oil guide (Olyslager data) for the 2018–2024 Jimny 1.5 and match the five-door handbook. On a used truck with gaps in its history, BigJimny's rule for every Jimny applies: change every fluid and filter now.
