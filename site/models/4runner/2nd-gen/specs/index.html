Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Most surviving 2nd gens have 30 years of deferred maintenance, so baseline everything. On a V6, treat cooling-system health as life-or-death for the head gaskets. Factory capacities for the N120/N130 aren't reliably published online. Figures marked AMSOIL come from AMSOIL's application guide for the 1992 3VZ-E, and plug data from Denso's catalogue; check those and anything still marked below against the owner's or factory service manual.
