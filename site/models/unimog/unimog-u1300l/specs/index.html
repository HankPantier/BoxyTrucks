Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Most 435s in the US have spent years in a German army depot and then a container. Expect old fluids, aged tires and seals that have sat dry. Get the operator's manual for your engine first; Mercedes printed it in English. Then change every fluid before you drive it far, as one owner did before a 1,650 km trip home.
