Mercedes-Benz Unimog U1300L (435) maintenance schedule

Daimler-Benz's 1982 operating instructions for the 435 series (U 1300 L) do include a maintenance schedule, but the only copy we found online is a scan whose interval columns don't read reliably. So this page gives the jobs and intervals we could read clearly, a purchase baseline for a truck of unknown history, and clearly attributed owner advice. Use a legible manual for your truck's exact intervals.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

From the 1982 operating instructions where legible; the scan's other interval columns couldn't be read, so check a clean manual.

WHENWHAT TO DO
Daily, before driving
  • Engine oil level
  • Coolant level
Weekly (daily in heavy dust)
  • Clean the air cleaner element
Every 24 months
  • Check coolant hoses and replace the antifreeze
  • Replace the air cleaner element (or after it has been cleaned five times)
At purchase (unknown history)
  • Engine oil and filter, fuel filters and air filter
  • Drain and inspect all four portals, both differentials and the gearbox; refill with the correct oils
  • Brake and clutch fluid flush
  • Coolant, hoses and antifreeze
  • Air dryer cartridge and tank drains; check the system holds pressure
  • Test both diff locks on air
  • Grease every chassis point on the lubrication chart

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

The operating instructions call for daily air-cleaner checks in severe dust. One owner on Reddit's r/unimog changes portal oil about every thousand miles, far more often than the per-oil-change interval other owners use.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover

ITEMWHENWHY
Portal oilAbout every 1,000 miles (one owner's interval)An r/unimog owner says portals need frequent oil changes and the front ones should be checked for leaks while running.
Diff lock piston O-ringsIf a lock engages unreliablyA U1300L owner found worn O-rings and a rusty piston behind front locks that didn't always work.
