Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume a surplus 404 has sat for a while, and baseline everything. It has far more oil-filled places than a car: engine, gearbox, two diffs and four hub reductions, plus a steering box, an oil-bath air cleaner and a fan hub, idler and water pump that run in gear oil. The figures below are owners' charts and a translated factory schedule; confirm them in the 404 operator's manual.
