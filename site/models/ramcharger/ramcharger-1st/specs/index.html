Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume every fluid is old and every rubber part is original until you've seen receipts. The first generation has two jobs most owners forget: greasing the full-time front hubs, and the NP203's oil fill, which takes engine oil, not gear oil. We couldn't verify most factory capacities, so check them against the Dodge factory service manual for your year.
