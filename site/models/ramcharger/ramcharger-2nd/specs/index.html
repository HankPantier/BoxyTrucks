Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing has been serviced. These trucks share most of their service parts with 1981–93 Dodge half-ton pickups, but the details change a lot by year: carburetor, TBI or Magnum, NP208 or NP241, open-knuckle or CAD front axle. Check what you have, and buy the factory service manual for your year, because most capacities aren't published anywhere else.
