Maintenance schedule · 1991–2002

Isuzu Trooper (second generation) maintenance schedule

Isuzu's Trooper maintenance book isn't published online that we could find, and Isuzu left the US market long ago, so this page doesn't invent a factory schedule. It's a purchase baseline plus the intervals owners report. The 3.5 V6's oil consumption makes one habit more important than any interval: check the oil at every fill-up.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

Not an Isuzu schedule. Purchase baseline from our buyer's guide; intervals are owner reports from the Edmunds and Bob Is The Oil Guy forums, stated as such.

WHENWHAT TO DO
At purchase
  • Engine oil and filter, PCV valve (under the plastic intake cover), air and fuel filters
  • Coolant and thermostat flush
  • 4L30-E pan drop and filter (no pressure flush on an unknown gearbox)
  • Transfer case and both differentials
  • Power steering and brake fluid
  • Timing belt, tensioner, idlers and water pump if there's no record
Every fill-up (3.5 V6)
  • Check the oil level; several 3.5s ran dry without the oil light coming on
Every oil change
  • Grease the U-joints and driveshafts
  • Inspect the air filter
Every 10,000–15,000 mi
  • Drop the 4L30-E pan, clean the filter and refill what drained (an Edmunds owner's gradual-refresh method)
About 90,000 mi
  • Timing belt (one 2000 owner says 90,000 is the recommendation; owners plan 75,000–100,000)
  • Spark plugs and coil boots with the belt
Every 2 years
  • Brake fluid

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Towing and dusty roads call for shorter oil and filter changes. After water, check the diffs and transfer case, and wash packed snow, ice and mud off the speed sensors, which can upset the TOD system.
