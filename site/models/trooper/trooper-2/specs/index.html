Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Baseline everything, and on a 3.5 start by measuring the oil consumption. Acura's factory specifications give the engine-oil and coolant capacities below for the SLX twin. Isuzu's own fluid grades aren't widely published online; the gearbox, transfer-case, axle and steering fluids below come from Liqui Moly's lookup, so confirm viscosities in the owner's manual. Book the timing belt if there's no receipt.
