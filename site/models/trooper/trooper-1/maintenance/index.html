Isuzu Trooper (first generation) maintenance schedule

We couldn't find Isuzu's factory maintenance schedule for the first-generation Trooper online, so this page doesn't give factory intervals except where a parts maker publishes one. A Trooper of unknown history needs everything below at purchase; after that, follow the owner's manual for your engine.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

Purchase baseline only, plus Gates' published timing-belt interval.

WHENWHAT TO DO
At purchase (unknown history)
  • Engine oil and filter, air and fuel filters, ignition
  • Timing belt and tensioner on the 2.3, 2.6 and 2.2 diesel if there's no receipt
  • Coolant, hoses, thermostat, radiator cap; pressure-test the system
  • Manual or automatic transmission fluid, transfer case and both differentials
  • Front hubs and wheel bearings
  • Brake fluid and hoses
  • Check every wheel stud
Every 60,000 miles
  • Timing belt on the 2.3 (1986–87), 2.6 (1988–91) and 2.2 diesel (1984–87), per Gates

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

No factory severe-service schedule is online. Watch the temperature gauge closely: owners link cracked 2.6 heads to overheating.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover

ITEMWHENWHY
Timing belt60,000 miles (Gates)Gates marks the 2.3, 2.6 and 2.2 diesel as interference engines, so a snapped belt can bend valves.
Head gasket (2.6)At the first sign of coolant lossAn OHMO forum owner calls it a known weak point and says cracking usually follows overheating.
