Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing was done after the truck left the army. On a 710 or 712 that means about a dozen separate gear-oil fill points, a hydraulic system for the lockers as well as the brakes and clutch, and an air-cooled engine that depends on clean fins and correct valve lash. The intervals come from the factory schedule (in hours, page 214 of the manual, per Portal Hub owners), but most US owners simply change everything on purchase and then once a year. Fit new copper washers on every plug.
