Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume a 50-year-old truck's fluids are any age and its rubber is original until proven otherwise. Baseline everything, then deal with the frame, steering and brakes before you trust it on a trail. Factory capacities for 1969–72 weren't available from sources we could verify, so check the Chevrolet shop manual for those.
