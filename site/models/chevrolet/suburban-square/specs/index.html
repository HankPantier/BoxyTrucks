Fluids, capacities & service intervals

These trucks are 35 to 50 years old, so assume every fluid, hose and bushing is overdue. Identify what you actually have (transfer case, engine, half- or three-quarter-ton), because the service differs. We couldn't verify factory capacities, so confirm them in the GM shop manual.
