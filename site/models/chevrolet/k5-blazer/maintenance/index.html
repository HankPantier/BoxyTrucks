Maintenance schedule · 1973–1991

Chevrolet K5 Blazer (1973–1991) maintenance schedule

GM's maintenance schedules for the 1973–1991 K5 aren't published online by GM that we could find, and drivetrains changed every few years, so this page doesn't invent a schedule. Instead it sets out what to do at purchase on a truck that's 35 to 50 years old, then the few intervals specialists publish. Identify the engine, transmission, transfer case and axles first.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

Not a GM schedule. A purchase baseline plus attributed intervals; use the factory service manual for your year for capacities and specs.

WHENWHAT TO DO
At purchase
  • Engine oil and filter, air filter and fuel filter
  • Coolant, thermostat, hoses and belts; check the heater core
  • Automatic transmission pan drop, new filter and refill
  • Transfer case (NP208 and NP241 take ATF, not gear oil) and both differentials
  • Repack the front wheel bearings and check the hubs
  • Brake fluid flush; on 1988–91 trucks, check recall 93V119 (front rotors)
  • Inspect the frame at the steering box for cracks
Every oil change
  • Grease the driveshaft U-joints and slip yokes (lifted trucks wear them fast)
  • Check the steering box mounting and power steering fluid
6.2 diesel glow plugs
  • GM's interval is 100,000 mi; DieselHub recommends replacing all eight by 60,000 mi
After deep water
  • Change both differentials and repack the front wheel bearings

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Diesels are sensitive to dust, so check the airbox seals as well as the filter. Lifted trucks run steep driveshaft angles and need the joints greased far more often.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover
