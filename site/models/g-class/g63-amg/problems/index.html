Exercise your lockers

Engage the lockers every six months or so, on almost any boxy truck. Both of my locker problems, on the 80 Series and the G500, came from systems that weren’t used often enough.

G63 AMG (W463) problems to check before you buy

  1. Is it really a G 63?Many G 550s have been fitted with AMG bumpers, grilles and badges. US G 63 VINs from Bring a Trailer listings begin WDCYC7DF (2013–2016) and WDCYC7DH (2018). A same-era G 550 reads WDCYC3HF. Check the VIN and data card, not the bodywork.
  2. Recall paperworkMake sure NHTSA 17V250 (steering coupling, 2013–2016) has been done. On 2016–17 trucks, also check 17V713 (ESP with DISTRONIC PLUS). Run the VIN through NHTSA's recall lookup before you hand over money.
  3. Oil consumption and misfiresAsk how much oil it uses between services and scan for stored codes. The M157 can suffer cylinder scoring at higher mileage. Misfires are often plugs, coils or oil-soaked cam-magnet connectors, but persistent misfires or consumption justify a borescope before purchase.
  4. Coolant around the turbosLook for coolant crust or a sweet smell around the turbos and the lines that feed them, and check both coolant reservoirs. MBWorld owners report cracked turbo coolant lines, and a pressure test is cheap.
  5. Oil at the cam magnetsUnplug a camshaft-adjuster magnet connector. If there's oil inside, it is wicking into the harness, and the magnets need replacing with sacrificial pigtails fitted.
  6. Seals under the truckCheck for gear oil on the inside of the front wheels and on the big AMG calipers (front axle seals), at the transfer-case outputs and at the pinion seals. An MBWorld member says pinion seal leaks are 'very common around 60k–80k'.
  7. Test all three lockersEngage centre, rear and front in order on loose ground and watch the indicators. A truck that has only ever been driven on pavement can have lockers that don't engage.
  8. Rust and drainsCheck the tailgate's lower edge, door bottoms, sills, rear frame tubes and spring mounts. A G 63 rusts like any other G, and 20-inch wheels don't stop road salt.

Owner questions about G63 AMG (W463) reliability

Is the M157 reliable?

It's strong when maintained, but neglect gets expensive. The well-documented jobs are spark plugs and coils, oil-leaking cam magnets, turbo coolant lines, and turbo oil feed lines on older engines. Cylinder scoring is the serious risk at higher mileage. Buy on service history and a borescope inspection, not on mileage alone.
