PART 01

Overview

Where it came from, why it matters, and how it changed year to year.

The story

The G 63 AMG replaced the supercharged G 55 AMG in 2012 and reached American showrooms that August as a 2013 model. It ran until the 2018 model year, the last before the redesigned second-generation G. It uses AMG's 5.5-litre biturbo V8 and a seven-speed automatic, and it was the first AMG G with neither a supercharger nor a five-speed. The W463 underneath is unchanged: ladder frame, solid axles on coils, a two-speed transfer case and three locking differentials. AMG gave the truck a much bigger engine and left the off-road hardware alone.

TURBOS REPLACE THE SUPERCHARGER

Mercedes revealed the G 63 and the V12 G 65 in April 2012, ahead of the Beijing show. The M157 V8 displaces 5,461 cc and makes 544 PS with 760 Nm (560 lb-ft) from 2,000 rpm. Mercedes quoted 0–60 mph in 5.3 seconds and a 130 mph limiter, and claimed 13 per cent lower fuel consumption on the European cycle than the G 55. The engine drives an AMG SPEEDSHIFT PLUS 7G-TRONIC with three modes, automatic throttle blips on downshifts and ECO start/stop.

The rest of the update came from the 2012 G-Class refresh. The dashboard and centre console came from the ML, and the truck gained a colour TFT instrument cluster, a tablet-style COMAND screen, DISTRONIC PLUS, Blind Spot Assist and LED daytime running lights. AMG added a twin-blade grille, a bumper with three large intakes, six-piston front calipers from the ML 63 on 14.8-inch discs, and 20-inch wheels on 275/50 R20 tyres. AutoGuide priced a 2013 US truck at $134,300, about $20,000 more than a G 550.

WHY OFF-ROADERS SHOULD CARE

In the bodywork that matters off-road, a G 63 is the same truck as a G 550. Kelley Blue Book listed a 36-degree approach angle and a 7,716 lb tow rating. MotorTrend gave 27 degrees of departure, 21 degrees of breakover, about 8 inches of ground clearance and 24 inches of wading depth. It still has a low-range transfer case and centre, rear and front locks controlled by dashboard buttons. The off-road weak points are AMG's own choices: low-profile 20s, a side exhaust that exits ahead of the rear wheels, and huge front brakes that limit which wheels will fit.

Buyers liked it. MotorTrend reported in late 2013 that the G 63 had become the best-selling AMG product in the US. That's why so many are on the used market. Most owners drove them on pavement, so the lockers on a typical used truck have rarely been used.

2016 UPDATE AND THE END

For the 2016 model year the truck was renamed Mercedes-AMG G 63. Output rose by 27 PS to 571 PS (563 hp), with torque unchanged at 760 Nm and 0–100 km/h in 5.4 seconds. All G-Class models got new shock absorbers, revised ASR and ABS calibration, new bumpers and new instrument dials. AMG also offered an Edition 463 package with 21-inch wheels and a stainless underguard, and a colour package with bright paints such as alien green and sunset beam. The G 550 switched to the 4.0-litre twin-turbo M176 at the same time, and the 4×4² followed in 2015 on a shortened chassis from the G 63 AMG 6×6, AMG's six-wheeler derived from the Australian Army's W461-family truck.

The 2018 model year was the last for this body in the US. Its successor kept the G 63 name but moved to the twin-turbo 4.0-litre M177 and independent front suspension. That makes the 2013–2018 truck the last AMG G with a solid front axle. On Bring a Trailer it has also become the cheapest way into a biturbo AMG G: from 2025 to October 2026, the median sale was about $65,500.

SIDEBAR · 2013–15 OR 2016–18?

Pre- and post-facelift G 63s

The 2013–2015 trucks have 544 PS (Mercedes USA quoted 544 hp) and the original dampers. The 2016–2018 trucks have 571 PS (563 hp), new shocks, updated ESP and ABS calibration and revised gauges. Torque, the seven-speed and the three lockers are the same throughout. EPA ratings barely change: 12 mpg city and 14 highway every year, with 2018 rated 15 highway.

In 2025–26 Bring a Trailer sales, 2013–15 G 63s had a median of about $61,000 and 2016–18 trucks about $76,750. A buyer who plans to lift the truck and fit tyres gets little extra off-road from the newer trucks. The extra money mostly buys lower mileage, newer electronics and recall work that's more likely to have been done.

PART 02

Specs & capability

How it’s built, what it can do, and what fits under it.

How it’s built

Under the AMG trim, the G 63 is still a W463. The body sits on a separate ladder frame, both axles are solid and coil-sprung, a two-speed transfer case drives all four wheels permanently, and three dashboard switches lock the centre, rear and front differentials. AMG changed the engine, gearbox, brakes, exhaust, wheels and damper settings.

TWO COOLING CIRCUITS

The M157 has a main engine circuit and a separate low-temperature charge-air circuit. AMSOIL lists capacities of 14.6 qt and 3.1 qt. Both circuits rely on plastic fittings and hoses next to very hot turbos. FCP Euro sells a turbo coolant-line kit for this engine because those lines are a common failure, and a split line can empty the system quickly. Check both reservoirs before every trail run.

What to pay, what to check, and how to tell a good one from a clone.

What to check before you buy

  1. 01
    Is it really a G 63?Many G 550s have been fitted with AMG bumpers, grilles and badges. US G 63 VINs from Bring a Trailer listings begin WDCYC7DF (2013–2016) and WDCYC7DH (2018). A same-era G 550 reads WDCYC3HF. Check the VIN and data card, not the bodywork.
  2. 02
    Recall paperworkMake sure NHTSA 17V250 (steering coupling, 2013–2016) has been done. On 2016–17 trucks, also check 17V713 (ESP with DISTRONIC PLUS). Run the VIN through NHTSA's recall lookup before you hand over money.
  3. 03
    Oil consumption and misfiresAsk how much oil it uses between services and scan for stored codes. The M157 can suffer cylinder scoring at higher mileage. Misfires are often plugs, coils or oil-soaked cam-magnet connectors, but persistent misfires or consumption justify a borescope before purchase.
  4. 04
    Coolant around the turbosLook for coolant crust or a sweet smell around the turbos and the lines that feed them, and check both coolant reservoirs. MBWorld owners report cracked turbo coolant lines, and a pressure test is cheap.
  5. 05
    Oil at the cam magnetsUnplug a camshaft-adjuster magnet connector. If there's oil inside, it is wicking into the harness, and the magnets need replacing with sacrificial pigtails fitted.
  6. 06
    Seals under the truckCheck for gear oil on the inside of the front wheels and on the big AMG calipers (front axle seals), at the transfer-case outputs and at the pinion seals. An MBWorld member says pinion seal leaks are 'very common around 60k–80k'.
  7. 07
    Test all three lockersEngage centre, rear and front in order on loose ground and watch the indicators. A truck that has only ever been driven on pavement can have lockers that don't engage.
  8. 08
    Rust and drainsCheck the tailgate's lower edge, door bottoms, sills, rear frame tubes and spring mounts. A G 63 rusts like any other G, and 20-inch wheels don't stop road salt.

Spotting the real thing

Dressing up G 550s as AMGs is common, and so are aftermarket 'G 63 conversions'. The VIN and the engine tell you what left Graz.

  1. ID
    VIN characters 4–8US G 63s sold on Bring a Trailer read WDCYC7DF… (2013–2016 examples) and WDCYC7DH… (2018). A 2011 G 55 reads WDCYC7BF… and a same-era G 550 WDCYC3HF….
  2. ID
    Model year (10th character)D = 2013, E = 2014, F = 2015, G = 2016, H = 2017, J = 2018.
  3. ID
    EngineA G 63 has the M157 5.5-litre biturbo V8: two turbochargers and AMG engine covers, and no supercharger on top. A G 550 has either the naturally aspirated M273 (to 2015) or the 4.0-litre M176.
  4. ID
    Brakes and wheelsFactory G 63s have six-piston front calipers from the ML 63 on 14.8-inch discs, and 20-inch AMG wheels.
  5. ID
    Power by year2013–2015 trucks are 544 PS; 2016–2018 trucks, badged Mercedes-AMG G 63, are 571 PS (563 hp).

Camshaft-adjuster magnets leaking oil
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · 1–2 hoursSHOP COST · Parts plus labor
SYMPTOMSOil inside the four camshaft-adjuster magnet connectors at the front of the heads. Possible cam-timing codes. On neglected engines, oil wicks along the harness toward the engine computer.
THE FIXReplace all four magnets. Fit sacrificial wiring pigtails so any future leak stops at a cheap connector. Clean the harness plugs with electronic cleaner and check the far end of the harness for oil. FCP Euro's kit is built for this job on the M157, M278 and related engines.

G 63 AMG questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

About 32 inches, on 20-inch wheels. An Expedition Portal owner runs 275/55R20 (about 32 in) on an unlifted G 63, and another 2017 G 63 owner was told by his dealer the size 'will work fine'. Most unlifted W463s fit 33-inch 275/70R18s, but one Expedition Portal member says 18-inch wheels 'will not fit on the G63' over its big front calipers, so try a wheel on the hub before you buy a set. Recode the tyre size so the speedometer and ESP stay accurate.

SOURCE · EXPEDITION PORTAL
What size are the factory tires?

275/50R20, according to TireSize.com's fitment data for the 2015, 2016 and 2018 G 63. The launch press material lists 9.5 × 20 AMG wheels. The Edition 463 package offered 21-inch wheels.

SOURCE · TIRESIZE.COM · AUTOBLOG · CARSCOOPS
Does it have low range and lockers?

Yes. It is a full W463 with permanent four-wheel drive, a low-range transfer case and centre, rear and front locking differentials.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · KBB
How much can it tow?

7,716 lb, according to Kelley Blue Book's 2013 G 63 guide. Check your own truck's manual and hitch.

SOURCE · KBB
What fuel economy should I expect?

EPA: 12 mpg city, 14 highway, 13 combined on premium for every model year from 2013 to 2017, with 2018 rated 15 highway. Bigger tyres and a roof rack lower it.

SOURCE · FUELECONOMY.GOV
What oil does it take, and how much?

AMSOIL lists 9 qt with the filter, in 0W-40 or 5W-40 full synthetic meeting Mercedes approval. Euro Premium Parts cites MB 229.5/229.51 and recommends changing it every 10,000 miles or yearly. FCP Euro's G 63 oil kit comes with 9 litres.

SOURCE · AMSOIL · EURO PREMIUM PARTS · FCP EURO
Is the M157 reliable?

It's strong when maintained, but neglect gets expensive. The well-documented jobs are spark plugs and coils, oil-leaking cam magnets, turbo coolant lines, and turbo oil feed lines on older engines. Cylinder scoring is the serious risk at higher mileage. Buy on service history and a borescope inspection, not on mileage alone.

SOURCE · FCP EURO · EURO PREMIUM PARTS · AU7O
G 63 or G 550?

Off-road they are the same truck: same frame, axles, transfer case and lockers. The G 63 adds roughly 150 hp, bigger brakes that rule out 18-inch wheels, and a more complicated twin-turbo engine. If you'll mostly drive trails, a G 550 is cheaper to buy and fit with tyres. If you'll mostly drive pavement, the G 63's engine is the reason to buy it.

SOURCE · MBUSA · EXPEDITION PORTAL
Which years are best?

2016–2018 trucks have 563 hp, new dampers and recalibrated ESP and ABS, and they sell for roughly $15,000 more on Bring a Trailer. 2013–2015 trucks are the bargain. On either, make sure the steering-coupling recall (17V250, 2013–2016) is done.

SOURCE · CARSCOOPS · NHTSA · BRING A TRAILER
Can I use the diff locks on pavement?

No. Locked differentials stop the wheels turning at different speeds, so on grippy surfaces the driveline binds and the tyres scrub. Lock them on loose ground at low speed and unlock before you're back on tarmac.

SOURCE · BOXYTRUCKS
What replaced it?

The second-generation G 63 for the 2019 model year. It has a 4.0-litre twin-turbo M177 with 577 hp, a 40:60 torque split and independent front suspension, and it still has three lockers.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · MBUSA
