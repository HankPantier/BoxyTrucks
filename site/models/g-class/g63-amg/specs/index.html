Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Baseline everything the week you buy it. On top of the usual W463 knuckle, axle and diff-lock care, the G 63 has a twin-turbo direct-injection V8 with two coolant circuits and a seven-speed whose fluid is easy to neglect. Assume nothing was done unless there's a receipt.
