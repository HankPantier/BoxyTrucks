Fluids, capacities & service intervals

A G 550 that’s changed hands has usually been serviced for the showroom, not the trail — assume nothing below the engine has been touched and baseline every fluid. Split the list by engine first: 2009–2015 trucks have the 5.5-litre M273, 2016–2018 trucks the 4.0 twin-turbo M176, and many parts differ.
