Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Baseline everything, because a W463 that 'never needed anything' usually has locker vacuum leaks, old ATF and a sagging headliner. The specs below are the published ones for the common 1999–2008 M113/722.6 trucks (Toolsource). Diesels, AMGs and 2007-on seven-speed trucks differ, so confirm capacities and approvals in WIS or the owner's manual for your VIN.
