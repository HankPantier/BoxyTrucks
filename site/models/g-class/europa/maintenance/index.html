Maintenance schedule · 1990s–2001

Mercedes-Benz Europa International Gs maintenance schedule

The Europa International trucks were imported outside Mercedes-Benz USA, and we couldn't find a factory maintenance schedule for them online. This page is a purchase baseline with the W463 diff and engine-oil intervals PistonHeads gives, plus the M104 inline-six jobs PeachParts warns about. Later V8 Europas (M113) share their engine with the US G 500, whose page has the Service A/B detail.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

No factory schedule online. Baseline, plus PistonHeads' W463 intervals and PeachParts' M104 notes.

WHENWHAT TO DO
At purchase (unknown history)
  • Engine oil and filter, air and fuel filters, spark plugs (and coil connectors on the M104)
  • Coolant, thermostat and hoses; drive belt and tensioner
  • Automatic transmission fluid and filter
  • Transfer case, both differentials and the front knuckles
  • Brake fluid
  • M104 G 320 (1994–95): inspect the engine wiring harness insulation
  • Test all three diff locks
As often as every 6,000 mi
  • Engine oil and filter (PistonHeads' 'extra care' interval for W463s)
Every 40,000 mi
  • Front knuckles, swivels and wheel bearings (the Europa interval in our buyer's baseline)
Every 40,000 km (≈25,000 mi) off-road / 60,000 km (≈37,000 mi) on-road
  • Front and rear differential oil (PistonHeads)
Every 2 years
  • Brake fluid (common Mercedes practice; check your booklet)

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Use PistonHeads' 40,000 km diff interval for off-road use, and check the diffs, transfer case and knuckles for water after crossings.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover

ITEMWHENWHY
M104 engine wiring harness (1993–95)At purchase and before any intake workPeachParts says the insulation flakes off and causes shorts and check-engine faults.
M104 head gasketLook for oil or coolant at the head at every serviceOne 1995 G 320's gasket failed at about 135,000 miles (500E Board).
Serpentine belt tensioner (M104 through 1995)When the belt is changedPeachParts says it's pricey and often fails on reassembly.
Battery drain and electricsIf the battery goes flat while parkedPistonHeads lists battery drain as a known issue on pre-2004 W463s.
Coil springsEvery servicePistonHeads says W463 springs break, especially at the rear.
Diff locksExercise them monthlyPistonHeads warns rarely used diffs can seize.
