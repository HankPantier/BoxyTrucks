Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Start by confirming exactly what you bought: a Europa truck can have the M103, M104 or M112 six or the M113 V8, a 4- or 5-speed automatic, and drivetrain fluids that changed through the 1990s. Then reset everything — these are trucks more than 25 years old, and service records rarely made the trip.
