Best Mercedes-Benz G-Class years to buy, and which to avoid

G-Class W463

1990–2018

In the US, a 2005–08 G 500. Handsome Motors says the 2002–04 trucks suffer electrical gremlins (seat motors, window regulators, door-lock actuators, transfer-case actuators) that were mostly gone by 2005–06, and Expedition Portal owners tell the same story about 2002–03. CarGurus adds that the very last 1999–2000 Europa G 500s are considered very reliable, and that 2009-on trucks have fewer problems than the early US ones. The 2016–18 twin-turbo trucks are a reliable option, Handsome Motors says, but not the most reliable. In Europe, Autocar's pick is the G 320 CDI.

G 500

1998–2008

2005–08. The 2002–04 trucks are the ones with the electrical gremlins. Handsome Motors, a G specialist dealer, lists seat motors and controls, window regulators and motors, door-lock actuators and transfer-case actuators, and says the faults got better every year until they were mostly gone by 2005–06. Expedition Portal owners say the same about 2002–03. One owner notes that Mercedes changed some electronics partway through 2003. CarGurus is harsher: it says 2002–05 trucks draw the most complaints, mostly electrical, and that the 2009-on G 550 is better still. Within 2005–08 you're really choosing a gearbox. 2005–06 trucks have the five-speed 722.6 with a dipstick, which one Expedition Portal regular prefers because it's easier to service. 2007–08 trucks have the seven-speed 7G-TRONIC, plus HID lamps and a reversing camera (see the question above for how to tell them apart). Some owners would rather have a pre-2002 Europa G 500 with its manual transfer-case lever and fewer electronics, but parts and paperwork are harder. Whatever the year, test every window, lock and seat before you hand over money.

  1. 2002–04Early US trucks have the electrical gremlins: Handsome Motors lists seat motors and controls, window regulators and motors, door-lock actuators and transfer-case actuators. Expedition Portal owners say the same about 2002–03Beware
  2. 2005–08The sweet spot. Handsome Motors says the early electrical faults got better every year and were mostly gone by 2005–06. 2005–06 trucks keep the simpler five-speed 722.6; 2007–08 trucks get the seven-speed 7G-TRONIC, HID lamps and a reversing cameraSweet spot

G 55 AMG

1999–2012

In the US, 2002–2004 G 55s are naturally aspirated (349 hp) and 2005–2011 are Kompressors (469 hp, later 493 and 500 hp). In Europe the supercharger arrived in 2004.

G 63 AMG

2012–2018 (US 2013–2018)

There's no consensus best year, so buy on condition. 2016–2018 trucks have 563 hp, new dampers and recalibrated ESP and ABS, and they sell for roughly $15,000 more on Bring a Trailer. 2013–2015 trucks are the bargain. Handsome Motors says the M157 in the G 63 got timing-chain and head-bolt updates, and wants the cooling system and water pump inspected on any of them. Whichever year you buy, make sure the steering-coupling recall (17V250, 2013–2016) is done.
