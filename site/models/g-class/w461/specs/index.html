Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Ex-fleet and ex-military W461s are often maintained by the book but stored for years, so refresh every fluid and rubber part. Military trucks run 24 V, and glow plugs, bulbs and blower motors must match the system. Specs vary by engine, from the 290 GD to the OM642 CDIs, so confirm capacities and fluid approvals for your VIN.
