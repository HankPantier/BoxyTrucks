Mercedes-Benz G 500 maintenance schedule

Mercedes runs the W463 on its Flexible Service System: the dash indicator calls for an alternating Service A and Service B, typically about every 10,000 miles or once a year. Service A is an oil service; Service B adds the cabin and air filters and brake fluid. The longer-interval items (transmission, transfer case, diffs, plugs) aren't in the A/B lists, so owners and specialists set them, as below.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

Service A/B contents from KBB's G-Class maintenance schedule; transfer case, transmission and knuckle intervals as quoted in our buyer's baseline (owners citing Mercedes, technician practice and Europa's own interval). Check the maintenance booklet for your VIN.

WHENWHAT TO DO
Service A (about every 10,000 mi or yearly, at the indicator)
  • Synthetic engine oil (MB-approved) and filter
  • Check and correct fluid levels
  • Multi-point inspection
Service B (alternates with A)
  • Engine oil and filter
  • Cabin filter and engine air filter
  • Brake fluid
  • Check fluid levels and inspect
Every 40,000 mi
  • Front knuckles, wheel bearings and CV boots (Europa's own interval)
Every 40,000–60,000 mi
  • 722.6 automatic transmission fluid and filter (common technician practice)
Every 60,000 mi
  • Transfer case and both differentials (Mercedes interval quoted by owners)
Every 2 years
  • Brake fluid, if Service B hasn't come round

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

On dirt roads, change the air filter far more often than every second service. After water crossings, check the diff, transfer case and knuckle oil for water and check the sunroof drains are clear.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover

ITEMWHENWHY
Oil filter housing gasket and valve cover gaskets (M113)Look for oil on top of the engine at every serviceThe M113's best-known oil leaks.
Harmonic balancerInspect at every serviceThe rubber layer separates with age.
Diff lock lines and actuatorsTest all three locks yearlyVacuum and pneumatic lines age; locks that won't engage are common.
Transmission connector sealLook for ATF on the side of the transmissionIt degrades and leaks (EuroAutoPro).
Coil springsListen for thumping after cold weatherAutocar says they crack.
Sunroof drainsYearlyBlocked drains sag the headliner and soak the interior.
