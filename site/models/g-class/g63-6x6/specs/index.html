Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Treat a 6×6 as a G 63 engine and gearbox on top of a fleet of differentials: three axles, six portal hubs, a transfer case with a through-drive, plus a compressor and air system. Mercedes and specialist service records matter more here than on any other G. Where a capacity isn't published, have a G specialist confirm it rather than guessing.
