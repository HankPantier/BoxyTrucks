Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Treat a 500 GE as a recommissioning, not an oil change: most surviving examples have low miles and long storage, so rubber, fuel and fluids are old even when the odometer isn't. Parts for the M117 V8 and the 1993-era W463 chassis are model-specific — order by VIN and engine number, and keep the old parts for originality.
