Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing was done. Classic & Sports Car tells buyers to ask about oil changes for the engine, gearbox, transfer box and diffs, and most W460s are now 35 to 45 years old. Change every fluid, fix the cooling system and get the locker hydraulics working before the first trail trip. Factory capacities vary by engine and gearbox, so check the manual for your truck's numbers.
