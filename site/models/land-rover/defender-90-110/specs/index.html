Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing has been done. Most of these trucks are 25 to 40 years old and have usually had several owners. Change every fluid, re-grease the swivels and propshafts, and confirm the timing-belt history on a Tdi. Specs differ between the US V8 (NAS) trucks and the 200/300Tdi, Td5 and Puma diesels, so identify the engine and gearbox first. Fill the axles, transfer box and swivels to their level plugs on level ground rather than trusting a book capacity.
