Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Every Discovery 1 is now 25-plus years old and many have had years of cheap ownership, so baseline everything, starting with the timing belt on a 300Tdi and the fluids in a transfer box and axles that may never have been changed. Do it with the truck on stands so you can inspect the rust and bushes at the same time.
