Fluids, capacities & service intervals

A Series truck has no service computer and no idea when it was last looked after, so assume nothing: change every oil, grease every nipple and adjust every brake before you trust it. The Series Land Rover owner's 'green bible' (workshop manual) lists the full service schedule; the items below are what owners on LandyZone and Rovers North treat as the catch-up.
