PART 01

Overview

Where it came from, why it matters, and how it changed year to year.

The story

The Discovery 4, sold as the LR4 in North America and the Middle East, is a thorough rework of the Discovery 3 rather than a new truck. It keeps the stepped roof, the 113.6-inch wheelbase and the Integrated Body Frame, a monocoque body bolted to a ladder chassis, and adds a new face, a nicer cabin and much better engines. It is the last Discovery built that way. That makes it a seven-seat expedition truck with independent air suspension, a two-speed transfer box and Terrain Response, all for less than a used Land Cruiser. The catch is that it is still a Land Rover, and the bills arrive in plastic.

A FACELIFT THAT WENT DEEP

Land Rover unveiled the Discovery 4 in the summer of 2009 and put it on sale in Britain on 1 September that year; in the US it arrived as the 2010 LR4. The nose, lamps and grille were smoothed to match the 2010 Range Rover and Range Rover Sport, and the dash was redone with a TFT driver's display and simpler switchgear. Most of the money went underneath, though: Range Rover Sport brakes, thicker anti-roll bars, a better ZF six-speed automatic, and Terrain Response retuned with a Sand launch mode and a Rock Crawl mode that brakes gently at walking pace.

The cross-linked air suspension and the electronically locking centre differential carried over from the LR3 (Discovery 3), and a locking rear differential stayed on the options list. Under the body is the same 'Integrated Body Frame' Land Rover introduced in 2004: build the passenger cell and engine bay as a monocoque, then mate it to a ladder frame that carries the gearbox and suspension. It's heavy, at about 5,600 lb for a US V8, but it's strong.

V8, THEN A SUPERCHARGED SIX

In America the LR4 launched with Jaguar Land Rover's new direct-injection 5.0-litre V8, rated at 375 hp and 375 lb-ft, from the same AJ133 family that went into the 2010 Range Rover. Europe mostly got diesels: a 3.0-litre twin-turbo V6 with 600 Nm, sold from the 2011 model year as the SDV6 alongside a single-turbo TDV6, plus a basic 2.7 TDV6 that was dropped for 2011. Diesel Discoveries in Europe also got the ZF eight-speed automatic for 2012, two years before America did.

The 2014 model year changed the US truck more than any facelift did. The V8 gave way to a supercharged 3.0-litre V6 with 340 hp and 332 lb-ft, the six-speed became a ZF eight-speed with stop-start, and a lighter single-speed transfer case became standard. If you wanted low range and the active locking rear differential, you now had to tick the $1,350 Heavy Duty package, which also added a full-size spare. EPA combined economy went from 14 mpg for the V8 to 16 mpg for the V6.

WHAT IT'S LIKE TO OWN ONE

Owners on LandRoverWorld and Expedition Portal tend to tell the same story. The truck is superb off-road and on a long drive, and it will tow 7,716 lb. It also eats brakes, and its cooling system is full of plastic crossover pipes and coolant manifolds that crack between roughly 50,000 and 100,000 miles. Early 5.0 V8s added a timing-chain tensioner problem, covered by Land Rover bulletin LTB00474 for 2010–2012 trucks in a published VIN range. The air suspension, meanwhile, will eventually want a compressor, a strut or a valve block.

None of that makes it a bad buy. It makes it a truck to buy with records, a scan tool and a plan. An Expedition Portal owner put it bluntly: a $14,000 LR4 can need $5,000–8,000 of deferred maintenance in the first year, and the purchase price is only the price of admission. Sort the cooling system and the chains, though, and the LR4 is far more capable on a trail than most luxury SUVs. When Car and Driver lined it up against the Lexus GX 460 and three road-biased rivals in 2011, it finished fourth of five, which says more about the test than the truck.

THE END OF THE LADDER

In March 2012 the one millionth Discovery came off the Solihull line, a Discovery 4, and Land Rover drove it and two sister trucks from Solihull to the Beijing Motor Show, recreating a 1950s overland expedition. That car is now on permanent display at the British Motor Museum in Gaydon. Discovery 4 production ran until 2016; owners report that the US line-up ended with Landmark and HSE Silver trims in the 2016.5 model year. Its successor, the 2017 Discovery, switched to a lighter unitary body and dropped the numeric suffix. That leaves the LR4 as the last Discovery with a separate frame under it, closing a line that began with the 1989 original.

SIDEBAR · JOURNEY OF DISCOVERY

The millionth Discovery takes the long way to Beijing

To mark the millionth Discovery in 2012, Land Rover recreated a 1950s Solihull-to-the-Far-East expedition with Discovery 4s. The convoy crossed Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan into western China, running the desert roads from Kashgar to Korla, the stretch shown at the top of this page. The trip was raising money towards a £1 million target for the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies.

Along the way G459 WAC, one of the pre-production Discovery 1s later converted into an amphibious vehicle, joined the celebrations at Lake Geneva. The millionth car ended the trip at the Beijing Motor Show and then went on permanent display at the British Motor Museum in Gaydon.

PART 02

Specs & capability

How it’s built, what it can do, and what fits under it.

How it’s built

Underneath the LR4 is the Discovery 3's Integrated Body Frame: a monocoque passenger cell and engine bay mounted to a separate ladder frame that carries the drivetrain and suspension. All four wheels are independently sprung on air struts that the computer can cross-link, so when one wheel drops, its partner on the same axle rises, as on a beam axle. Ride height runs from access height to an off-road setting with 9.4 in (240 mm) of ground clearance. Terrain Response sets throttle, gearbox, differentials, traction control and ride height for the selected surface.

What to check before you buy

  1. 01
    Coolant smell, coolant top-ups, crossover historyThe plastic coolant crossover pipes and manifolds are the LR4's best-known weak point on both the 5.0 V8 and the 3.0 SC V6. Owners report failures from about 50,000 miles and treat 60,000–90,000 miles as replacement time. A sweet coolant smell after a drive, a falling expansion tank or a 'low coolant' message means it's already started. Ask for invoices showing genuine crossover pipes, the water pump and the thermostat.
  2. 02
    5.0 V8: cold-start rattleLand Rover bulletin LTB00474 covers a rattle or click from the front of the engine on 2010–2012 MY LR4s in VIN range AA513326–CA652214, caused by worn timing-chain tensioner levers. The fix is two tensioners (LR051008) and two levers (LR051013); the revised levers have a steel insert, and old and new parts must not be mixed. Listen to a stone-cold start, and get proof of the work if the VIN is in range. A ticking roughly once a second is more likely the fan clutch.
  3. 03
    Air suspension: the overnight testRaise the truck to off-road height, then check that it stays level overnight and that the compressor doesn't run constantly. A truck that sags after a few days has a leak at a strut, valve block or Voss fitting. Owners trace leaks with soapy water. If the compressor has been replaced, Arnott notes that the LR3/LR4 unit needs a software update and a new relay.
  4. 04
    Which drivetrain do you have?Every 2010–2013 US LR4 has a two-speed transfer box. From 2014 the base truck is single-speed with a Torsen centre differential, and low range plus the active rear locker came only with the $1,350 Heavy Duty package, which also brought a full-size spare. Owners check the 4x4 information screen (a single centre-lock icon means no HD package) and the spare, and look under the rear axle for the locker's actuator motor.
  5. 05
    3.0 SC V6: supercharger rattleA rattle like gravel in a can from the top of the engine at idle is usually the supercharger's spring-type coupler isolator wearing out. A supercharger rebuilder quoted on LandRoverWorld says it starts going at around 50,000 miles. It isn't urgent, but the supercharger has to be partly dismantled or removed, so it pairs naturally with the rear coolant manifold job.
  6. 06
    Front lower control arms and brakesAt 5,600-plus lb, the LR4 is hard on lower control-arm bushings, ball joints and brakes. Clunks over bumps, steering wander and uneven tyre wear point at the arms. Owners say brakes wear quickly and cost a lot to replace, and early trucks had a TSB for rear brake squeal when reversing.
  7. 07
    RecallsRun the VIN through NHTSA. Every 2010–2016 LR4 is in campaign 21V635000 for a fuel-pump flange that can crack and leak. Some 2012–2013 trucks built in August–September 2012 are in 15V386000 for panoramic-roof adhesive, and some 2015s are in 15V214000 for ABS software that can disable stability control and Hill Descent Control.
  8. 08
    Oil changes and recordsThe factory oil interval was 15,000 miles. Owners on LandRoverWorld and Expedition Portal recommend 5,000–7,500 miles, and some link the 5.0 chain-guide wear to long intervals. A truck with dealer stamps at 15,000 miles and no chain or cooling work is a project priced as a driver.

Spotting the real thing

US LR4s are all petrol, but drivetrain content varies a lot by year and option, and the expensive engine fixes are VIN-specific. Know exactly what you're looking at.

  1. ID
    Model year from the VINLand Rover quotes VIN ranges by their last eight characters, which begin with the model-year letter: A = 2010, B = 2011, C = 2012, D = 2013, E = 2014, F = 2015, G = 2016. That's the 10th VIN character under US federal rules. Use it to check a 5.0 V8 against bulletin LTB00474's range AA513326–CA652214.
  2. ID
    Engine2010–2013 US trucks have the 5.0 V8; 2014–2016 have the 3.0 supercharged V6. A diesel Discovery 4 in the US is not a factory US vehicle.
  3. ID
    Low range and rear lockerAll 2010–2013 US trucks have the two-speed transfer box. On 2014–16 trucks, low range and the active rear locker came only with the Heavy Duty package. Owners check the 4x4 information screen (a single centre-lock icon means no HD package), the spare (HD added a full-size one) and the rear diff for the locker's actuator motor. Owners don't agree on whether every 2010–2013 truck has the rear locker, so check physically or with a VIN decode.
  4. ID
    Late trimsFor 2016.5, owners report that the HSE Silver and HSE Landmark replaced the HSE and HSE Lux; Landmark trucks have Landmark badging, bright silver roof rails and 20-inch split-spoke wheels.
  5. ID
    Armoured SentinelLand Rover's factory-armoured Discovery 4 Sentinel (B6 protection) is described as almost indistinguishable from a standard truck, so a heavy, oddly specified D4 deserves a closer look.

Importing & legality

Land Rover sold only petrol LR4s in the US, so the TDV6 and SDV6 Discovery 4 diesels are gray-market. NHTSA's 25-year exemption is counted from the date of manufacture, and the first Discovery 4s were built in 2009, so the earliest diesels won't become eligible until 2034. Until then, a US-registered diesel D4 should be treated with suspicion.

Coolant crossover pipe, water pump & thermostat (5.0 V8) →
DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · ≈ 1 day DIY (pump and both pipes)SHOP COST · One owner's dealer bill for pump, front and rear manifolds, hoses and belts on a 2015: $5,500
SYMPTOMSCoolant smell after driving, slowly falling coolant level or 'low coolant' warning, crusty coolant around the front of the engine or the back of the block, rising temperature under load.
THE FIXDrain the coolant. Remove the engine covers and air intake to reach the front crossover (water pump outlet to block, LR018275) and the rear heater manifold. Pull the pipes and make sure no broken pipe ends or O-rings stay behind in the block. Clean the ports, fit new O-rings lubricated with coolant (not grease), and seat the new pipes squarely. Owners warn against cheap copies and fit genuine or aluminium parts. Replace the water pump and thermostat while you're in there, then refill with the correct 50/50 coolant and bleed the system.

Asked on every forum

Land Rover LR4 / Discovery 4 questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

US trucks came on 255/50R19 or 255/50R20. On the 20s, LandRoverWorld owners say 265/50R20 fits at standard height without rubbing and is the largest size that still fits the spare well when fully inflated. A 285/50R20 rubs slightly at full lock and needs the spare deflated to fit underneath. Owners who switch to aftermarket 18-inch wheels run 265/65R18 with no rubbing at standard height; 285/60R18 brings only mild liner rub at full compression or lock.

SOURCE · LANDROVERWORLD
5.0 V8 or 3.0 supercharged V6?

The 2010–2013 V8 makes 375 hp and 375 lb-ft and has a two-speed transfer box as standard, but 2010–2012 engines in the bulletin's VIN range can need timing-chain tensioners. The 2014–2016 V6 makes 340 hp and 332 lb-ft, gets the eight-speed and a better EPA rating (16 vs 14 mpg combined), but has its own supercharger-isolator and cooling-pipe issues, and needs the HD package for low range.

SOURCE · MOTORAUTHORITY · EPA · LTB00474
Which years are the best to buy?

There's no clean answer. The 2010–13 V8s are the ones with standard low range, but treat every one as a timing-chain risk until you see the tensioner and lever invoice; the class-action settlement covered 2012–2014 5.0 trucks. The 2014–16 supercharged V6 avoids that problem and ReRev rates those years best, but low range and the rear locker came only with the Heavy Duty package, and the plastic cooling parts fail on both engines. Buy the records, then the year.

SOURCE · LTB00474 · OPENCLASSACTIONS · REREV
Does my LR4 have low range and a rear locker?

All 2010–2013 US LR4s have the two-speed transfer box. Owners disagree on whether every 2010–2013 truck also got the rear locker, so check physically or with a VIN decode. For 2014–2016, low range and the active rear locker came only with the Heavy Duty package ($1,350), which also included a full-size spare.

SOURCE · WTOP · LANDROVERWORLD
Is the 5.0 timing-chain problem a deal-breaker?

No, if it's been done or priced in. Bulletin LTB00474 covers 2010–2012 LR4s in VIN range AA513326–CA652214, and the fix is new tensioners and steel-insert levers (8.1 hours of labour by Land Rover's own timing). Don't assume a 2013 is immune: a parts-catalogue lookup posted on LandRoverWorld shows the revised tensioners, levers and guides only from VIN DA696315, partway through 2013, and a US class action settled in 2021 covered 2012–2014 LR4s and Range Rover Sports with the 5.0. Many owners stress shorter oil-change intervals.

SOURCE · LTB00474 · LANDROVERWORLD · OPENCLASSACTIONS
How often should I replace the coolant crossover pipes?

There's no factory interval, but owners treat them as a maintenance item: failures are reported from about 50,000 miles, replacement every 60,000 is considered cautious, and past 100,000 miles original pipes are 'borrowed time'. The SC V6 can overheat quickly once coolant is lost, so don't ignore a coolant smell.

SOURCE · LANDROVERWORLD · EXPEDITION PORTAL
What oil does it take, and how much?

The 5.0 V8 takes SAE 5W-20 meeting WSS-M2C925-A, about 8.0 litres (17 US pints) with the filter. Land Rover's 2014 service bulletin also specifies 5W-20 to WSS-M2C925-A for the SC V6, and owners say 2015–16 handbooks call for 0W-20 to STJLR.51.5122. The oil level is shown electronically rather than on a conventional dipstick, and most owners extract the old oil from the top and change the cartridge filter on top of the engine.

SOURCE · LR OWNER GUIDE · TSB SGI14-07
How much can it tow?

The advertised maximum is 7,716 lb (3,500 kg) braked, and the gross train weight is 14,859 lb. One LandRoverWorld owner tows a 6,600-lb boat and trailer and came away impressed. The Discovery 4 also added Trailer Stability Assist.

SOURCE · JALOPNIK · LR OWNER GUIDE
What fuel economy should I expect?

EPA ratings: 12/17 mpg city/highway (14 combined) for the 2010 V8, 13/17 for 2013, and 15/19 (16 combined) for the 2014–2016 SC V6, all on premium. An Expedition Portal V8 owner averages about 14 mpg. The petrol tank holds 86 litres (23 US gal).

SOURCE · FUELECONOMY.GOV
Can I lift it and keep the air suspension?

Yes. Lift rods that replace the height-sensor links, such as Proud Rhino's PR25, raise it about 2.5 in with no reprogramming. One LandRoverWorld owner found that strut spacers with 18-inch wheels drove far better than lift rods with wheel spacers. If you're done with air altogether, Terrafirma sells a +2 in air-to-coil conversion.

SOURCE · LUCKY8 · LANDROVERWORLD
Which scan tool do owners use?

The GAP IIDTool is the common choice. Owners use it to reset ride height and to match the EAS software to a replacement Hitachi or AMK compressor. A generic OBD-II reader can't do those jobs.

SOURCE · LANDROVERWORLD · GAP
How deep can it wade?

Land Rover quotes 700 mm (28 in) at off-road height and 600 mm (24 in) at standard height.

SOURCE · LR OWNER GUIDE
Was the diesel sold in the US?

No. US LR4s were petrol-only: the 5.0 V8 for 2010–2013 and the 3.0 SC V6 for 2014–2016. The TDV6 and SDV6 diesels were sold elsewhere.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA
