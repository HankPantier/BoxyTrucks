Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Most LR4s are now past 100,000 miles on a 15,000-mile factory oil interval, and the handbook calls the gearbox 'filled for life'. Baseline everything, and treat the cooling-system plastics and the air suspension as part of the service, not as optional extras. Fluid specs below are from Land Rover's Discovery 4 owner guides; check your own handbook for 2014–16 SC V6 figures.
