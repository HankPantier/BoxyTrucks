Land Rover LR3 / Discovery 3 maintenance schedule

Land Rover's North American LR3 schedule alternates two oil-and-filter services every 7,500 miles or 6 months. Every service includes a pollen filter, an air cleaner element, a full underside inspection and a check of the fault lamps with the dealer tool. The long-life fluids are where LR3s get neglected: the transmission, coolant and axle oils are on a 10-year clock, and most trucks have passed it.

Service schedule

From Land Rover's 2006–2009 LR3 service schedule. The 2005 schedule is similar; check your book.

WHENWHAT TO DO
Every 7,500 mi / 6 months
  • Engine oil and filter
  • Replace the pollen filter and air cleaner element
  • Rotate wheels; inspect brakes, tires, suspension, steering, exhaust and hoses
  • Check the tie rod torque (76 N·m / 56 lb-ft)
  • Inspect the serpentine belt; check fault lamps and road test
Every 3 years
  • Brake fluid
Every 6 years
  • Flexible brake hoses
75,000 mi and 150,000 mi
  • Transfer case oil
  • Locking rear differential oil (if fitted)
90,000 mi (4.0 V6) / 105,000 mi (4.4 V8)
  • Spark plugs
105,000 mi (V6) / 150,000 mi (V8)
  • Serpentine belt
Every 10 years
  • Engine coolant
  • Automatic transmission fluid and filter
  • Front and rear axle oil

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Land Rover's arduous-use rule halves the oil interval to 3,750 miles. Replace the air cleaner more often in dust or off-road. For the ZF 6HP26, the level can only be set at 30–35 °C fluid temperature (ZF).

Age items the schedule doesn't cover

ITEMWHENWHY
ZF 6HP26 fluid and panNow if the truck is past 10 years with no recordThe factory 10-year interval has passed on every LR3; ZF sells the pan, filter and fluid as a kit.
Engine coolantNow if there's no recordAlso on a 10-year interval in Land Rover's schedule.
Front and rear axle oilNow if there's no recordAlso a 10-year item; sooner after deep water.
Brake hosesEvery 6 yearsLand Rover schedules flexible brake hose replacement on age, not mileage.
