Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Most LR3s have had cheap ownership for years, and on this truck deferred maintenance shows up as warning lights. Baseline the fluids in the ZF gearbox, transfer box and diffs that many owners never touch, do the TDV6 timing belt unless there's proof, and check the air suspension and parking brake with a diagnostic tool while the truck is on stands.
