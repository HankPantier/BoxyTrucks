Fluids, capacities & service intervals

The LX 600 is still young enough that a baseline is mostly about proving what's been done. Check the engine recall status first, then do the early gear-oil change owners recommend, and service the AHC on trucks that have it. Specs come from the LX 600 owner's manual (OM60V34U), and intervals from the 2023 Lexus maintenance schedule.
