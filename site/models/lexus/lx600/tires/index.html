Lexus LX 600 biggest tire size: stock and lifted

The biggest tire owners run on a Lexus LX 600 at every lift height, with the trimming it takes, the wheel specs you need and the lift kits that get you there.

Read the full Lexus LX 600 guideCommon problemsYears to avoidSpecs & fluids
The short answer

The factory size on 22s is 265/50R22, about 32 in. Owners on aftermarket 20s run 275/60R20 (about 33 in) and 285/55R20 without rubbing, and 275/70R18 fits on Toyota TRD 18-inch wheels. 285/50R22 on factory wheels needed cutting and spacers. Without a lift, plan on about 33 in.
