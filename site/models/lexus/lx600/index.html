Lexus LX 600

“The only 300 Series Land Cruiser America could buy, sold with 22-inch wheels and an apology for the missing V8. Brilliant chassis, so check the engine's paperwork.”

PART 01

Overview

Where it came from, why it matters, and how it changed year to year.

The story

When Toyota decided the 300 Series Land Cruiser wouldn't be sold in North America, the LX 600 became the only way to buy one here. Unveiled on October 13, 2021, it moved to the new GA-F ladder frame, lost 441 lb compared with the LX 570 and swapped the old 5.7-liter V8 for a 409-hp, 479 lb-ft 3.4-liter twin-turbo V6 and a 10-speed automatic. The trim is fancier than any Land Cruiser buyer asked for, but the low range, locking center diff and solid rear axle are pure Land Cruiser.

THE LAND CRUISER AMERICA COULDN'T BUY

The LX has always been a Land Cruiser in a better suit, from the 80 Series-based LX 450 through the 200 Series-based LX 570. Lexus says the LX has been sold in more than 50 countries and regions, with over 500,000 sold by August 2021. Its 112-in wheelbase dates back to the first LX of 1995. When the 300 Series arrived without a US version, one launch report summed up the mood: 'Many were disappointed to learn that the 300-Series Toyota Land Cruiser wouldn't come to the U.S.'

The LX 600 filled that hole with five grades at launch: Standard, Premium, Luxury, F Sport and Ultra Luxury. The Ultra Luxury is a four-seat limousine with 48-degree reclining rear seats. The F Sport adds 22-inch forged wheels, a rear stabilizer and a Torsen limited-slip rear differential. None of the US grades got front or rear lockers. Those stayed in Japan, which got an LX 'Offroad' grade with all three differentials locking.

WHAT THE NEW CHASSIS BROUGHT

Lexus describes the GA-F frame as 20 percent more rigid. The front uses high-mounted double wishbones, and the rear keeps a four-link axle with lateral control arms. Suspension travel is 100 mm of bound and 145 mm of rebound, and rear rebound alone grew by 20 mm. Active Height Control offers Low, Normal, Hi1 and Hi2 heights, and adaptive dampers use linear solenoid valves. At normal height, the approach, breakover and departure angles are 25°, 23° and 20°.

The off-road electronics got smarter too. Multi-Terrain Select has six modes, including a first-for-Lexus Auto setting that reads the terrain from sensor data. Crawl Control offers five speeds, and the Multi-Terrain Monitor adds four cameras and an underfloor view. Lexus rates it to climb 45 degrees and wade 700 mm (27.5 in). Not everyone wants the gadgets, though. Slee Off-Road's 'Marshmello' build was a Premium LX 600 custom-ordered without AHC so it could run Dobinsons suspension.

THE ENGINE QUESTION

The V35A-FTS is where the LX 600 story gets complicated. In 2024, Toyota recalled 102,092 Tundras and LX 600s, 3,524 of them LXs built from July 30, 2021 to November 25, 2022, because machining debris could damage the main bearings. The remedy was a new engine. In November 2025, recall 25V-767 added another 9,895 US-market LXs built from February 26, 2022 to April 2, 2024. That count includes 10 trucks that had received affected service engines. At first Lexus told dealers the remedy was still being developed. NHTSA's record now says dealers inspect the No. 1 main bearing using inspection software and drive data and replace the engine if necessary, with owner letters mailed August 28, 2026.

The recall report notes that some later engines use improved manufacturing processes or an improved main bearing. On IH8MUD, owners argue about it constantly. Some see the recall as an open-ended engine warranty, while others are waiting for Toyota to name a root cause. Owners also report a clunk into first gear on rolling stops and laggy throttle tied to the pre-collision system. Neither is dangerous, but both are worth knowing about before you test-drive one.

WHERE IT STANDS

The LX 600 has sold steadily for something this expensive, with 3,642 US sales in 2022, 6,959 in 2023, 6,830 in 2024 and 7,464 in 2025, when 2,883 of those were the new LX 700h hybrid. That hybrid makes 457 hp and 583 lb-ft. In October 2024, Lexus finally gave Americans the triple-locked LX it had kept in Japan: the LX 700h Overtrail, with front and rear lockers on top of the center lock.

For 2027, the gas LX 600 continues in Premium, Luxury and F Sport Handling grades. The EPA rates the 2025 LX 600 at 17 city, 22 highway and 19 combined. It's still the most luxurious way into a 300 Series chassis, and with the shorter, boxier GX 550 now on the same platform, it's no longer the only GA-F Lexus that can go off-road.

SIDEBAR · THE LX AMERICA DIDN'T GET (AT FIRST)

Japan's triple-locked LX, and its US answer

When the LX 600 launched, Japan got a model called the LX Offroad, with front, center and rear differential locks. US buyers got a choice between leather and more leather. The closest US equivalent was the F Sport, whose Torsen rear limited-slip and 22-inch forged wheels suit a canyon road more than a trail.

That changed on October 10, 2024, when Lexus revealed the LX 700h Overtrail with front and rear lockers plus the standard center lock. Owners on IH8MUD have worked out the rules. The center lock can engage on the move, 4L needs the truck stopped in neutral, and the axle lockers need 4L and the center lock engaged first, at very low speed.

PART 02

Specs & capability

How it’s built, what it can do, and what fits under it.

How it’s built

The LX 600 is a 300 Series Land Cruiser underneath. It sits on the GA-F ladder frame, which Lexus says is 20 percent more rigid, with high-mounted double wishbones up front and a four-link solid rear axle with lateral control arms. Power from the 3.4-liter twin-turbo V6 goes through a 10-speed automatic and a two-speed transfer case to a lockable center differential. Hydraulic Active Height Control and AVS adaptive damping manage ride height and comfort, and Multi-Terrain Select, Crawl Control and an underfloor camera handle the trail.

What to check before you buy

  1. 01
    Recall status, in writingRun the VIN for 24V-381 (engine replacement, trucks built July 2021 to November 2022) and 25V-767 (Lexus 25LA07, built February 2022 to April 2024). Get dealer paperwork showing what was done. A replaced engine is a selling point.
  2. 02
    Listen for a knockThe recalls list knocking, rough running, a no-start and a loss of power as symptoms. Start it cold and listen at idle and under light load.
  3. 03
    Rolling-stop behaviorOwners report a clunk into first gear during rolling stops on 2022–2024 trucks, plus sluggish throttle that eases when the pre-collision system is set to its shortest distance. Test both so you know what's normal for these trucks.
  4. 04
    Which grade?The F Sport gets a Torsen rear LSD and a rear stabilizer bar, but also 22-in forged wheels that limit tire choice. Ultra Luxury is a four-seater. US LX 600s have no axle lockers in any grade.
  5. 05
    AHC healthCycle through Low, Normal, Hi1 and Hi2 and confirm each height is reached and held. Some trucks were ordered without AHC. Know which one you're buying, because lift options differ.
  6. 06
    Tailgate and electronicsAn owner-reported power hatch that beeps and refuses to close was usually a weak key-fob signal or a hatch that needed a manual close to relearn its position. Check that everything works before you assume a fault.

Spotting the real thing

Every US LX 600 sits on the same GA-F platform as the Land Cruiser 300, so the real differences are grade, AHC and recall status.

  1. ID
    VINUS trucks start JTJ and carry 7CX in positions 6–8. NHTSA's vPIC decoder reads them as the 310 Series with the V35A-FTS, built at Toyota Auto Body's Yoshiwara plant. The 10th character is the model year (N = 2022, P = 2023, R = 2024, S = 2025).
  2. ID
    LockersUS LX 600s have a lockable center diff only. Triple locks came on Japan's LX Offroad grade and, in the US, on the LX 700h Overtrail, which is a different model.
  3. ID
    F Sport Handling22-in forged wheels and a Torsen limited-slip rear differential.
  4. ID
    AHC or notMost trucks have Active Height Control, but Slee Off-Road's build was a Premium custom-ordered without AHC so it could run Dobinsons suspension. Check before planning a lift.
  5. ID
    Engine recall status2022–2024 trucks may be in 24V-381 or 25V-767 (Lexus 25LA07). Ask for paperwork showing the inspection or replacement engine.

Asked on every forum

Lexus LX 600 questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

The factory size on 22s is 265/50R22, about 32 in. Owners on aftermarket 20s run 275/60R20 (about 33 in) and 285/55R20 without rubbing, and 275/70R18 fits on Toyota TRD 18-inch wheels. 285/50R22 on factory wheels needed cutting and spacers. Without a lift, plan on about 33 in.

SOURCE · IH8MUD
Is my LX 600 part of the engine recall?

If it's a 2022–2024, possibly. Recall 24V-381 covered 3,524 LX 600s built from July 30, 2021 to November 25, 2022, with engine replacement as the remedy. Recall 25V-767 (Lexus 25LA07) covers 9,895 LXs built from February 26, 2022 to April 2, 2024. Check your VIN at lexus.com/recall or nhtsa.gov. The 25V-767 remedy is a No. 1 main-bearing inspection, with engine replacement if needed.

SOURCE · MOTOR1 · NHTSA
How much can it tow?

8,000 lb with trailer brakes, per the owner's manual (1,650 lb without). KBB lists the same 8,000-lb rating for every 2027 LX.

SOURCE · LX 600 OWNER'S MANUAL · KBB
Is the LX 600 just a 300 Series Land Cruiser?

Underneath, yes: same GA-F platform and 112-in wheelbase. Toyota didn't sell the LC300 in North America, which made the LX 600 the US version.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · LEXUS PRESSROOM
Does it have lockers?

There's a lockable center diff, but no US LX 600 has front or rear lockers. The F Sport gets a Torsen limited-slip rear. Triple locks came on Japan's LX Offroad grade and, in the US, on the LX 700h Overtrail.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · LEXUS PRESSROOM
How do I use 4L and the diff locks?

Owners say the center diff lock can be engaged while moving slowly in Drive. Shifting into 4L requires the truck to be stopped and in neutral. On trucks with axle lockers, the front and rear lockers need 4L and the center lock engaged first, at very low speed. If you switch off the engine in 4L, MTS won't come back on automatically.

SOURCE · IH8MUD
What fuel economy should I expect?

The EPA rates the 2025 LX 600 at 17 mpg city, 22 highway and 19 combined, on premium fuel.

SOURCE · FUELECONOMY.GOV
How much lighter is it than the LX 570?

Lexus says 441 lb (200 kg) lighter, with a frame it calls 20 percent more rigid.

SOURCE · LEXUS PRESSROOM
What are the off-road angles?

At normal ride height, 25° approach, 23° breakover and 20° departure. Lexus hasn't published figures for the Hi1 and Hi2 heights. Maximum wading depth is 700 mm (27.5 in).

SOURCE · YAHOO AUTOS · LEXUS
Why does it clunk into first gear?

Owners report a clunk into first on rolling stops, and sometimes on 6–5 downshifts, in 2022–2024 LX 600s. There's no known fix. Gentle throttle while the transmission decides helps, and larger tires make it less noticeable. Owners say the LX 700h hybrid doesn't do it.

SOURCE · IH8MUD
Should I buy one without AHC?

If you plan to lift it, maybe. Slee Off-Road's LX 600 build was a Premium custom-ordered without AHC so it could run Dobinsons suspension. AHC trucks keep the adjustable ride heights but limit your lift choices.

SOURCE · SLEE OFF-ROAD
