Fluids, capacities & service intervals

The 3UR-FE has a timing chain, so there's no belt to worry about. The LX 570's baseline is about fluids, the AHC and the known coolant weak spots: early radiators, the water pump and the valley leak. Before buying, enter the VIN on the Lexus owners site to see dealer service history, as the IH8MUD FAQ suggests. Capacities below come from the Lexus-hosted LX 570 owner's manual (OM60P32U), a 2016-or-later edition, so confirm them for earlier trucks.
