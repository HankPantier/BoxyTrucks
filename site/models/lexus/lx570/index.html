PART 01

Overview

Where it came from, why it matters, and how it changed year to year.

The story

The LX 570 is the 200 Series Land Cruiser in Lexus clothes, and for 13 US model years it was the most capable thing in a Lexus showroom. It sits on a new ladder frame with double-wishbone front suspension and a multi-link solid rear axle, and it uses the 5.7-liter 3UR-FE V8 with 383 hp and 403 lb-ft. Full-time four-wheel drive runs through a Torsen center differential that can be locked, and Crawl Control does the throttle and brake work for you on steep, slow terrain. Then Lexus added four-corner hydraulic Active Height Control with Adaptive Variable Suspension. That system is the main difference between the LX and the Land Cruiser, and it divides owners.

FIRST, FOR ONCE

Development of both the 200 Series and the LX began in 2002, and the design was approved in 2004. Lexus showed the 2008 LX 570 at the New York International Auto Show on April 4, 2007. It was the first LX to be revealed before its Land Cruiser twin. The frame was all new. A third of its mass is high-tensile steel, all three crossmembers are hydroformed, and the tow hitch is built into the frame, as on the Tundra. Lexus said the platform covered 240,000 test kilometers in subtropical forest, the Australian Outback and American deserts.

The LX was 102 mm longer than the LX 470 on the same 112.2-in wheelbase, and it lost the old truck's plastic lower cladding. The front suspension gave 9 in of wheel travel. ClubLexus quotes Car and Driver calling it "the most desirable SUV on the planet" and cites a 0–60 mph time of 6.7 seconds for a truck weighing about three tons.

THE V8 AND THE ROBOTS

The 3UR-FE is a 32-valve, chain-driven quad-cam V8 with dual VVT-i, shared with the Tundra and Sequoia. It drove a six-speed AB60F automatic, and from the 2016 refresh an eight-speed AE80F. Towing was rated up to 8,500 lb at launch; the 2016-on owner's manual lists 7,000 lb with trailer brakes. The electronics did as much as the hardware. Crawl Control works like an off-road cruise control in both directions. The truck also has multi-terrain ABS, Hill-start Assist and A-TRAC traction control, and from 2013 Multi-Terrain Select and Turn Assist, which brakes the inside rear wheel in tight low-range turns.

The AHC has six settings and can raise the truck about 3 in or lower it about 2 in from normal ride height, using a knob on the console. The AVS dampers are diagonally cross-linked through a hydraulic system, an idea Wikipedia compares to the 4Runner's X-REAS. One thing the LX doesn't have is the Land Cruiser's KDSS. An IH8MUD member fitting 35s points out that this actually makes big tires easier to fit on the Lexus.

THREE FACES

The LX 570 had three distinct looks. The 2008–2011 trucks are the plain-faced originals, with a 2011 bumper update that hinted at the spindle grille. There was no 2012 model year in the US. For 2013 came the first proper spindle grille, plus Multi-Terrain Select, Turn Assist and a five-speed Crawl Control. Lexus unveiled the 2016 refresh at Pebble Beach in August 2015, and it kept only the doors and roof. It brought an all-new interior and the eight-speed, and it was the first LX sold officially in Japan.

The last years brought niche variants. A two-row, five-seat version arrived for 2018. There were 500-unit Inspiration Series runs in 2019 and 2021, and a Sport package for 2020. US sales peaked at 7,915 in 2008, the launch year, and ran between about 3,100 and 6,000 a year after that, ending at 3,563 in 2021.

HOW IT ENDED

Production ended in October 2021, when the 300 Series-based LX 600 replaced the V8 with a twin-turbo V6. Toyota pulled the 200 Series Land Cruiser from North America after 2021 as well, so the LX 570 and its twin bowed out together as the last of the V8s.

On the used market the LX 570 is something of a bargain. DrivingLine noted that used LX 570s typically sold for less than comparable Land Cruisers, even though they were pricier and better equipped new. The discount is mostly fear of AHC repair bills. After a decade of ownership, one long-time IH8MUD poster concluded that the system had turned out to be "brilliantly engineered and built," without the catastrophic tales of woe owners had expected.

SIDEBAR · THE LX THEY DIDN'T SELL YOU

Diesels, superchargers and a 4.6

US buyers only got the 383-hp 3UR-FE. Elsewhere the J200 LX came in several more flavors. The LX 450d used the 4.5-liter 1VD-FTV turbodiesel V8, with 268 hp and 479 lb-ft, from 2015 to 2021. Hong Kong got an LX 460 with the 4.6-liter 1UR-FE (313 hp) for 2012–2014. Gulf and Australian LX 570s were rated at 362 hp.

The wildest was a supercharged Middle East special edition for 2014–2015, rated at 450 hp and 521 lb-ft. That's 67 hp more than any LX 570 sold in America.

Specs & capability

How it’s built, what it can do, and what fits under it.

How it’s built

The LX 570 rides on the new J200 ladder frame, with double-wishbone independent front suspension, a multi-link solid rear axle and a frame-integrated hitch. Full-time 4WD runs through a two-speed transfer case and a Torsen center differential that can be locked, backed by A-TRAC. US trucks have no factory axle lockers. Instead of the Land Cruiser's coil springs and KDSS, every LX uses four-corner hydraulic AHC with AVS adaptive damping and automatic load leveling.

What to check before you buy

  1. 01
    AHC leaks and behaviorCycle the truck through Low, Normal and High and make sure it levels evenly. The IH8MUD AHC sticky includes a Toyota diagram of leak-prone junctions. Fluid at those points means you're about to learn a lot about hydraulics. A healthy system also lifts an extra inch or so in some low-range recovery situations, one owner reports.
  2. 02
    Radiator (2008–2010)Owners of early trucks report cracks in the top plastic tank, next to the molded platform. Look for a wet line or dried pink coolant along the top. Later radiators carry a different part number.
  3. 03
    Water pumpForum members say 2008 trucks had water-pump problems that have since been fixed. Look for pink crust at the pump weep hole and ask whether it's been replaced. One high-mileage owner paid about $700.
  4. 04
    Valley coolant leakPink coolant dripping from the top of the transmission hump at the back of the engine usually means the valley leak: the heat-exchanger cover and union pipe under the intake. It's not hard to fix, but it takes a lot of labor.
  5. 05
    Driveshaft clunkA 'thunk' when taking off or stopping often points to dry slip yokes and U-joints. Ask when the driveshafts were last greased. One owner paid about $780 for a propeller-shaft assembly to cure it.
  6. 06
    StarterSeveral owners have had the starter solenoid fail with no warning and leave them stranded. On a high-mileage truck, proactive replacement is common.
  7. 07
    Records by VINThe IH8MUD FAQ points out that the Toyota and Lexus owner sites show dealer service history once you enter the VIN, which is a good check before buying.
  8. 08
    HVAC recirculation doorIH8MUD members warn that the recirculation door hinge can be broken by a careless cabin-filter change. Check that the recirc control actually changes the airflow.

Spotting the real thing

The LX 570 shares everything that matters with the 200 Series Land Cruiser, so check the facelift, the drivetrain generation and whether it's one of the limited Inspiration trucks.

  1. ID
    VINUS trucks start JTJ and carry 7AX in positions 6–8 (e.g. JTJHY7AX). NHTSA's vPIC decoder reads the series as URJ201L with the 3UR-FE, built at Toyota Auto Body's Yoshiwara plant. The 10th character is the model year (8 = 2008 through M = 2021, with no C for 2012).
  2. ID
    Which facelift2008–2011: original nose. 2013–2015: first spindle grille. 2016–2021: big spindle, new sheet metal, eight-speed and a new interior.
  3. ID
    SeatingTwo-row, five-seat trucks exist only from 2018 on.
  4. ID
    Inspiration Series2019: 500 trucks in Black Onyx with a Moonlight white interior and blacked-out trim. 2021: 500 trucks in Eminent White Pearl or Black Onyx with black-and-Rioja-red leather.
  5. ID
    No axle lockersUS trucks have a lockable Torsen center diff, A-TRAC and Crawl Control, but no factory front or rear lockers. Any locker is aftermarket.

Driveshaft service (grease and re-index)
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · An afternoonSHOP COST · A tube of MP grease
SYMPTOMSA clunk or 'thunk' when pulling away or stopping, driveline vibration and dry slip yokes.
THE FIXWith the wheels on the ground, chocked and the parking brake on, remove the transfer-case insulator (four 12 mm bolts). Paint-mark both flanges, then unbolt the rear shaft at the transfer case (captured bolts, 14 mm nuts) and at the diff. Clean the shaft, grease the U-joints and slip yoke until fresh grease purges, and reinstall on your marks with the fasteners torqued. Do the front shaft the same way. Remember that with both shafts off, only the parking brake is holding the truck.

Asked on every forum

Lexus LX 570 questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

The stock size is 285/50R20. The practical limit is about 33 in, and even that meant moving the front fender liner forward about an inch on one IH8MUD member's LX. His 35x12.5R20 build needed the liner moved further, the sidestep return trimmed and the body mount reshaped, with spacers giving about +35 mm effective offset. The LX is easier to fit than a Land Cruiser because it doesn't have the KDSS bar.

SOURCE · IH8MUD · BAT
How much can an LX 570 tow?

Up to 8,500 lb at launch. The 2016-on owner's manual lists 7,000 lb (with trailer brakes) for the facelifted truck, so check the manual for your year. The hitch receiver is built into the frame.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · LX 570 OWNER'S MANUAL (OM60P32U)
What fuel economy should I expect, and does it need premium?

Yes, the EPA lists premium fuel. The six-speed 2008 is rated 12 city, 18 highway and 14 combined. The eight-speed 2016 is rated 13 city, 18 highway and 15 combined.

SOURCE · FUELECONOMY.GOV
Does it have lockers?

The US LX 570 has a lockable Torsen center differential plus A-TRAC, Crawl Control and, from 2013, Multi-Terrain Select. It has no factory front or rear axle lockers. Owners who want them fit aftermarket units such as ARB Air Lockers.

SOURCE · SLEE OFF-ROAD · WIKIPEDIA · IH8MUD
Is the AHC going to bankrupt me?

Probably not, if you buy a healthy one. One long-time IH8MUD poster wrote that after ten years in service the LX 570's AHC had turned out to be well engineered, without the expected horror stories. But leaks at line junctions and actuators are expensive, so inspect the system carefully before you buy.

SOURCE · IH8MUD AHC STICKY
LX 570 or 200 Series Land Cruiser?

They share the same frame, V8, transmission and transfer case. The LX swaps coil springs and KDSS for AHC, adds more luxury equipment and 20-in wheels, and over time got its own styling, with unique front and rear ends from 2016. DrivingLine noted that used LX 570s were typically priced below comparable Land Cruisers.

SOURCE · DRIVINGLINE · WIKIPEDIA · IH8MUD
Which years should I buy?

The 2008–2010 trucks have the most radiator-crack reports, and owners say 2008s had water-pump issues. The 2013–2015 trucks add Multi-Terrain Select and Turn Assist. The 2016 and later trucks get the eight-speed and a new interior. There is no 2012 model year in the US.

SOURCE · IH8MUD · SLEE OFF-ROAD
Is it a timing belt or a chain?

A chain. The 3UR-FE is a chain-driven DOHC V8, so there's no 90k belt service like the LX 470's.

SOURCE · SLEE OFF-ROAD
Is there a 2012 LX 570?

Not in the US. Production of the 2011 model ran late and the 2013 started early, so the 2012 model year was skipped.

SOURCE · SLEE OFF-ROAD
What does Crawl Control actually do?

It works like an off-road cruise control. It controls throttle and brakes to hold a slow, steady speed over obstacles, forward or in reverse. From 2013 you can choose from five speeds, and Turn Assist brakes the inside rear wheel to tighten low-range turns.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · SLEE OFF-ROAD
