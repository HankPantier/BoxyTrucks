Maintenance schedule · 1996–1997

Lexus LX 450 maintenance schedule

The LX 450 is a 1996–1997 FZJ80 Land Cruiser with Lexus trim, and it shares the Land Cruiser's mechanical maintenance. Toyota's 1997 US maintenance guide for the Land Cruiser (FZJ80) was posted on IH8MUD, and it's the clearest factory schedule for the 80 we've found. It runs on 15,000-mile / 12-month services with the big items every 30,000. Most owners change oil far more often than 15,000 miles; the schedule is the minimum, not a target. Diesel 80s (1HZ, 1HD-T) follow their own markets' books.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

Toyota 1997 Land Cruiser maintenance schedule (US, 1FZ-FE petrol), as posted on IH8MUD. Earlier 3F-E trucks and diesels differ; check your book.

WHENWHAT TO DO
Every 15,000 mi / 12 months
  • Replace engine oil and filter
  • Grease the steering knuckles
  • Lubricate the driveshafts and re-torque their bolts
  • Rotate tires
  • Inspect transmission and diff fluid, ball joints and boots, brakes, exhaust, steering box and linkage
Every 30,000 mi / 24 months
  • Replace the air filter and coolant
  • Replace spark plugs (non-platinum)
  • Repack the front wheel bearings and grease the driveshaft bushing
  • Inspect transmission, transfer and diff fluid, drive belts, fuel lines and the fuel cap gasket
60,000 mi / 48 months
  • Everything in the 30,000-mile service
  • Inspect and adjust valve clearance
  • Inspect the drive belts
90,000 mi / 72 months
  • Everything in the 30,000-mile service
120,000 mi / 96 months
  • Everything in the 60,000-mile service, including valve clearance

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Toyota's special conditions: on rough, muddy or salted roads, check the chassis and body nuts and bolts at each service. On dusty roads, inspect the air filter at every 15,000-mile service. When towing or carrying a camper or roof load, also replace the automatic transmission and diff fluid at 15,000 miles and the transmission, transfer and diff fluid at each 30,000-mile service. Slee's guide suggests the front diff about every 20,000 km with ARB Air Lockers.
