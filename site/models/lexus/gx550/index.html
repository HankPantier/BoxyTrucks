PART 01

Overview

Where it came from, why it matters, and how it changed year to year.

The story

The third-generation GX looks like what people wanted the GX 460 to look like: square, upright and visibly built for a trail. Underneath is the GA-F ladder-frame platform it shares with the new Prado, sold in the US as the 250 Series Land Cruiser, and with the LX 600. It also has a 349-hp twin-turbo V6, a 10-speed automatic, full-time four-wheel drive through a locking Torsen center differential and, on the Overtrail grades, the first factory rear locker ever fitted to a GX.

REVEALED BEFORE ITS OWN TWIN

Lexus showed the GX on June 8, 2023, ahead of the Land Cruiser Prado it's based on, which tells you how important the GX had become in America. The GX 460 was outselling itself in old age, with US sales above 30,000 in both 2021 and 2023. Production of the new truck started in November 2023 at Toyota's Tahara plant in Japan, the only place the GX is built.

The Overtrail grades grew out of Lexus's 'Overtrail Project,' first shown at the Tokyo Outdoor Show. They get 33-inch all-terrain tires on 18-inch wheels, a body 4.52 in wider than the old GX, E-KDSS and an electronically locking rear differential. Expedition Portal's first look called it the best overland Lexus yet 'by a wide margin.' It also noted the front fascia was designed with aftermarket bumpers and hidden winch mounts in mind.

WHAT CHANGED UNDERNEATH

The 4.6-liter V8 is gone. In its place is the V35A-FTS 3.4-liter twin-turbo V6 from the LX 600 and Tundra, tuned here to 349 hp and 479 lb-ft. That's 48 more hp and 150 more lb-ft than the 460. The wheelbase grows to 112 in, which Expedition Portal points out is the same as the 80, 100 and 200 Series Land Cruiser wagons. KDSS goes electronic: E-KDSS can decouple the front and rear anti-roll bars at the push of a button instead of relying on purely hydraulic balancing.

Lexus also fitted a redesigned transfer case with faster high-low shifts and a center diff that splits torque 50:50. Multi-Terrain Select gets Auto, Sand, Mud and Rock modes in low range, plus Auto, Dirt, Sand, Mud and Deep Snow in high. Five-speed Crawl Control returns. The official angles are 26° approach, 24° breakover and 21° departure. The EPA rates it at 15 mpg city, 21 highway and 17 combined, which is only a modest improvement over the V8.

THE ASTERISK

The V35A arrived with baggage. In November 2025, Toyota filed recall 25V-767 covering about 127,000 Tundras and Lexus LXs and GXs whose engines may contain machining debris that can damage the main bearings. Possible symptoms include knocking, rough running, a no-start or a loss of power while driving. The GX population is 3,717 US-market 2024 trucks built between May 31, 2023 and April 10, 2024. At filing, Lexus said the remedy was still being developed. It has since been set: dealers inspect the No. 1 main bearing using inspection software and drive data and replace the engine if needed, and owner letters went out on August 28, 2026.

Toyota's report says some later engines were made with improved manufacturing processes and some with an improved main bearing. For a used buyer, that makes the VIN check the most important step in any GX 550 inspection. Buyers clearly didn't flinch. US sales reached 30,914 in 2024 and 37,180 in 2025, the best year the GX has ever had in the US. The square GX turned out to be the one Lexus should have built years ago, and the platform it shares with the 6th-gen 4Runner and the 250 means the aftermarket arrived early.

SIDEBAR · OVERTRAIL VS. EVERYTHING ELSE

What the Overtrail actually adds

Every GX 550 has full-time 4WD, low range and a Torsen center diff that locks. The Overtrail and Overtrail+ add the hardware enthusiasts actually care about: an electronically locking rear differential (engaged from a console switch), E-KDSS that disconnects the front and rear anti-roll bars for articulation, and standard 33-inch all-terrain tires on 18-inch wheels. The body is 4.52 in wider than the GX 460's, compared with 3.74 in for other grades.

The trade-offs are space and towing. Lexus lists seven seats only for the Premium, Premium+, Luxury and Luxury+ grades. On 2027 specs, towing runs from 7,601 lb (Luxury+) to 9,096 lb (Overtrail), so the off-road trim also happens to tow the most.

How it’s built, what it can do, and what fits under it.

How it’s built

The GX 550 rides on the GA-F platform, a ladder frame shared with the LX 600, the Land Cruiser 250/Prado and, according to Expedition Portal, the 300 Series and the Tundra. Front suspension is a new double wishbone design, the rear is multi-link, and AVS adaptive damping is available. Full-time 4WD runs through a redesigned two-speed transfer case and a Torsen limited-slip center differential that locks. Overtrail grades add an electronic rear locker and E-KDSS.

What to check before you buy

  1. 01
    Run the VIN for 25V-7672024 GX 550s built May 31, 2023 to April 10, 2024 may be in the engine debris recall (Lexus campaign 25LA07). Check lexus.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls, and get the dealer paperwork showing what was done.
  2. 02
    Listen to a cold startThe recall's listed symptoms are knocking, rough running, a no-start and a loss of power. Any knock from a V35A is a stop-and-ask-questions noise, not a negotiating point.
  3. 03
    Overtrail or not?If you want the rear locker, E-KDSS and 33s from the factory, it has to be an Overtrail or Overtrail+. Other grades have the locking center diff but no axle locker.
  4. 04
    Seats vs. capabilitySeven seats are listed only on the Premium and Luxury grades. Families that need a third row are buying a different GX from the overlanders.
  5. 05
    Tire upgradesTrucks already on bigger tires may have a top-shock spacer or adjusted control arms. Ask what was done to clear them, and check for rub marks on the liners at full lock.
  6. 06
    Aftermarket wiringLights, compressors and dual batteries are popular early mods. Look for tidy fusing and switch panels, not taps into factory harnesses.

Spotting the real thing

The GX 550 shares its bones with the Land Cruiser 250, so the questions are which grade it is and whether it was built before the engine-recall fix.

  1. ID
    VINUS trucks start JTJ. NHTSA's vPIC decoder reads them as the 252 Series with the V35A-FTS engine, built at Tahara, Japan. The 10th character is the model year (R = 2024, S = 2025, T = 2026).
  2. ID
    Overtrail vs road gradesOvertrail and Overtrail+ have the electronically locking rear differential, E-KDSS, 33-in A/T tires on 18s and a body 4.52 in wider than the GX 460. Seven seats are listed only on the non-Overtrail grades.
  3. ID
    2025+ Overtrail liftFrom 2025 the Overtrail sits 1 in higher from the factory, which matters if you're comparing a used 2024 against a later truck.
  4. ID
    Engine recall status2024 trucks built between May 31, 2023 and April 10, 2024 fall under 25V-767 (Lexus 25LA07). Ask for dealer paperwork showing the inspection or engine replacement.

THE FIXLexus's GX 550 owner's manual calls for ILSAC GF-6A SAE 0W-20. You can use 5W-20 in a pinch, but the manual says to go back to 0W-20 at the next change. DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 15 minutes to inspect · 1–2 hours for front padsSHOP COST · Covered under warranty if the bulletin applies; otherwise pads, hardware and fluid
SYMPTOMSA squeal from the front brakes when driving forward or in reverse. Lexus bulletin L-SB-0036-24 covers it on some 2024 GX 550s. Owners of the twin LC250 on LandCruiserForum mostly hear it first thing in the morning, or when it's cold and humid.
THE FIXIf the truck is under warranty, start at the dealer and ask about L-SB-0036-24. A LandCruiserForum member said the bulletin repair is almost a carbon copy of a Tundra fix. Tire rotation, wheel swaps and TPMS registration
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 1 hour, plus a 10–30 minute driveSHOP COST · Free if you do it; new sensors for a second wheel set
SYMPTOMSIt's 5,000 miles since the last rotation. Or the tire positions are wrong on the display after a rotation, or you're fitting a second set of wheels. If the sensor IDs aren't registered, the manual says the TPMS light blinks for a minute after about 20 minutes of driving and then stays on.
THE FIXThe Lexus schedule rotates the tires every 5,000 miles. Torque the wheel nuts to 97 ft-lb and recheck them after 1,000 miles. After a rotation, register the wheel positions. Park for 15 minutes or more with the engine off, then start it and choose Vehicle customize > Tire pressure > Tire rotation > Continue on the center display. Drive at about 25 mph or faster, with occasional left and right turns, for 10–30 minutes. Reversing clears the data. New sensors use 'New tire registration' on the same menu, and the GX stores two sets of IDs ('Set 1' and 'Set 2'), so a winter or trail wheel set can be swapped in about two minutes once registered. If you change tire size or run different pressures, set the tires cold and then use 'Set current air pressure.' Lexus warns the system may not work properly on non-genuine wheels. Every wheel needs a sensor. The spare hangs under the rear on a chain, and the holding bracket is tightened to 34.7 ft-lb.

12V battery: test, jump-start or replace
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 45 minutes to replaceSHOP COST · A battery, or nothing for a test
SYMPTOMSSlow cranking or a no-start, the smart access system not unlocking the doors, or a red or clear eye in the battery indicator (on trucks that have one). Blue means good, clear means it needs charging, and red means replace.
THE FIXThe battery sits under the engine compartment cover: push the tab in, pull the lock release, lift the cover, then pull it forward. To jump-start, open the positive terminal cover, remove the engine cover, and connect to the positive terminal and the metal ground point the manual shows (not the negative terminal). Run the donor vehicle for about five minutes before you try to start. To replace, turn the engine switch off, disconnect the negative terminal first, and fit a battery with the same LN4 case size, at least 80 Ah (20-hour rate) and at least 689 CCA. The manual warns that a battery of a different size can't be secured properly, and an unsuitable one may not restart the engine. Afterwards, re-initialize the power back door, and the moonroof and power running boards if fitted, plus the power windows if they act up. The engine may not start on the first try after reconnection. If you're planning a dual-battery setup, a GX 550 owner on IH8MUD says there's practically no room under the hood with the twin turbos, intakes and intercooler, so he put a lithium battery and a DC-DC charger behind the second row.
TOOLS & PARTSSocket set · battery tester or multimeter (12.3 V or higher with the headlights test in the manual) · jumper cables · terminal brush. The smart key uses a CR2032, which the schedule replaces at 30,000 miles. Lexus recommends a dealer do it.

DIFFICULTY · 1 / 5TIME · ≈ 10 minutes to check. Dealer or over-the-air update.SHOP COST · Free under recall
SYMPTOMSThe rearview camera freezes or goes blank in reverse on trucks with Panoramic View Monitor (25V-744, 2024–2025). The instrument cluster may not show some warnings or indicators (26V-341, 2024). The load-carrying-capacity modification label may show the wrong values (26V-179, 2024–2025).
THE FIXRun the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or lexus.com/recall. NHTSA's records give the remedies. For 25V-744, dealers update the parking-assist software (Lexus 25LB06). For 26V-341, the combination-meter software is updated over the air (Lexus 26LA06/26LB06). For 26V-179, dealers replace the label (Lexus 26LA04/26LB04). Note that NHTSA's database lists the truck as model 'GX', so a search for 'GX 550' can come back empty. The Lexus schedule also checks for available over-the-air software updates at every service.

Asked on every forum

Lexus GX 550 questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

The Overtrail comes on 33-inch all-terrains and 18-inch wheels. On IH8MUD's LC250/GX550 tire thread, 265/70R18 fits with no problems and many owners run 275/70R18 (about 33.2 in) without mods. 285/70R18 is where rubbing starts. 35s generally need a level or small lift.

SOURCE · IH8MUD · LEXUS
How much can it tow?

At launch, Lexus quoted up to 8,000 lb. Current Lexus specs list it by grade: 9,063 lb (Premium), 9,041 lb (Premium+), 7,844 lb (Luxury), 7,601 lb (Luxury+) and 9,096 lb (Overtrail).

SOURCE · LEXUS · LANDCRUISERFORUM
What fuel economy should I expect?

The EPA rates it at 15 mpg city, 21 highway and 17 combined. Expedition Portal called the combined figure a surprise, even though it beats the GX 460.

SOURCE · LEXUS · EXPEDITION PORTAL
Is my GX 550 part of the engine recall?

Possibly, if it's a 2024. Recall 25V-767 (Lexus 25LA07) covers 3,717 US-market 2024 GXs built between May 31, 2023 and April 10, 2024, for machining debris that can damage the engine. Check your VIN at lexus.com/recall or nhtsa.gov. The remedy is a No. 1 main-bearing inspection, with engine replacement if needed.

SOURCE · NHTSA 25V-767
What does the Overtrail get that other grades don't?

An electronically locking rear differential, E-KDSS that disconnects the anti-roll bars, standard 33-inch A/T tires on 18s, and a wider body. Seven seats are listed only for the non-Overtrail grades.

SOURCE · LEXUS PRESSROOM
How does the 4WD work?

It's full-time 4WD on every GX 550, with a Torsen limited-slip center differential that locks and splits torque 50:50. A two-speed transfer case gives you low range. Multi-Terrain Select offers Auto, Sand, Mud and Rock in low range, and Auto, Dirt, Sand, Mud and Deep Snow in high. Crawl Control works in low range at five speeds.

SOURCE · LEXUS PRESSROOM
Is it a Land Cruiser underneath?

Yes. It shares the GA-F platform with the Land Cruiser Prado, sold in the US as the Land Cruiser 250, and with the LX 600. The GX was revealed first, on June 8, 2023.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · LEXUS
What are the off-road angles?

Lexus quotes up to 26° approach, 24° breakover and 21° departure.

SOURCE · LEXUS PRESSROOM
Is there a hybrid?

At launch, Lexus said a hybrid powertrain would come to the US later. US GX 550s use the 3.4-liter twin-turbo V6.

SOURCE · LEXUS PRESSROOM
V6 or the old V8: which is stronger?

On paper, the V6. It makes 349 hp and 479 lb-ft, compared with 301 hp and 329 lb-ft from the GX 460's 4.6 V8. The V8's advantage is its long track record, while the V35A is still early in its story and has had recalls.

SOURCE · LEXUS · WIKIPEDIA
What oil does it take, and how much?

Lexus's owner's manual calls for ILSAC GF-6A 0W-20 and lists 7.7 qt with a new filter (7.4 qt without). 5W-20 is allowed only as a stopgap until the next change. The standard interval is 10,000 miles or 12 months, or 6 months / 5,000 miles if you mostly drive dirt or dusty roads, make short trips below freezing, or idle a lot.

SOURCE · LEXUS OWNER'S MANUAL
Which years are the best to buy?

It's too new for a consensus. The one documented problem year is 2024: recall 25V-767 covers early 2024 trucks for V35A machining debris, so check the VIN and get the dealer paperwork. NHTSA lists that recall for 2024 GXs only; 2025 trucks share the camera-software and load-label recalls but not the engine one.

SOURCE · NHTSA
