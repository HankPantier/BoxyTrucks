Lexus GX 460 problems: 8 things to check

Lexus GX 460 problems to check before you buy

  1. Service history firstGXOR's buying guide puts maintenance records ahead of mileage. A high-mileage truck with records beats a low-mileage one without. Pull the Lexus dealer history on the Lexus Drivers site before you go and look.
  2. Coolant on the bell housingCrawl behind a front wheel and look up at the transmission bell housing. Pink crust there points to the coolant valley leak, which shows up around 100k miles. Also check the water pump weep hole at the front of the engine.
  3. Oil around the filterOil residue near the oil filter and lower engine can be the driver-side timing cover, not the filter. Dealers have quoted $2,600–3,500 because the engine has to come down. Price it in or walk.
  4. Cold-start shiftingDrive it from a cold start for 15 minutes. If it won't shift 3–4 and throws P2714/P0761 with VSC and 4LO lights, you've found the solenoid problem that has led some owners to $7–8k transmission replacements.
  5. Secondary air pump (2010–2013)Failures of the pump and its air injection valves were common on early trucks and can put the truck in limp mode. Lexus extended coverage. Check whether the updated parts are already in.
  6. Center diff lock and driveshaftLock and unlock the center diff. A seized actuator can cost $1k+. Listen for driveshaft clunks. Grease is a temporary fix, and a Lexus TSB covers updated parts.
  7. Rear air springsPremium ('10–'13) and Luxury ('14-on) trucks ride on rear air. Look for sag after sitting overnight and a busy compressor. ClubLexus regulars say failures are rarer than on the GX 470 and fixes are cheap, but check anyway.
  8. Frame rust and radiatorFrame rust is the dealbreaker on salt-belt trucks. GXOR also notes radiators often start leaking between 100k and 150k miles. It's an easy job, but factor it in.

Lexus GX 460 recalls and service bulletins

  • Vehicle stability control too slow ↗ 2010 · NHTSA 10V-159After Consumer Reports' 'Don't Buy' rating, trucks built Nov 16, 2009 to Apr 8, 2010 got a VSC reprogram to stop sideways skids in sharp, fast turns.
  • Takata front airbag inflators (replacement inflators) ↗ 2010–2017 · NHTSA 19V-741Inflators fitted under an earlier Takata recall used the same design and may rupture. Dealers replace them again.
  • Takata front passenger airbag inflator (zone-based) ↗ 2010–2011 · NHTSA 16V-340Passenger frontal airbag inflators in trucks originally sold or registered in certain high-humidity areas may rupture. Dealers replace the inflator.
  • Takata front passenger airbag inflator ↗ 2010–2017 · NHTSA 19V-005Zone-based Takata inflator replacement that covers GX 460s by state of registration.
  • Low-pressure fuel pump ↗ 2014–2015 · NHTSA 20V-012The fuel pump may fail, causing a stall. Dealers fit a new pump.
  • Low-pressure fuel pump (expansion) ↗ 2018–2019 · NHTSA 20V-682Same pump failure, on later trucks. Dealers fit an improved pump assembly.
  • Load-capacity modification label ↗ 2018 · NHTSA 17V-831Incorrect load-carrying-capacity labels. Dealers supply corrected labels.
  • Air injection pump and air switching valve (check-engine light) ↗ · Lexus WEP ZLHCovered moisture-damaged secondary-air parts on 2010–2013 trucks for 10 years from first use, regardless of mileage, so most coverage has now run out.

Check any VIN for open recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls ↗, or see the recall tracker.

Owner questions about Lexus GX 460 reliability

Which years should I buy or avoid?

2014 and later. ClubLexus moderators call air-pump failure common on 2010–2013 trucks and say it should be far less of an issue on 2014-on, though the valves can still fail. 2014-on also gets the spindle-grille facelift; 2020-on Off-Road package trucks add Crawl Control, MTS and a transmission cooler, and 2022–2023 add CarPlay. If you buy a 2010–2013, confirm the updated air-pump parts are in. Lexus changed little mechanically, so condition and records still matter more than year.

SOURCE · CLUBLEXUS · GXOR · WIKIPEDIA
Is it reliable?

The core drivetrain has a good reputation. The well-documented failures are leaks and accessories: water pumps, the coolant valley, the timing cover, early air pumps and, on some trucks, the cold-start P2714/P0761 shift fault. Budget for those, and a 200k-mile GX 460 is not unusual.
