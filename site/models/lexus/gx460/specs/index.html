Fluids, capacities & service intervals

The 1UR-FE has a timing chain, so a GX 460 baseline is all fluids plus the known leak points. Check the coolant valley, the water pump weep hole and the timing cover before you buy. Specs come from the Lexus GX 460 owner's manual (OM60G24U), and intervals from the 2016 Lexus maintenance schedule.
