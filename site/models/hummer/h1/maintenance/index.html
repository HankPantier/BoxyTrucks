AM General Hummer H1 maintenance schedule

AM General's HUMMER Recommended Maintenance Schedule (#0574480) runs on a short 3,000-mile cycle with three inspection groups, A, B and C. It's demanding because the H1 is: 26 or so grease fittings, geared hubs, inboard brakes, CTIS and a transfer case that can drink the transmission's fluid. AM General says a truck over 24,000 miles that's behind on service should start at the 12,000-mile service.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

From AM General's HUMMER Recommended Maintenance Schedule (#0574480), as summarized in our buyer's baseline and Flash Off-Road's service articles. Fluids vary a lot by year; decode the VIN first (fourth digit engine, fifth transmission).

WHENWHAT TO DO
Every 3,000 mi (Group A)
  • Replace engine oil and filter (diesel-rated oil)
  • Grease every chassis fitting, including the two steering column fittings behind the kick panel
  • Check diff and geared hub oil level and condition
  • Inspect steering and front end; check halfshaft bolts
  • Test CTIS inflate and deflate
  • Check the transfer case level (an overfull case means the 'vampire')
Every 6,000 mi
  • Replace the fuel filter element; drain water as needed
  • Clean or replace the air filter
  • Inspect the serpentine belt and halfshaft boots
Every 12,000 mi
  • Flush the coolant and check the thermostats
  • Service the automatic transmission (match the filter to your pan)
  • Service the transfer case (NVG 242)
  • Alignment check
Every 24,000 mi
  • Check the batteries
After deep water
  • Change the geared hub oil; milky oil means water got in

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Off-road and water use is what the H1 schedule assumes. Flash Off-Road flushes the transmission more often than the book, and recommends synthetic Dexron in the transfer case. Never mix DOT 5 silicone brake fluid (1992–1998) with DOT 3 (1999 on, with ABS).
