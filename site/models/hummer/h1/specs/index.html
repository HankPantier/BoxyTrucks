Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume the last owner skipped things. H1s changed constantly in production, so decode the VIN first: the fourth digit is the engine and the fifth the transmission. AM General's HUMMER Recommended Maintenance Schedule (#0574480) runs on a 3,000-mile cycle with inspection groups A, B and C, and a truck that's over 24,000 miles and behind on service should start at the 12,000-mile service.
