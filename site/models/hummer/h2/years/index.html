Hummer H2 years to avoid, and the best years to buy

Every AM General Hummer H2 model year: which ones owners and specialists recommend, which have documented problems, which carry recalls, and what they sell for now.

Read the full Hummer H2 guideCommon problemsBiggest tireSpecs & fluids
The short answer

If budget allows, 2008–09 for the 6.2, the six-speed and the much better interior; owners say they're better in every way. They're also the rarest and most expensive, and some of their parts are specific to those years. Among 2003–07 trucks, one owner would pick a mid-2005 to 2007 truck over a 2003 for the stronger front diff and better build.

Hummer H2 year by year

YEARVERDICTWHAT CHANGED
1999—In December, GM buys the Hummer name from AM General; AM General keeps building the trucks
2000—GM shows the H2 concept, which has independent rear suspension that the production truck drops
2001—The H2 SUT pickup concept, with an Avalanche-style Midgate, is shown at the New York auto show
2003—The production H2 goes on sale in 2002 as a 2003 model, built by AM General in Mishawaka: 6.0 L LQ4 V8, 4L65-E four-speed, BorgWarner 44-84 full-time transfer case, standard Eaton rear ELocker, 4.10 axles
2003RecallFirst-year recalls: two wiper-motor campaigns (02V283000, 08V456000), the key and shifter override (03V075000) and the Hydro-boost relief valve (04V045000, also 2004)
2004–2005RecallHydro-boost accumulator (05V043000) and second-row center seat belt routing (05V163000) recalls
2005—The H2 SUT pickup goes on sale; the 6.0 is now rated at 325 hp and 365 lb-ft
2005–2007—Owners say the front diff was strengthened and build quality improved from mid-2005; one owner would choose a 2005.5–07 truck over a 2003
2006—Avtotor begins licensed H2 assembly in Kaliningrad, Russia (to 2009). Power steering hose recall 05V455000
2006–2009RecallHeated washer fluid module recalls (08V441000, then 10V240000 after new fire reports)
2008Sweet spotMajor update: 6.2 L L92 V8 (393 hp, 415 lb-ft), 6L80 six-speed, towing up to 8,000 lb, crawl ratio about 39:1, stronger transfer case, quicker steering, new interior with a rotary 4WD dial, curtain airbags and panic brake assist. Owners call the 2008–09 trucks the most refined H2s
2009—Last year. Production ends as GM tries to sell Hummer; the planned Black Chrome Limited Edition stops at six trucks, according to Wikipedia

“Sweet spot” means owners and specialists recommend those years; “Beware” means documented problems; “Recall” means check the recall was done rather than walk away.

What a Hummer H2 costs (October 2026)

H2s are still affordable, but clean, unmodified, low-mile trucks, especially 2008–09s, now bring real money. Owners say untouched late trucks are getting hard to find.

What to bring when you look at a Hummer H2

Six inexpensive tools that catch most of the problems above before you hand over the money. Or book a specialist: see how to get a pre-purchase inspection.

  • Bluetooth OBD-II scannerReads stored and pending codes, and shows the readiness monitors. If several say "not ready", someone may have cleared the codes just before you arrived.
    Amazon ↗
  • Paint thickness gaugeThick readings on one panel mean body filler or a respray, which usually means an accident or rust repair the seller hasn’t mentioned.
    Amazon ↗
  • Inspection borescope cameraFits through drain holes into frame rails, rockers and sills, where these trucks rust from the inside out.
    Amazon ↗
  • Bright LED flashlightFor the underside: leaks at the axle seals, transfer case and rear main, and the frame behind the rear wheels.
    Amazon ↗
  • Telescoping inspection mirrorShows the back of the engine, the top of the fuel tank and the inner fenders without crawling under.
    Amazon ↗
  • Compression testerOn a high-mileage or long-parked engine, uneven compression is the cheapest way to spot a tired engine before you pay for it. Diesels need a diesel-rated tester.
    Amazon ↗
