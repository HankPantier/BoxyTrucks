Maintenance schedule · 2003–2009

AM General Hummer H2 maintenance schedule

GM didn't give the H2 a fixed oil interval. The oil-life monitor calls for a service, and the owner's manual alternates two services, Maintenance I and Maintenance II, with at least one a year. The fixed-mileage items (coolant, plugs, transmission) sit in the manual's 'Additional Required Services' table. We've listed what the manual pages we could read confirm, and marked the rest as owner practice.

Read the full guide

Service schedule

From the 2004 and 2005 H2 owner's manuals (GM). Where the manual page online didn't show an interval clearly, the item is marked 'check your manual'.

WHENWHAT TO DO
When the CHANGE ENGINE OIL light comes on, at least once a year (Maintenance I and II)
  • Change engine oil and filter and reset the oil-life system
  • Lubricate chassis components (not the ball joints below 10°F, per the manual)
  • Inspect the engine air filter and replace if needed
  • Rotate tires and check pressures and wear
  • Inspect the brake system
  • Check coolant and washer fluid levels and look for leaks
Every 5 years or 150,000 mi
  • Engine cooling system service (coolant change), whichever comes first
Every year
  • Pressure-test the cooling system and cap (manual recommendation)
Per the manual's Additional Required Services table
  • Spark plugs and wires, engine air filter replacement, transmission fluid and filter, transfer case fluid and accessory drive belt inspection (check your manual for the intervals for your year)

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

The manual has a severe-service transmission schedule for trucks that tow, climb hills or sit in hot stop-and-go traffic. Owners call the 4-speed the weak link and do a pan drop and filter change on that schedule rather than a flush. Change both differentials after any deep water crossing, and check that the transfer case vent hose isn't kinked.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover

ITEMWHENWHY
Heater core quick-connectsIf coolant runs down the passenger side of the bell housingThe plastic fittings crack with age; check them before blaming the core.
Power steering fluidEvery oil changeThe pump also powers the Hydro-boost brakes, so a steering leak affects braking.
Rear air suspensionCheck the rear holds height overnightLeaks make the compressor run constantly and wear it out.
Front endEvery serviceTie rods, idler and pitman arms, lower ball joints and hub bearings carry a lot of weight on big tires.
BatteryTest yearlyLow voltage causes 4WD and locker faults on the H2.
