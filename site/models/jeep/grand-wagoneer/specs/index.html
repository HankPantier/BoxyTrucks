Fluids, capacities & service intervals

A Grand Wagoneer is at least 35 years old. Treat every fluid, rubber part and ignition part as original unless there are receipts. Capacities below are marked for confirmation because we couldn't find a sourced factory fill table. The parts catalogs split by year: 1984–86 versus 1987–91 (filters, ignition modules), and York versus Sanden A/C compressors (1978–88 versus 1989–91).
