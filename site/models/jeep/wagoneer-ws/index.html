PART 01

Overview

Where it came from, why it matters, and how it changed year to year.

The story

The WS is Jeep's full-size, body-on-frame, three-row SUV, revealed on March 11, 2021 as the 2022 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer and built at Warren Truck Assembly in Michigan. It borrows two of the most loved names in the family, from the SJ Wagoneer of 1963 and the original Grand Wagoneer, and aims them at the Chevrolet Tahoe, the Cadillac Escalade and the Range Rover. It launched with Hemi V8s, switched to a twin-turbo straight six, and spent its first five years arguing with itself about whether it was even a Jeep.

TEN YEARS OF 'SOON' (2011–2021)

Sergio Marchionne promised a new Grand Wagoneer at the Detroit show in January 2011, then planned on a Grand Cherokee and Durango platform. The project slipped repeatedly: a 2013 delay, a dealer preview promised for August 2015, teaser photos in October 2016 pointing at a 2019 model, and another delay in March 2017. In 2017 FCA committed $1 billion to retool Warren Truck for it, a figure that later rose to $1.5 billion. Spy photographers caught a Ram 1500-based test mule in December 2019, and COVID pushed production back again.

Jeep showed the Grand Wagoneer Concept on September 3, 2020, with no Jeep badge anywhere on it, and revealed the production trucks on March 11, 2021. Stellantis described them as a 'premium extension of the Jeep brand', with Wagoneer-certified dealers, a client-service concierge and ten written promises to every owner. Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer shared one body on a new frame that MotorTrend says is loosely based on the Ram 1500 (DT), but different enough that almost nothing is shared, and 2 inches wider than the Ram. They are built on the same Warren line as the Ram 1500 Classic.

TWO NAMES, ONE TRUCK, NO BADGE

Here is the part that confused everyone. The Wagoneer and the Grand Wagoneer are the same size. The Wagoneer (Series I, II, III and Carbide) was the mainstream model aimed at the Tahoe. The Grand Wagoneer (Series I, II, III and Obsidian) was the luxury version aimed at the Escalade and Navigator, with a two-tone black roof, fender flares, power side steps, bigger screens and, at launch, a CNBC-reported top price above $111,000. For 2023 Jeep added the Wagoneer L and Grand Wagoneer L, a foot longer on a 130-inch wheelbase. MotorTrend says buyers thought the Grand Wagoneer was a stretched Wagoneer. It wasn't: 'Grand' meant pricier, not bigger.

The branding didn't help. Wikipedia describes the launch branding as 'Wagoneer by Jeep', and the trucks wore Wagoneer badges with no Jeep logo. On February 29, 2024, Jalopnik reported that Jeep CEO Antonio Filosa had said Wagoneer would stop being a sub-brand once the electric Wagoneer S launched, and that future Wagoneers would wear Jeep badges. On October 14, 2025, Jeep revealed the facelifted 2026 Grand Wagoneer: the standalone Wagoneer nameplate was dropped, every truck became a Grand Wagoneer, Jeep badges went on front and rear, the Series trim names were replaced, and pricing started at $64,740. MotorTrend's headline that day was blunt: 'The Jeep Wagoneer Is Dead'.

FROM HEMI TO HURRICANE

The 2022 Wagoneer used the 5.7-liter Hemi V8 with Stellantis's 48-volt eTorque mild-hybrid system, rated at 392 hp and 404 lb-ft on a 3.21 axle ratio. The 2022 Grand Wagoneer got the 6.4-liter Hemi with 471 hp and 455 lb-ft on a 3.92 axle. Both drove through an eight-speed ZF-designed 8HP75 automatic. For 2023 the 3.0-liter Hurricane twin-turbo straight six arrived in the L models: 420 hp and 468 lb-ft in standard-output form, or 510 hp and 500 lb-ft as the high-output engine in the Grand Wagoneer. It was also offered as an option above the 5.7 in the regular Wagoneer.

For 2024 the V8s were dropped and every WS got a Hurricane. Jeep kept the 10,000-pound tow rating and said the six was up to 15 percent more efficient than the V8 it replaced. The high-output engine went up to 540 hp and 521 lb-ft for 2025. For 2026 only the 420-hp standard-output engine is offered, and the high-output version is on hold while Jeep readies a range-extended plug-in hybrid with a 92-kWh battery. Asked about a Hemi comeback, Jeep chief Bob Broderdorf told MotorTrend 'no announcements at this time'.

HOW BOXY, HOW OFF-ROAD, HONESTLY

Boxy, yes, in the way a filing cabinet is boxy: slab sides, an upright tailgate and a seven-slot grille stretched across 214.7 inches of body, or 226.7 inches for an L. Car and Driver says the 2026 facelift made the design squarer still, with the headlights set higher. But this is a fully independent family hauler, not a solid-axle truck. It has short- and long-arm front suspension with aluminum lower arms and a five-link independent rear on coil springs or Quadra-Lift air springs. The SJ's leaf springs and Dana 44 front axle are a world away.

Jeep never gave it a Trail Rated badge. The best setup is the Advanced All-Terrain Group, which adds a two-speed transfer case (2.64:1 low), a 3.92 axle, a rear electronic limited-slip differential, 18-inch all-terrain tires, skid plates, tow hooks and a 48:1 crawl ratio. With the air suspension in Off-Road 2 it has 10 inches of clearance, 25/24/22-degree approach, departure and breakover angles, and 24 inches of wading depth. MotorTrend ran an Advanced All-Terrain Wagoneer for 20,672 miles. Its verdict: keep it to dirt and fire roads, because the belly is too low and the flanks too exposed for anything harder, even at full height. The Grand Wagoneer is better at reaching a trailhead with a 10,000-pound camper than at driving the trail itself.

SIDEBAR · THE CONCIERGE ERA

Wagoneer Client Service, ten promises and the vanishing ambassadors

To justify Escalade money, the 2021 launch promised a buying and ownership experience separate from the Jeep showroom. Wagoneer-certified dealers would have a dedicated display and delivery area and specially trained 'Wagoneer Ambassadors'. The brand made ten promises to every owner and offered a Client Service concierge, and Stellantis's launch release listed five years of dealer maintenance, including oil changes and tire rotations.

Within three years the plan was being wound down. When Jalopnik called four 'Wagoneer Certified' dealers in Southern California in 2024, one salesman said he wasn't familiar with the program. A Jeep vice president told the outlet that the premium experience would continue under the Jeep badge.

Specs & capability

How it’s built, what it can do, and what fits under it.

How it’s built

The WS has a separate, fully boxed high-strength-steel ladder frame with crossmembers welded inside and out, aluminum hood and doors, a composite liftgate and about 6,500 welds in the body. MotorTrend says it is loosely related to the Ram 1500 (DT) but shares almost nothing with it, and MotorTrend's 2026 coverage calls the platform STLA Frame. Suspension is independent at all four corners, on coils or four-corner air springs with semi-active damping. Power goes through a ZF-designed 8HP75 eight-speed automatic to one of three Quadra-Trac or Quadra-Drive systems. Rear-wheel drive was offered on the Wagoneer, while four-wheel drive was standard on the Grand Wagoneer.

What to check before you buy

  1. 01
    Run the VIN for recalls firstNHTSA lists more than a dozen WS campaigns. Many are rear-camera software fixes (23V-577, 24V-436, 24V-944, 26V-326, 26V-531). There's also an airbag-control software fix (21V-873), a B-pillar trim that can foul the curtain airbag (23V-545), third-row belt buckles (23V-716), door and window trim that can fall off (25V-593, 25V-642), roof-rack crossbar pivot bolts (26V-584) and TPMS software (26V-559). Make sure each one is closed.
  2. 02
    Cycle the air suspension through every heightOn an air-sprung truck, run it from Park to Off-Road 2 and back with the engine idling, and look for a 'Service Air Suspension' message. Early 2022 trucks built on or before February 15, 2022 are covered by TSB 02-001-22: unseated air lines, an unplugged control module, disconnected height sensors and even red shipping caps left on the compressor vent hose.
  3. 03
    Ask whether the software is currentThis truck runs on software. Bulletins cover blank or frozen 10.1- and 12-inch center screens (radio update TSB 08-039-22), power-steering module flashes (08-202-22) and telematics resets (08-173-21). MotorTrend's long-term Wagoneer was updated at every dealer service. Check the screens, cameras, remote start and app connection before you sign.
  4. 04
    2022 5.7 eTorque: test low-speed stop-startIn 2024 NHTSA opened a probe into reports of 2022 Ram 1500s and Wagoneers losing power, with the engine shutting off at low speed. On a test drive, do several stops and restarts in traffic and note any warning lights. Ask whether any dealer work was done on the eTorque system or the 48 V battery, which sits under the cabin floor.
  5. 05
    Know which 4x4 system it hasQuadra-Trac I is single-speed, with no low range. For trails, look for Quadra-Trac II or Quadra-Drive II with the 2.64:1 low range. Best of all is the Advanced All-Terrain Group, with a 3.92 axle, rear electronic LSD, 18-inch all-terrain tires, skid plates and tow hooks. A rear-drive Wagoneer is a different vehicle altogether.
  6. 06
    Check the oil in the service recordsThe standard-output Hurricane specifies 0W-20, while the high-output Hurricane in Grand Wagoneers specifies 0W-40 (MS-A0921). A Grand Wagoneer serviced with the wrong oil by a quick-lube shop is a reason to ask more questions. The 6.4 also uses 0W-40, and the 5.7 uses 0W-20.
  7. 07
    If you'll tow, look for the right packageThe 10,000-lb rating applies to properly equipped Wagoneers. The Heavy-Duty Trailer Tow package adds the trailer brake controller, hitch zoom and heavy-duty cooling. MotorTrend found the hitch sat oddly high, so they needed a big drop hitch, and the air suspension kept adjusting ride height with a trailer attached.
  8. 08
    Price it against a new oneA new 2026 Grand Wagoneer starts at $64,740, so check new-truck pricing and incentives before paying close to that for a used one. Bring a Trailer has sold only one WS so far: a 2025 Grand Wagoneer L Series III for $78,500 in January 2026.

Spotting the real thing

Wagoneer, Grand Wagoneer, their L versions and three engines all share one body, and the badges changed in 2026. Here's how to tell what you're looking at.

  1. ID
    Wagoneer vs Grand Wagoneer (2022–2025)Same body and size. Grand Wagoneers have a two-tone black roof, fender flares, power retractable side steps, a 12.3-inch cluster and a different grille, and air suspension is standard. Wagoneers have a standard side step and a 10.25-inch cluster, and air suspension was optional. From 2026 everything is badged Grand Wagoneer, with Jeep logos front and rear.
  2. ID
    Short vs LL models ride on a 130.0-inch wheelbase (123.0 standard) and are 226.7 inches long (214.7 standard). Look for a longer rear door-to-D-pillar section and quarter glass.
  3. ID
    Which engineAMSOIL's lookups list engine code T for the 5.7 Hemi (sales code EZL), J for the 6.4 Hemi (ESG) and P for the 3.0 Hurricane, split into EFH (standard output) and EFC (high output). The window sticker or a VIN decoder confirms which one you have, and that decides the oil grade.
  4. ID
    Air or coil suspensionQuadra-Lift trucks carry sales code SER, show ride-height controls next to the Selec-Terrain toggle and offer Tire Jack mode in the Suspension menu. Coil trucks can't be raised from the cabin.
  5. ID
    4x4 system and off-road packageQuadra-Trac I has no low range. Two-speed trucks (Quadra-Trac II, Quadra-Drive II) have a low-range selection. The Advanced All-Terrain Group adds 18-inch all-terrain tires, front tow hooks, skid plates, a 3.92 axle and the rear electronic LSD, so check under the front for the skid plate.

Then baseline the fluids most owners skip: both axles, the transfer case, the transmission and brake fluid. Specs differ by engine. The standard-output Hurricane, the high-output Hurricane and the two Hemis use different oils and filters. Diagnose a 'Service Air Suspension' warning →
DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · ≈ 1–2 hours to inspectSHOP COST · Covered by TSB 02-001-22 on affected 2022 trucks; parts plus labor otherwise
SYMPTOMS'Service Air Suspension' in the cluster, the truck stuck at one height, or air ride not working front and rear. On early 2022 trucks the code C15AE-00 (compressor pressure line leak) is common.
THE FIXStart with what FCA's TSB checks. Put the truck in Tire Jack mode from the radio's Suspension menu before you lift it. Remove the front inner wheel liners. Check that the air suspension control module connector is fully seated and the compressor vent hose is connected with no red shipping caps left on it. Confirm that each air line goes into its spring up to the tape mark, within 1 mm. Check that the front and rear ride-height sensors are plugged in and their linkage rods are attached. Deflating the springs and clearing codes needs a scan tool with air-suspension functions (the dealer uses wiTECH). Leaks after that call for soapy water on the lines and the valve block.

Asked on every forum

Jeep Wagoneer & Grand Wagoneer (WS) questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

We couldn't find an owner consensus we trust yet, so we won't guess. Factory sizes are 275/65R18 all-terrains with the Advanced All-Terrain Group, 275/55R20 and 285/45R22, on a 6x139.7 bolt pattern. Limitless Tire's fitment guide lists 275–305 mm widths on 18- to 26-inch wheels. Test-fit anything taller at full lock in Off-Road 2 and at Park height, because the air suspension drops the body 1.6 inches. Running bigger tires on yours? Tell us.

SOURCE · MOTORTREND · LIMITLESS TIRE
What's the difference between a Wagoneer and a Grand Wagoneer?

Price and equipment, not size. Both share the same body and wheelbase. Through 2025 the Grand Wagoneer added standard air suspension, Quadra-Drive II, a two-tone roof, fender flares, power side steps, bigger screens and the bigger engine (6.4 Hemi, then the high-output Hurricane). The long 'L' versions of each are 12 inches longer. From 2026 every truck is called Grand Wagoneer.

SOURCE · STELLANTIS · MOTOR1
Is it actually any good off-road?

Good for its size, but it isn't a Wrangler. It isn't Trail Rated. With the Advanced All-Terrain Group it has low range, a rear electronic LSD, skid plates, 10 inches of clearance and a 48:1 crawl ratio. MotorTrend's 20,000-mile test truck was happiest on dirt and fire roads. Its belly sat too low and its flanks were too exposed for harder trails, even at maximum height.

SOURCE · MOTORTREND
Does it have lockers or low range?

There are no lockers. Quadra-Trac II and Quadra-Drive II have a two-speed transfer case with 2.64:1 low range. Quadra-Drive II adds a rear electronic limited-slip differential. Quadra-Trac I is single-speed with no low range, and rear-drive Wagoneers have no 4x4 at all.

SOURCE · MOTORTREND · STELLANTIS
Which engine should I buy: Hemi or Hurricane?

The 5.7 eTorque (2022–23 Wagoneer) and the 6.4 (2022–23 Grand Wagoneer) are familiar Hemis. The 3.0 Hurricane twin-turbo six makes more power and torque. Jeep said the 420-hp version is up to 15 percent more efficient than the V8, and MotorTrend loved its smoothness. The 2022 5.7 eTorque was part of a 2024 NHTSA probe into engines shutting off at low speed, so check its history.

SOURCE · MOTOR1 · MOTORTREND · LA TIMES
How much can it tow?

Up to 10,000 lb for a properly equipped Wagoneer, which Jeep calls best in class. Motor1 reported a 9,860-lb rating for the 2023 Grand Wagoneer. Get the Heavy-Duty Trailer Tow package for the brake controller and extra cooling, and a deep drop hitch: MotorTrend found the receiver mounted high.

SOURCE · STELLANTIS · MOTOR1 · MOTORTREND
What fuel economy does it get?

MotorTrend's 2023 4x4 Wagoneer with the 420-hp Hurricane was rated 16 mpg city, 26 highway and 19 combined. Over 20,672 miles it averaged 16.4 mpg, and its best tank was 20.3 mpg.

SOURCE · MOTORTREND
What oil does it take?

It depends on the engine. The 5.7 and the standard-output 3.0 take 0W-20 meeting MS-6395. The 6.4 and the high-output 3.0 in Grand Wagoneers take 0W-40, with the 6.4 calling for MS-12633 and the HO Hurricane for MS-A0921. The owner's manual and AMSOIL both list 7 qt with the filter for the V8s and 7.5 qt for the Hurricane.

SOURCE · OWNER'S MANUAL · AMSOIL
Why doesn't it have a Jeep badge?

At launch in 2021, Wagoneer was sold as a premium extension of Jeep with its own badges, dealer program and concierge. In February 2024 Jeep said it would stop being a sub-brand and future Wagoneers would wear Jeep badges. The facelifted 2026 Grand Wagoneer, revealed October 14, 2025, has Jeep logos front and rear.

SOURCE · STELLANTIS · JALOPNIK · CAR AND DRIVER
Can I lift it?

Coil-spring trucks can take Rough Country's 3-inch bolt-on kit (60800) for 2022–2025 4WD Wagoneers. It does not fit trucks with Quadra-Lift air suspension. Air-sprung trucks already lift themselves to 10 inches of clearance in Off-Road 2.

SOURCE · ROUGH COUNTRY
Is it reliable?

Too new for a long record. NHTSA lists more than a dozen recalls, mostly software, camera and trim fixes. Early 2022 trucks had an air-suspension assembly TSB. MotorTrend's long-termer needed only two routine services totalling $330.69 in 20,672 miles. Buy one with the recalls done and the warranty still running.

SOURCE · NHTSA · MOTORTREND
