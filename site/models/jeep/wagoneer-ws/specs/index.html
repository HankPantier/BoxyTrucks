Fluids, capacities & service intervals

A WS is new enough that most are still under factory or extended warranty, so get every recall and software flash done at the dealer first. Then baseline the fluids most owners skip: both axles, the transfer case, the transmission and brake fluid. Specs differ by engine. The standard-output Hurricane, the high-output Hurricane and the two Hemis use different oils and filters. Fluid types, schedules and the oil and coolant capacities below are from the 2022 and 2025 Wagoneer owner's manuals on Mopar's site; axle and transmission capacities are from AMSOIL's application lookups.
