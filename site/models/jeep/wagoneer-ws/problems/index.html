Exercise your lockers

Engage the lockers every six months or so, on almost any boxy truck. Both of my locker problems, on the 80 Series and the G500, came from systems that weren’t used often enough.

Jeep Wagoneer (WS) problems to check before you buy

  1. Run the VIN for recalls firstNHTSA lists more than a dozen WS campaigns. Many are rear-camera software fixes (23V-577, 24V-436, 24V-944, 26V-326, 26V-531). There's also an airbag-control software fix (21V-873), a B-pillar trim that can foul the curtain airbag (23V-545), third-row belt buckles (23V-716), door and window trim that can fall off (25V-593, 25V-642), roof-rack crossbar pivot bolts (26V-584) and TPMS software (26V-559). Make sure each one is closed.
  2. Cycle the air suspension through every heightOn an air-sprung truck, run it from Park to Off-Road 2 and back with the engine idling, and look for a 'Service Air Suspension' message. Early 2022 trucks built on or before February 15, 2022 are covered by TSB 02-001-22: unseated air lines, an unplugged control module, disconnected height sensors and even red shipping caps left on the compressor vent hose.
  3. Ask whether the software is currentThis truck runs on software. Bulletins cover blank or frozen 10.1- and 12-inch center screens (radio update TSB 08-039-22), power-steering module flashes (08-202-22) and telematics resets (08-173-21). MotorTrend's long-term Wagoneer was updated at every dealer service. Check the screens, cameras, remote start and app connection before you sign.
  4. 2022 5.7 eTorque: test low-speed stop-startIn 2024 NHTSA opened a probe into reports of 2022 Ram 1500s and Wagoneers losing power, with the engine shutting off at low speed. On a test drive, do several stops and restarts in traffic and note any warning lights. Ask whether any dealer work was done on the eTorque system or the 48 V battery, which sits under the cabin floor.
  5. Know which 4x4 system it hasQuadra-Trac I is single-speed, with no low range. For trails, look for Quadra-Trac II or Quadra-Drive II with the 2.64:1 low range. Best of all is the Advanced All-Terrain Group, with a 3.92 axle, rear electronic LSD, 18-inch all-terrain tires, skid plates and tow hooks. A rear-drive Wagoneer is a different vehicle altogether.
  6. Check the oil in the service recordsThe standard-output Hurricane specifies 0W-20, while the high-output Hurricane in Grand Wagoneers specifies 0W-40 (MS-A0921). A Grand Wagoneer serviced with the wrong oil by a quick-lube shop is a reason to ask more questions. The 6.4 also uses 0W-40, and the 5.7 uses 0W-20.
  7. If you'll tow, look for the right packageThe 10,000-lb rating applies to properly equipped Wagoneers. The Heavy-Duty Trailer Tow package adds the trailer brake controller, hitch zoom and heavy-duty cooling. MotorTrend found the hitch sat oddly high, so they needed a big drop hitch, and the air suspension kept adjusting ride height with a trailer attached.
  8. Price it against a new oneA new 2026 Grand Wagoneer starts at $64,740, so check new-truck pricing and incentives before paying close to that for a used one. Bring a Trailer has sold only one WS so far: a 2025 Grand Wagoneer L Series III for $78,500 in January 2026.

Jeep Wagoneer (WS) recalls and service bulletins

  • Airbag control module software ↗ 2022 · NHTSA 21V-873The occupant restraint controller could have software that disables the driver, passenger and knee airbags without warning.
  • Second-row center-seat recliner strap ↗ 2022 · NHTSA 21V-919The strap can bind on the trim so the seat back doesn't lock upright (FMVSS 207).
  • B-pillar trim and curtain airbag ↗ 2022–2023 · NHTSA 23V-545Upper B-pillar trim may not be fully seated and could interfere with side-curtain airbag deployment.
  • Rear camera (CVPAM software) ↗ 2022–2024 · NHTSA 23V-577Park-assist module software may stop the rearview image displaying in reverse.
  • Third-row seat belt buckles ↗ 2022–2024 · NHTSA 23V-716Third-row occupants may not be able to reach or fasten the buckle.
  • Steering column control module weld ↗ 2023–2024 · NHTSA 24V-199A poorly welded clockspring/column module can stop the driver's airbag deploying. Part of a multi-model Stellantis campaign.
  • Rear camera (radio software) ↗ 2022–2023 · NHTSA 24V-436Radio software may prevent the rearview image from displaying.
  • Rear camera module software ↗ 2024 · NHTSA 24V-944Incorrect camera-module software may stop the rearview image displaying.
  • Door trim may detach ↗ 2022–2025 · NHTSA 25V-593Driver and passenger door trim may not have been installed properly and can come off on the road.
  • Window quarter trim may detach ↗ 2022–2024 · NHTSA 25V-642Quarter trim on the door windows may not be secured and can come off.
  • Camera harness (2026 Grand Wagoneer) ↗ 2026 · NHTSA 26V-326The front camera harness may not be connected, which can blank the rearview image.
  • Gear-position indicator software ↗ 2023 Wagoneer · 2024 Wagoneer L · NHTSA 26V-413The gear selection indicator may not display, which risks a rollaway.
  • ESC and brake-assist software (2026) ↗ 2026 · NHTSA 26V-414Brake control module software may cause a loss of stability control and electronic brake assist.
  • Rear camera image (2026, multi-model) ↗ 2026 · NHTSA 26V-531The rearview image may fail to display in reverse.
  • Tire pressure monitoring software ↗ 2024–2026 · NHTSA 26V-559TPMS may fail to detect low pressure or light the warning lamp.
  • Roof rack crossbar pivot bolts ↗ 2022–2026 (all WS models incl. L) · NHTSA 26V-584Pivot bolts may not have been tightened, so the crossbars can detach.
  • Air suspension inoperable (early 2022 builds) ↗ · TSB 02-001-22 Rev. A · RSU 22-083For 2022 WS trucks with Quadra-Lift (sales code SER) built on or before Feb 15, 2022: check for unseated air lines, unplugged ASCM or height sensors, and shipping caps left on the vent hose. DTC C15AE-00.
  • Blank or unresponsive 10.1 in / 12 in center display ↗ · TSB 08-039-22 Rev. BRadio (R1 head unit) software update to perform before replacing the display.
  • Electric power steering module update ↗ · TSB 08-202-22 Rev. BEPS module flash for MIL or EPS warning messages.
  • Telematics box module update ↗ · TSB 08-173-21 Rev. ATBM reflash for inoperative remote functions or Wi-Fi.
  • NHTSA investigation: engine stall with intermittent restart ↗ · NHTSA preliminary evaluation (2024)Opened in 2024 into 2022 Ram 1500 and Wagoneer engines shutting off at low speed.

Check any VIN for open recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls ↗, or see the recall tracker.

Owner questions about Jeep Wagoneer (WS) reliability

Is it reliable?

Too new for a long record. NHTSA lists more than a dozen recalls, mostly software, camera and trim fixes. Early 2022 trucks had an air-suspension assembly TSB. MotorTrend's long-termer needed only two routine services totalling $330.69 in 20,672 miles. Buy one with the recalls done and the warranty still running.
