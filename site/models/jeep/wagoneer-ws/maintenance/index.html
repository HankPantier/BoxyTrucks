Maintenance schedule · 2022–present

Jeep Wagoneer & Grand Wagoneer (WS) maintenance schedule

Jeep's 2025 Wagoneer handbook covers the 3.0 Hurricane twin-turbo six, which replaced the 5.7 and 6.4 V8s used in the 2022–2023 trucks. Oil goes by the indicator, within 500 miles of the message and never past 10,000 miles or 12 months. The rest runs on fixed mileages. As on other Jeeps, axle fluid changes are tied to 'severe' use, which includes off-road driving and frequent towing.

Service schedule

From Jeep's 2025 Wagoneer owner's handbook (3.0L). V8 trucks from 2022–2023 follow their own book; check it for the V8 plug interval.

WHENWHAT TO DO
When the oil-change indicator comes on (max 10,000 mi / 12 months)
  • Engine oil and filter
  • Inspect battery, CV and U-joints, brakes and hoses, cooling system, exhaust
  • Inspect the air filter if driven in dust or off-road
  • Deploy the power side steps, clean the linkages and lubricate them
Every 10,000 mi
  • Rotate tires (sooner if tread depth differs by 2/32 in or more)
  • Inspect CV and universal joints
Every 12,000 mi
  • Replace the cabin air filter
Every 20,000 mi
  • Inspect front suspension, tie rod ends and boots
  • Inspect front and rear axle fluid
  • Inspect brake linings and pads, and parking brake function
Every 30,000 mi
  • Replace the engine air filter
  • Inspect transfer case fluid
Every 60,000 mi
  • Spark plugs, 3.0L (mileage only)
100,000 mi
  • Replace the PCV valve
10 years or 150,000 mi
  • Flush and replace the engine coolant
150,000 mi
  • Replace the accessory drive belt; inspect the tensioner and pulley
  • Change the transfer case fluid

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Jeep's handbook says to change, not just inspect, the front and rear axle fluid at the 20,000-mile checks if the truck is used off-road, for frequent trailer towing, or as a police, taxi or fleet vehicle. Short trips, towing, extreme temperatures and long idling all bring the oil-change message on sooner.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover

ITEMWHENWHY
Engine coolant10 years even at low milesThe handbook's limit is 10 years or 150,000 miles, whichever comes first.
Power side stepsEvery oil change, more often in winterJeep asks for the linkages to be washed out and lubricated at each oil change.
