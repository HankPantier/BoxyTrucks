PART 01

Overview

Where it came from, why it matters, and how it changed year to year.

The story

The four-door was the JL that mattered most, so it came first: Wrangler Unlimited production began at Toledo in November 2017, months ahead of the two-door, and it ran alongside the outgoing JK Unlimited until April 2018 so dealers could keep up with demand. It grew 3.5 inches in overall length over the JK Unlimited, to 188.4 inches on a 118.4-inch wheelbase, while losing weight. It kept the separate frame, the solid axles and the removable doors. Then, over the next few years, Jeep gave it more powertrains than any Wrangler before.

DIESEL, HYBRID, HEMI

Launch engines were the 285-hp 3.6 Pentastar, with optional eTorque, and the 270-hp 2.0 turbo four. For 2020 North America got the VM-built 3.0 EcoDiesel V6 with 260 hp and 443 lb-ft (600 Nm). It was dropped for 2024. The 4xe plug-in hybrid followed for 2021: the 2.0 turbo plus electric motors and a 17 kWh battery, for 375 hp, 470 lb-ft and an EPA-rated 21 miles of electric range.

Also for 2021 came the Rubicon 392, with the 6.4-liter Hemi V8 from Dodge's Scat Pack cars making 470 hp and 470 lb-ft. Quadratec's specs show it uses the full-time Selec-Trac case and the heavier ZF 8HP75 automatic rather than the Rubicon's Rock-Trac. Its 1,351-lb payload is the highest in the range. Jeep announced 'final editions' for 2024 and 2025, then reversed course in August 2025: CEO Bob Broderdorf said the 392 would stay, and a V8 Gladiator would join it.

THE FAMILY OVERLANDER

For overlanders the Unlimited is the obvious JL. It tows 3,500 lb, or 5,000 lb with the 2024 full-float Dana 44 HD rear axle and the automatic on non-4xe trucks, and it has a cargo area that takes a fridge and drawers. Payload is the limit: Quadratec lists 880 lb for a 2020 Sahara and 892 lb for a Rubicon, so a steel-bumpered, roof-racked build overruns it fast. The JL Unlimited is also the basis for the Gladiator pickup, which borrows its styling and interior.

It isn't perfect. The IIHS rated it Marginal in the small-overlap crash test after a 2020 Unlimited rolled onto its side, and a 2022 retest ended the same way. The 4xe has been recalled for battery fire risk, and 2021–2025 trucks have been recalled for power-steering pump wiring. Both recalls told owners to park outside until repaired.

2024 AND BEYOND

The 2024 refresh brought the four-door the same updates as the two-door: a 12.3-inch Uconnect 5 screen, side-curtain airbags in both rows, a new grille, a windshield-frame antenna, an available Warn winch and the Rubicon X with 35s. The 4xe gained a cheaper Sport S trim and a four-outlet 'Power Box'. Wikipedia reports that Stellantis then dropped the Wrangler 4xe from the US market in 2025. The JL Unlimited competes with the Bronco, the Defender 110, the Ineos Grenadier and the sixth-gen 4Runner, and it's still the only one with doors you can leave at home.

SIDEBAR · THE 4xe AND ITS FIRE RECALLS

Park it outside, don't plug it in

In October 2024, KBB reported a recall of 154,032 2020–2024 Wrangler 4xe and 2022–2024 Grand Cherokee 4xe plug-in hybrids, because some high-voltage battery cells could be damaged and catch fire, parked or moving. Owners were told to park outdoors, away from buildings, and to stop charging until the repair was done. The remedy was a software update and, where needed, a new battery pack. AutoGuide reported that an internal review of customer data had turned up 13 fire incidents.

Before you buy a used 4xe, check NHTSA for completed recall work. Pickup Truck + SUV Talk lists both 24V-720 and the later 25V-741 for 4xe batteries.

How it’s built

Buying one

What to pay, what to check, and how to tell a good one from a clone.

What they cost

Ordinary four-door JLs have sunk to used-SUV money, but the 392 holds its value well. A late, low-mile Rubicon 392 still brings most of its sticker.

Early Sport / Sahara$14k–25k
Rubicon, EcoDiesel or 4xe$25k–45k
Rubicon 392$43k–92k

What to check before you buy

  1. 01
    Run the VIN for every recallLook for the frame and track-bar welds and the steering damper on 2018–19 trucks, the clutch on manuals, the 2018 park-lock rod (18V-280) and cruise-control software (18V-332) campaigns, the power-steering pump (26V-363, 2021–2025), the 4xe battery (24V-720 / 25V-741) and the EcoDiesel fuel pump (22V-767 and 23V-263). Get dealer printouts showing they're complete.
  2. 02
    4xe: test the chargingPlug it in. Pickup Truck + SUV Talk says owners often complain the 4xe won't charge from an outlet, blaming software, connections or an overheating control module.
  3. 03
    EcoDiesel: fuel and emissions historyAsk for fuel-filter receipts and the recall record. A high-pressure pump failure is expensive, and debris contamination is what the recall addressed.
  4. 04
    392: it's a different 4WD systemThe 392 uses full-time Selec-Trac and the ZF 8HP75, not Rock-Trac. Check the differential and transfer-case fluids. KBB put a new 2025 Rubicon 392 at $101,990, so price used ones against the trim and condition.
  5. 05
    Steering feelWandering or vague steering on 2018–2020 trucks points to the early aluminum steering box (owners cite TSB 08-074-20). Shake after bumps points to the damper, track bar or worn joints.
  6. 06
    Pentastar oil coolerLook for oil in the engine valley and down the back of the block.
  7. 07
    Payload versus buildRead the door sticker, then add up the bumpers, winch, rack, drawers and passengers. Quadratec lists 880–1,351 lb for 2020–21 four-doors depending on trim.
  8. 08
    Do you want the 2024 updates?Side-curtain airbags, the bigger screen, a 5,000-lb tow option and the factory winch all arrived for 2024, the same year the EcoDiesel disappeared.

Spotting the real thing

The four-door JL comes with five powertrains, and the 4xe, 392 and EcoDiesel were four-door only. Check what's under the hood before you look at anything else.

  1. ID
    Real RubiconDana M210 (44-class) front and M220 rear with Tru-Lok electronic lockers, Rock-Trac with 4:1 low range, and an electronic sway-bar disconnect. Sport and Sahara have the M186 ('Dana 30') front and M200 ('Dana 35') rear, with an optional limited slip, and Command-Trac with 2.72:1 (Selec-Trac optional on Sahara).
  2. ID
    4xePlug-in hybrid with a 2.0 turbo, 17 kWh battery, 375 hp and 470 lb-ft; it has a charge port and a smaller 17.2-gal tank confirm in manual. Check that the battery-fire recall (25V-741) has been done.
  3. ID
    Rubicon 3926.4 Hemi with 470 hp and 470 lb-ft, four-door only, with full-time Selec-Trac and the ZF 8HP75. Sold 2021–2025 as the Rubicon 392 ('Final Edition' in 2024–25), then as the Moab 392 for 2026. Check whether it has the 35-inch Xtreme Recon setup.
  4. ID
    EcoDiesel3.0 VM Motori turbodiesel V6 (260 hp), four-door only, 2020–2023. Check the high-pressure fuel pump recalls (22V-767 and 23V-263) have been completed.
  5. ID
    Xtreme ReconFactory LT315/70R17 BFGoodrich KO2s on 17x8 beadlock-capable wheels, 4.56 gears and a 1.5 in lift, offered from 2021 on the four-door Rubicon and 392.
  6. ID
    Selec-TracThe full-time MP3022 Selec-Trac transfer case (2.72:1 low) is a Sahara option and standard on the Rubicon 392; other Rubicons use Rock-Trac (4:1).

Post-purchase baseline

After you buy it

Baseline a used JL by engine. The 3.6 Pentastar, the 2.0 turbo, the 3.0 EcoDiesel, the 4xe and the 392 use different oils, plugs and cooling loops, and the 8-speed takes ZF-spec fluid, not ATF+4. Before spending money, run the VIN through NHTSA's recall lookup: JLs have had recalls for frame welds, clutches, power-steering pumps and 4xe batteries. Then change the gear oil, which owners skip most.

Fix steering wobble: damper, track bar, then the rest →
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 1–2 hours to inspect and fit a damperSHOP COST · Free damper replacement on affected early trucks under FCA's 2019 customer-satisfaction program; otherwise a damper or track bar
SYMPTOMSFront-end shimmy or full death wobble after a bump at 45–65 mph, with the steering wheel shaking side to side.
THE FIXOn 2018–2019 trucks, check the history first. In August 2019 FCA began offering a stronger steering damper at no charge. FCA said its engineers found air could get into the original damper in cold weather, when the oil thickens, so it lost damping. Its Customer Satisfaction Notification V41 covered about 192,000 2018–2019 JLs. Then inspect the front end the same way as on any solid-axle Jeep: have a helper rock the wheel while you watch both ends of the track bar, then check the tie-rod and drag-link ends, ball joints, control-arm bushings and wheel bearings. Replace worn parts, torque everything at ride height, and get an alignment. A damper on its own masks wobble; it doesn't cure it.

Replace a loose aluminum steering box (TSB 08-074-20) →
DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · ≈ 2–3 hours plus an alignment checkSHOP COST · JLWranglerForums members report dealer quotes of $1,500+ out of warranty
SYMPTOMSLight, vague, loose steering and wandering on the highway, sometimes with play you can see at the pitman arm. One owner described it as 'super light, vague and loose' at nearly 100,000 miles.
THE FIXEarly JLs used an aluminum steering gear, and Quadratec says Jeep moved to cast-iron boxes. Owners on JLWranglerForums cite TSB 08-074-20 for the swap (we couldn't see the bulletin itself), who also note it won't cure death wobble by itself. DIY: disconnect the battery, mark and remove the pitman arm with a puller, unbolt the intermediate shaft, disconnect the pressure and return lines (catch the fluid), then unbolt the box from the frame. Fit the new box with new bolts, refill and bleed the system with the specified fluid, and center the steering wheel.

Replace a leaking Pentastar oil cooler / filter housing →
DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · ≈ 4–6 hoursSHOP COST · Housing assembly plus gaskets, coolant and oil
SYMPTOMSOil, and sometimes coolant, pooling in the valley under the intake and running down the back of the engine. Pickup Truck + SUV Talk lists cracked Pentastar oil-filter housings and cooler adapters as a known JL issue.
THE FIXThe job follows the JK procedure. Remove the engine cover and intake plumbing, the upper and lower intake manifolds, and the cooler's sensors and bolts. Clean the valley, fit the new housing with new seals, then refill the oil and coolant and check for leaks. Torque the oil filter cap to 18 ft-lb; overtightening is a suspected contributor to cracking.

Power-steering pump: recall check, then replacement →
DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · ≈ 2 hours DIY; recall work at the dealerSHOP COST · Free under recall 26V-363 where it applies; otherwise a Mopar pump
SYMPTOMSLoss of power assist or a 'Service Power Steering' message. AARP reports that the June 2026 recall covers about 1,077,000 2021–2025 Wranglers and Gladiators whose electro-hydraulic pump connection can overheat and start a fire, even when parked. Owners were told to park outside until it's fixed, and at least 51 fires were linked to the fault.
THE FIXCheck the VIN on nhtsa.gov first. If the recall applies, the dealer inspects and repairs or replaces parts free of charge, so don't DIY around it. For an out-of-warranty pump failure on a truck that isn't covered: disconnect the battery, drain the reservoir, unplug the pump's electrical connector and pressure/return lines, unbolt the pump, fit the new one, then refill with the Mopar electric steering pump fluid listed in the manual and bleed by turning lock to lock with the engine running.

EcoDiesel: fuel-pump recall and fuel-filter service →
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 1 hour for the filterSHOP COST · Recall work free; a fuel filter for routine service
SYMPTOMSStalling, hard starting or a diesel smell. Pickup Truck + SUV Talk lists recall 23V-263 for possible high-pressure fuel pump failure on some 2021–2023 EcoDiesels, where debris in the fuel system can cause a stall or a fuel leak.
THE FIXConfirm the recall has been done. Then treat the fuel filter as a wear item, because the 3.0's high-pressure pump hates water and dirt. Relieve the pressure, change the filter, prime the system as the service manual describes, and check for leaks. Use only ultra-low-sulfur diesel; the owner's-manual sheet allows biodiesel up to B5, or B7 with shorter service intervals.

Fix 2.0T / 4xe coolant hose leaks →
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 1–2 hoursSHOP COST · A Mopar hose and coolant
SYMPTOMSA dropping coolant level, a sweet smell, or crusty residue around the turbo coolant lines or the coolant bottle hoses on a 2.0 turbo (the 4xe uses the same 2.0 turbo engine). Pickup Truck + SUV Talk lists a TSB for turbo coolant return line leaks.
THE FIXPressure-test the cold system to find the leak. Drain enough coolant to get below the leak, replace the hose and its clips, refill with OAT coolant and bleed the loop. Remember the 2.0T has a separate intercooler loop, and that the 4xe also has high-voltage battery cooling. Leave anything orange-cabled to a trained technician. Check the dealer history too, since the TSB repair may already have been done.

Re-spring for an overland load →
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 2–3 hours for rear coils; a day for a full kitSHOP COST · A pair of coils, or a heavy-load lift kit
SYMPTOMSRear sag, bottoming out on washboard, or the truck sitting on its bump stops once the drawers, fridge and water are loaded. Payload can be as low as 880 lb (Quadratec lists that for the 2020 Sahara four-door).
THE FIXWeigh the loaded truck at a scale first. Then fit springs rated for the real load: support the frame, unbolt the shocks and sway-bar links, lower the axle, swap the coils, and refit at ride height before torquing the bushings. For a permanently heavy build, a heavy-load kit with matched shocks is the better answer. Re-check the track bar and alignment afterwards.

Asked on every forum

Wrangler JL Unlimited questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

On a Rubicon, 35s. Driving Line ran 35x12.50R17s on a stock-height JL Unlimited Rubicon with bump-stop spacers and the factory flares. They cleared on level ground but hit the inner fenders when flexed. Performance Plus Tire says Jeep ships the Rubicon 392 on LT315/70R17s, which are nearly 35s (check early 392s: the 35s were also part of the optional Xtreme Recon package). Performance Plus Tire says a Rubicon 4xe has about 2.1 inches less clearance than the gas Rubicon, so it needs bump-stop extensions for 35s. On a Sport or Sahara, stay with 33s and a wheel change, or lift 2–2.5 inches for 35s.

SOURCE · DRIVING LINE · PERFORMANCE PLUS
Which engine should I buy?

3.6 if you want a manual or the simplest drivetrain; 2.0T for torque and economy; EcoDiesel (2020–23) for towing range; 4xe for silent electric trail driving and short commutes on battery; 392 for 470 hp. Each brings its own recalls, so check them before you choose.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · QUADRATEC
How much can it tow?

3,500 lb for most four-doors. From 2024, Hagerty says the full-float Dana 44 HD rear axle raises that to 5,000 lb on non-4xe trucks with the eight-speed automatic.

SOURCE · QUADRATEC · HAGERTY
What's the payload?

Quadratec lists 1,190 lb for a 2020 Sport four-door, 880 lb for a Sahara, 892 lb for a Rubicon and 1,351 lb for the 2021 Rubicon 392. The door sticker on your truck has the real figure.

SOURCE · QUADRATEC
Is the 4xe safe to buy after the fire recalls?

Yes, once the recall work is done. The October 2024 recall covered 154,032 Wrangler and Grand Cherokee 4xe models, with a software update and, where needed, a battery replacement. Owners were told to park outside and stop charging until it was fixed. Confirm completion on NHTSA, and test that the truck charges.

SOURCE · KBB · AUTOGUIDE
How far does the 4xe go on electricity?

The EPA rates it at 21 miles and 49 MPGe on its 17 kWh battery. Hagerty says the 2024 update recalibrated E-Save mode to recover energy about 50 percent more effectively.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · HAGERTY
Is the Rubicon 392 being discontinued?

Not any more. Jeep announced final editions for 2024 and 2025, then in August 2025 CEO Bob Broderdorf said, 'We will extend the availability of the 392 on Wrangler,' and announced a V8 Gladiator too.

SOURCE · KBB
What oil does each engine take?

3.6: 5 quarts of 0W-20 (MS-6395). 2.0T: 5 quarts of full-synthetic 5W-30, API SN PLUS (MS-13340). 3.0 EcoDiesel: 9 quarts of 5W-40 synthetic (MS-12991). For the 4xe and the 392, use the owner's manual confirm in manual.

SOURCE · QUADRATEC · OWNER'S MANUAL SPEC
How did the JL Unlimited do in crash tests?

Badly in one test. In the IIHS small-overlap test, a 2020 Unlimited's front axle tucked under the structure and the truck tipped onto its side. A 2022 retest ended the same way, so it was rated Marginal both times. Side-curtain airbags arrived with the 2024 refresh.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · HAGERTY
Does the JL Unlimited get death wobble?

It can, like any solid-axle Jeep. Early trucks got a free stronger damper in 2019. The cure is a tight front end: track bar, tie-rod ends, ball joints, bushings and bearings, followed by an alignment.

SOURCE · QUADRATEC
What do I need to know about the power-steering recall?

Recall 26V-363 (June 2026) covers about 1,077,000 2021–2025 Wranglers and Gladiators. A high-resistance connection at the electro-hydraulic pump can overheat and start a fire even when parked, and owners were told to park outside until dealers can inspect and repair it free.

SOURCE · AARP · NHTSA
Which years are the best to buy?

No year is the consensus pick, so we don't flag one. The one to be wary of is 2018: SlashGear counted 11 recalls and more than 1,000 complaints, mostly about highway wander, and NHTSA's list includes track-bar bracket welds (18V-675, 2018–19), the steering shaft (18V-343) and rear frame-rail corrosion (18V-231). SlashGear calls 2020-on trucks the more enticing buy for their engine choices, but each brings its own recalls: EcoDiesel fuel pumps (22V-767, 23V-263), 4xe high-voltage batteries (23V-787, 25V-741) and, on 2021–25 trucks, the power-steering pump wiring (26V-363). Buy the truck whose recall list you can see closed.

SOURCE · SLASHGEAR · NHTSA
