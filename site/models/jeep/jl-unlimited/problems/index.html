Jeep Wrangler JL Unlimited problems to check before you buy

  1. Run the VIN for every recallLook for the frame and track-bar welds and the steering damper on 2018–19 trucks, the clutch on manuals, the 2018 park-lock rod (18V-280) and cruise-control software (18V-332) campaigns, the power-steering pump (26V-363, 2021–2025), the 4xe battery (24V-720 / 25V-741) and the EcoDiesel fuel pump (22V-767 and 23V-263). Get dealer printouts showing they're complete.
  2. 4xe: test the chargingPlug it in. Pickup Truck + SUV Talk says owners often complain the 4xe won't charge from an outlet, blaming software, connections or an overheating control module.
  3. EcoDiesel: fuel and emissions historyAsk for fuel-filter receipts and the recall record. A high-pressure pump failure is expensive, and debris contamination is what the recall addressed.
  4. 392: it's a different 4WD systemThe 392 uses full-time Selec-Trac and the ZF 8HP75, not Rock-Trac. Check the differential and transfer-case fluids. KBB put a new 2025 Rubicon 392 at $101,990, so price used ones against the trim and condition.
  5. Steering feelWandering or vague steering on 2018–2020 trucks points to the early aluminum steering box (owners cite TSB 08-074-20). Shake after bumps points to the damper, track bar or worn joints.
  6. Pentastar oil coolerLook for oil in the engine valley and down the back of the block.
  7. Payload versus buildRead the door sticker, then add up the bumpers, winch, rack, drawers and passengers. Quadratec lists 880–1,351 lb for 2020–21 four-doors depending on trim.
  8. Do you want the 2024 updates?Side-curtain airbags, the bigger screen, a 5,000-lb tow option and the factory winch all arrived for 2024, the same year the EcoDiesel disappeared.

Jeep Wrangler JL Unlimited recalls and service bulletins

  • Front track bar bracket welds (the 'frame weld' recall) ↗ 2018–2019 · NHTSA 18V-675Track bar brackets may be improperly welded and separate from the frame, reducing steering capability; dealers inspect and repair the welds.
  • Intermediate steering shaft weld ↗ 2018 · NHTSA 18V-343The shaft may not have been properly welded and can split, so the wheel loses centre (recall U48).
  • Rear underbody ladder rail corrosion ↗ 2018 · NHTSA 18V-231The rail can corrode, weakening the rear floor and loosening the left rear seat mount; dealers apply rust protection (recall U27, 106 vehicles).
  • Steering column stub shaft ↗ 2019 · NHTSA 19V-201Improperly machined stub shafts can break and detach the steering wheel (recall V29).
  • Manual-transmission clutch pressure plate ↗ 2018–2020 · NHTSA 20V-124Pressure plate may overheat and fracture (recall W12).
  • Clutch overheating software ↗ 2018–2021 · NHTSA 21V-028Expansion of the clutch pressure-plate overheating recall to 2018–2021 manual trucks.
  • Clutch assembly replacement ↗ 2018–2023 · NHTSA 23V-116Further expansion to 2018–2023 manual trucks (pressure plate may overheat and fracture); 24V-572 re-flashes some 2021 trucks already remedied under it, because the new software could stall the engine.
  • Left lower control arm bracket weld ↗ 2020 · NHTSA 20V-042Bracket and weld may be mispositioned (recall W01, 3,005 vehicles).
  • Frame stud can puncture fuel tank ↗ 2020–2022 · NHTSA 23V-191In a crash a frame stud may puncture the tank (recall 28A).
  • Instrument cluster short ↗ 2018–2024 · NHTSA 24V-652The cluster can short internally and fail (recall 30B).
  • Electric-hydraulic power steering pump wiring fire ↗ 2021–2025 · NHTSA 26V-363Pump wiring can overheat and start a fire even when parked; owners told to park outside until repaired (1,076,999 vehicles across Wrangler and Gladiator).
  • 3.0 EcoDiesel high-pressure fuel pump ↗ 2020–2022 · NHTSA 22V-767The HPFP may fail and stall the engine (recall Z96).
  • 3.0 EcoDiesel high-pressure fuel pump (expansion) ↗ 2021–2023 · NHTSA 23V-263Same pump failure on later diesels (recall 01A).
  • 4xe high-voltage battery fire (first campaign) ↗ 2021–2023 · NHTSA 23V-787Battery may fail internally and catch fire; owners told not to charge and to park outside until repaired (32,125 vehicles).
  • 4xe high-voltage battery fire (expanded) ↗ 2020–2024 · NHTSA 24V-720Same fire risk on a wider population; replaced by 25V-741, so trucks fixed under it need the new remedy.
  • 4xe high-voltage battery fire (current remedy) ↗ 2020–2025 · NHTSA 25V-741Software update and battery replacement as needed; park outside and don't charge until done (320,068 vehicles incl. Grand Cherokee 4xe).
  • 4xe HV battery 200-amp fuse fasteners ↗ 2022–2023 · NHTSA 22V-768Wrong fasteners on the 200-amp fuse can overheat and cut drive power; reissued as 23V-303.
  • 4xe engine shutdown on lost communication ↗ 2021–2023 · NHTSA 22V-865A communication loss can shut the engine down.
  • 4xe over-the-air update loss of drive ↗ 2023–2025 · NHTSA 25V-710A software error in an OTA update can cause loss of drive power (recall A7C).
  • 4xe engine debris ↗ 2024–2025 · NHTSA 25V-766Debris inside the engine can cause failure or an engine-bay fire (recall 78C).
  • Rear door latch (four-door) ↗ 2018 · NHTSA 18V-786Right rear door latch may be mislocated and not latch (recall UB6).
  • Death wobble: steering damper replacement ↗ · CSN V41About 192,000 2018–2019 JLs get a new front steering damper for a sustained steering-wheel shimmy after bumps, especially above 55 mph and below 40°F. It's a customer satisfaction notification, not a safety recall.
  • Steering-wheel shimmy after road irregularities ↗ · TSB 19-002-19November 2019 bulletin for 2018–2020 JL (and 2020 Gladiator) that points technicians to a diagnosis and repair video for the steering linkage and damper.

Check any VIN for open recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls ↗, or see the recall tracker.

What to bring when you look at a Jeep Wrangler JL Unlimited

Six inexpensive tools that catch most of the problems above before you hand over the money. Or book a specialist: see how to get a pre-purchase inspection.

  • Bluetooth OBD-II scannerReads stored and pending codes, and shows the readiness monitors. If several say "not ready", someone may have cleared the codes just before you arrived.
    Amazon ↗
  • Paint thickness gaugeThick readings on one panel mean body filler or a respray, which usually means an accident or rust repair the seller hasn’t mentioned.
    Amazon ↗
  • Inspection borescope cameraFits through drain holes into frame rails, rockers and sills, where these trucks rust from the inside out.
    Amazon ↗
  • Bright LED flashlightFor the underside: leaks at the axle seals, transfer case and rear main, and the frame behind the rear wheels.
    Amazon ↗
  • Telescoping inspection mirrorShows the back of the engine, the top of the fuel tank and the inner fenders without crawling under.
    Amazon ↗
  • Compression testerOn a high-mileage or long-parked engine, uneven compression is the cheapest way to spot a tired engine before you pay for it. Diesels need a diesel-rated tester.
