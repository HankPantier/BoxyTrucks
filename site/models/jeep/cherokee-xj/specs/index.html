Fluids, capacities & service intervals

XJs are cheap, so most have been run on a budget. Assume nothing has been done. The figures below come from a NAXJA member's fluid-capacity summary (posted in a thread titled '2001 owner's manual fluids', though the post doesn't say which year each figure applies to), with ExtremeTerrain's XJ service guide where noted. The two disagree on AW4 fluid, coolant capacity, transfer-case fill and manual-gearbox oil grade, so check the label on your truck or the manual for your year.
