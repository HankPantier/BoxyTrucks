Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume a used Gladiator has had oil changes and nothing else. Run the VIN through NHTSA's recall lookup first, then do the gear oils, transfer case and brake fluid, which owners most often skip. Fluid types below come from Mopar's specifications as compiled on JeepGladiatorForum and a 2021 owner's-manual excerpt posted there; engine capacities come from Stellantis's 2020 specification sheet. The EcoDiesel and Pentastar use different oils and filters.
