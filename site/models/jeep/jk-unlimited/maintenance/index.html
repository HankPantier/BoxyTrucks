Maintenance schedule · 2007–2018

Jeep Wrangler JK Unlimited maintenance schedule

The JK had two gasoline engines, and the schedule changed with them. The 2007–2011 3.8 V6 trucks run Jeep's two-column schedule: Schedule A for normal driving and Schedule B for severe use, which most Jeeps see. The 2012–2018 3.6 Pentastar trucks change oil when the dash says so, with a hard limit. Owners who go off-road should treat the severe column as their schedule, whatever the year.

Service schedule

From Jeep's 2010 Wrangler owner's manual (3.8 V6) and 2012 Wrangler owner's manual (3.6). Later years are similar; check your book.

WHENWHAT TO DO
2007–2011 (3.8): every 6,000 mi / 6 months
  • Engine oil and filter
  • Rotate tires
2012–2018 (3.6): when the oil-change indicator comes on
  • Engine oil and filter; never more than 8,000 mi or 6 months
  • Change it sooner after extended off-road driving
Every oil change
  • Inspect the exhaust and brake hoses
  • Check coolant level, hoses and clamps
  • After off-road driving, inspect the underside and check fasteners for looseness
Every 30,000 mi (3.8)
  • Inspect the engine air filter
  • Inspect and top up the manual transmission and transfer case fluid
60,000 mi or 60 months (3.8)
  • Flush and replace the coolant (again at 102,000, 120,000 and 150,000 if not done)
  • Inspect the drive belt and PCV valve
64,000 mi (3.6)
  • Replace the engine air filter and cabin filter
  • Inspect the brake linings, front suspension, tie rod ends and boots
72,000 mi (3.6)
  • Inspect the CV joints, exhaust, and front and rear axle fluid
96,000 mi (3.8)
  • Spark plugs and ignition cables
104,000 mi or 60 months (3.6)
  • Flush and replace the coolant
120,000 mi (3.8)
  • Drain and refill the transfer case

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Jeep's Schedule B for the 3.8 (towing, off-road, dust, short trips): oil and filter every 3,000 miles or 3 months; drain and refill the front and rear axles every 15,000 miles; inspect the transfer case and air filter every 15,000; automatic transmission fluid and filters at 60,000 and 120,000; spark plugs at 99,000. The 2012 book changes the manual transmission fluid at 64,000 for off-road, desert, towing or plowing use, and the automatic and transfer case fluid for towing or fleet use. Clean and lube the soft-top zippers every 3,000 miles.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover

ITEMWHENWHY
Pentastar oil filter housing (2012–2018)Look into the valley with a flashlight at every oil change80/20 Automotive: the plastic housing warps, cracks or leaks at its O-rings, and oil pools out of sight.
Clockspring (2007–2010)Check the VIN for recall 16V-290 nowNHTSA: dust in the clockspring can disable the driver's airbag.
Front endEvery oil change and after every lift installTrack bar, tie rods, ball joints and bearings cause death wobble.
Front axle tubes and C'sOnce a year on trucks with 35s or bigger4 Wheel Parts: the JK's front tubes and C's are thin and known to bend.
