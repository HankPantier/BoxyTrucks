Fluids, capacities & service intervals

A Comanche is at least 34 years old, and most have spent years as cheap work trucks, so assume nothing has been done. The drivetrain is the XJ's, so the capacities below come from the 2001 Cherokee owner's manual as quoted on NAXJA, for the parts the two share. Check your own truck's manual and the axle tags, especially for Dana 44 and Metric Ton rear axles.
