Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume a 30-year-old YJ has had whatever was cheapest. Change every fluid, chase the vacuum system and grounds, and know which drivetrain you have: the carbureted 4.2 (1987–90) and the injected 4.0 (1991–95) need different tune-up parts, and the 1987–89 six may still have the Peugeot gearbox.
