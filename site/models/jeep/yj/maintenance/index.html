Maintenance schedule · 1987–1995

Jeep Wrangler YJ maintenance schedule

Jeep's own YJ maintenance book isn't published online, so the intervals below are the ones in Haynes's YJ manual, which follows the factory schedule. Most YJs are now 30-plus years old and worked hard, so service everything once at purchase. Three YJ habits matter more than any mileage: keep the vacuum disconnect healthy, keep the grounds clean and keep rust out of the frame and floors.

Service schedule

From Haynes's Jeep Wrangler 1987–2017 manual as listed in our buyer's baseline. Not the Jeep factory book; check your owner's manual if you have it.

WHENWHAT TO DO
Every 7,500 mi / 12 months (3,000 mi in severe use)
  • Replace engine oil and filter
  • Grease the chassis, U-joints and spring bushings
Every 12,000 mi (severe use)
  • Automatic transmission fluid and filter
Every 30,000 mi / 24 months
  • Replace the air filter, PCV valve and fuel filter
  • Replace spark plugs, plus cap and rotor
  • Replace coolant
  • Replace manual gearbox, automatic transmission, transfer case and both differential oils
About every 2 years
  • Replace brake and clutch hydraulic fluid
After deep water crossings
  • Check and change the differential oils (ExtremeTerrain)

Severe service: towing, dust, water, short trips

Haynes drops the oil interval to 3,000 miles and the automatic fluid to 12,000 miles in severe use: towing, short trips, dust, mud and off-road. Change the air filter sooner on dusty trails.

Age items the schedule doesn't cover

ITEMWHENWHY
Vacuum front-axle disconnectAt purchase and whenever 4WD is slow to engageQuadratec: a maze of small vacuum lines decides whether 4WD works, and they dry-rot.
Electrical groundsAt purchaseFirewall ground straps and battery terminals cause flickering lights and jumpy gauges; Quadratec calls it a ten-minute fix with sandpaper.
Frame, spring hangers and floorsOnce a yearQuadratec: YJs are notorious for rust at the rear spring mounts, frame rails, crossmembers and the driver's floor.
Clutch slave cylinder (early YJs)Look for fluid under the bellhousingIt's inside the bellhousing, so the gearbox has to come out.
Front endEvery serviceTrack bar, tie rods and ball joints wear and cause death wobble.
