Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Scramblers are rare enough that most have been half-restored at least once, so trust nothing. Change every fluid, grease every fitting, check the ignition and carburetor, and look at the AMC 20 hubs. Capacities below are from ExtremeTerrain's CJ chart, which covers the 1981–86 drivetrains.
