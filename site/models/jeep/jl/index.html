PART 01

Overview

Where it came from, why it matters, and how it changed year to year.

The story

Jeep unveiled the JL at the Los Angeles Auto Show on November 29, 2017, and the short two-door, the purest Wrangler, followed the four-door into production at Toledo in early 2018. On paper it changed little: still body-on-frame, still solid axles front and rear, still doors and a windshield that come off. In practice it was a thorough rethink. Wikipedia puts it about 90 kg (198 lb) lighter than the JK, with a lower beltline, bigger windows and a grille whose seven slots lean back like the TJ's.

WHAT CHANGED UNDERNEATH

The 3.6 Pentastar carried over at 285 hp and 260 lb-ft, now with an optional eTorque mild-hybrid system. It drove through either a six-speed manual or the 850RE eight-speed automatic. A 2.0-liter turbo four with 270 hp and 295 lb-ft arrived later as an eight-speed-only option. Steering stayed recirculating-ball, now with electric pump assistance, and three 4WD systems were offered: part-time Command-Trac, full-time Selec-Trac and the Rubicon's Rock-Trac with 4:1 low range.

The axles were new Dana designs. Way of Life's breakdown lists an M186 front and M200 rear (the familiar 'Dana 30 and Dana 35' sizes) on the Sport and Sahara, an optional M220 rear bundled with the limited slip, and wider M210/M220 Dana 44s with electric lockers on the Rubicon. Quadratec's 2020 specs give the two-door Rubicon 10.8 inches of ground clearance on a 96.8-inch wheelbase, and a 1,000-lb payload.

A ROUGH START

Early JLs had real problems. Over 18,000 2018–2019 Wranglers were recalled for a poorly welded track-bar bracket, and NHTSA then opened an investigation covering about 270,000 JLs over frame-weld and steering complaints. In August 2019 FCA started fitting a stronger steering damper free of charge, after finding that the original could take in air in the cold and lose damping. Manual trucks were recalled because the clutch pressure plate could overheat, and Jeep later switched the early aluminum steering boxes to cast iron.

None of that stopped the JL from winning the 2019 Four Wheeler SUV of the Year as a Rubicon, along with Motor Trend's SUV of the Year. It even starred in a Super Bowl LII ad, with Jeff Goldblum recreating his Jurassic Park chase in a silver two-door Rubicon.

THE 2024 REFRESH

For 2024 Jeep gave the JL a mid-cycle update rather than a replacement. Every Wrangler got a 12.3-inch Uconnect 5 screen, side-curtain airbags for both rows, a slimmer grille with blacked-out slot frames, and an antenna built into the windshield frame so it stops catching branches. Hagerty lists the rest: a factory 8,000-lb Warn winch option, power seats sealed for fording, a full-floating Dana 44 HD rear axle for the Rubicon, 33-inch tires and a rear locker for the Willys, and a new Rubicon X with 35s.

The refreshed two-door still faces the same rivals as before, and more of them: the Ford Bronco, and further up the price list the Defender 90 and the G-Class. None of them has removable doors, a fold-down windshield and solid axles at both ends.

SIDEBAR · THE BLM'S RUBICONS

Government-issue two-doors

The photos on this page show a white two-door JL Rubicon wearing Bureau of Land Management door logos in California's Johnson Valley OHV Area. The BLM published the pictures into the public domain, which is why we can show them.

It's a fitting job for the short JL. Its short wheelbase suits tight desert washes, and like every Rubicon it has lockers front and rear and a disconnecting sway bar.

How it’s built, what it can do, and what fits under it.

How it’s built

The JL keeps a separate ladder frame, coil springs and solid axles at both ends, with a recirculating-ball steering box powered by an electrically driven hydraulic pump. The two-door rides on a 96.8-inch wheelbase. The engines are the 3.6 Pentastar V6, with an optional eTorque belt starter-generator, and a 2.0-liter turbo four.

What to check before you buy

  1. 01
    Run the VIN for recalls before anything elseJL recalls include the 2018–2019 track-bar bracket weld (18V-675), the manual-clutch pressure plate (20V-124, 21V-028 and 23V-116), the 2018 transmission park-lock rod (18V-280) and cruise-control software (18V-332) campaigns, and the 2021–2025 power-steering pump wiring fire risk (26V-363). Make sure they've all been done.
  2. 02
    Feel the steering at highway speedVague, light or wandering steering on a 2018–2020 truck points to the early aluminum steering box (owners cite TSB 08-074-20). A shake after bumps points to the damper, track bar or worn front-end parts.
  3. 03
    Look at the track-bar weldsExtremeTerrain warns 2018 owners to be 'mindful of the track bar to body weld.' Check both track-bar brackets for cracks and the frame welds for poor penetration, which is what owners complained about to NHTSA.
  4. 04
    Manual trucks: clutch historyAsk whether the clutch recall was done, test for slip in a high gear at low revs, and smell for a cooked clutch after a short climb.
  5. 05
    Peek at the valley on a 3.6Oil weeping at the back of the engine, under the intake, means the oil cooler/filter housing is going.
  6. 06
    2.0T: coolant level and plug receiptsCheck the coolant bottle and turbo lines for leaks, and ask for proof of the 30,000-mile spark plug changes the factory schedule calls for.
  7. 07
    eTorque: listen for start-stop faultsPickup Truck + SUV Talk lists early auxiliary-battery failures as a known JL issue, with flickering dash lights and random warnings.
  8. 08
    Decide whether you need the 2024 refreshThe 2024 update brought side-curtain airbags, the 12.3-inch screen and the full-float rear axle option. A 2018–2023 truck with its fixes done is the value buy.

Spotting the real thing

Two-door JLs come with three powertrains and two very different axle packages, and the big-ticket hardware (4xe, 392, EcoDiesel) was four-door only.

  1. ID
    Real RubiconDana M210 (44-class) front and M220 rear with Tru-Lok electronic lockers, Rock-Trac with 4:1 low range, and an electronic sway-bar disconnect. Sport and Sahara have the M186 ('Dana 30') front and M200 ('Dana 35') rear, with an optional limited slip, and Command-Trac with 2.72:1 (Selec-Trac optional on Sahara).
  2. ID
    Factory 35sFactory Xtreme 35 trucks (2.0T two-door Rubicon and Willys from 2024; standard on the 2.0T two-door Rubicon X) have LT315/70R17 BFGoodrich KO2s on 17x8 beadlock-capable wheels, 4.56 gears, a 1.5 in lift and a reinforced swing gate.
  3. ID
    EngineThe 3.6 Pentastar comes with the 6-speed manual or 8-speed auto. The 2.0 turbo is auto-only. There was never a two-door 4xe, 392 or EcoDiesel.
  4. ID
    Pre- or post-facelift2024-on trucks have the 12-inch Uconnect 5 screen with horizontal dash vents. All but Sport and Sport S also get the new grille.
  5. ID
    JL vs JK at a glanceThe JL's headlights cut into the grille, the spare sits lower and the Jeep logo is no longer on the grille.

After you buy it

Baseline a used JL by engine. The 3.6 Pentastar, the 2.0 turbo use different oils, plugs and cooling loops, and the 8-speed takes ZF-spec fluid, not ATF+4. Before spending money, run the VIN through NHTSA's recall lookup: JLs have had recalls for frame welds, clutches, power-steering pumps. Then change the gear oil, which owners skip most.

Fix steering wobble: damper, track bar, then the rest
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 1–2 hours to inspect and fit a damperSHOP COST · Free damper replacement on affected early trucks under FCA's 2019 customer-satisfaction program; otherwise a damper or track bar
SYMPTOMSFront-end shimmy or full death wobble after a bump at 45–65 mph, with the steering wheel shaking side to side.
THE FIXOn 2018–2019 trucks, check the history first. In August 2019 FCA began offering a stronger steering damper at no charge. FCA said its engineers found air could get into the original damper in cold weather, when the oil thickens, so it lost damping. Its Customer Satisfaction Notification V41 covered about 192,000 2018–2019 JLs. Then inspect the front end the same way as on any solid-axle Jeep: have a helper rock the wheel while you watch both ends of the track bar, then check the tie-rod and drag-link ends, ball joints, control-arm bushings and wheel bearings. Replace worn parts, torque everything at ride height, and get an alignment. A damper on its own masks wobble; it doesn't cure it.

Replace a loose aluminum steering box (TSB 08-074-20)
DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · ≈ 2–3 hours plus an alignment checkSHOP COST · JLWranglerForums members report dealer quotes of $1,500+ out of warranty
SYMPTOMSLight, vague, loose steering and wandering on the highway, sometimes with play you can see at the pitman arm. One owner described it as 'super light, vague and loose' at nearly 100,000 miles.
THE FIXEarly JLs used an aluminum steering gear, and Quadratec says Jeep moved to cast-iron boxes. Owners on JLWranglerForums cite TSB 08-074-20 for the swap (we couldn't see the bulletin itself), who also note it won't cure death wobble by itself. DIY: disconnect the battery, mark and remove the pitman arm with a puller, unbolt the intermediate shaft, disconnect the pressure and return lines (catch the fluid), then unbolt the box from the frame. Fit the new box with new bolts, refill and bleed the system with the specified fluid, and center the steering wheel.

Clutch: check the recall, then replace if it slips
DIFFICULTY · 4 / 5TIME · ≈ A long day (transmission out)SHOP COST · Recall work is free; an out-of-warranty clutch kit plus labor
SYMPTOMSSlipping under load, a burning smell, or a clutch-overheat warning after crawling. Recall 20V-124 covered manual Wranglers built from August 23, 2017 to February 13, 2020 because the pressure plate could overheat, fracture and, in the worst case, start a fire.
THE FIXRun the VIN through NHTSA first. TFLcar reported 29,818 Wranglers in the original recall, and later recalls widened it: 21V-028 (2018–2021 manuals) and 23V-116 (2018–2023 manuals), with 24V-572 re-flashing some 2021 trucks already fixed under 23V-116. If the recall work has been done and the clutch is simply worn, support the transmission and transfer case, remove the driveshafts, crossmember and exhaust as needed, and drop the transmission. Replace the clutch disc, pressure plate and release bearing, and check the flywheel. Bleed the hydraulics, and replace the slave cylinder while the transmission is out.
TOOLS & PARTSTransmission jack · alignment tool · torque wrench · brake bleeder · a friend.

Replace a leaking Pentastar oil cooler / filter housing
DIFFICULTY · 3 / 5TIME · ≈ 4–6 hoursSHOP COST · Housing assembly plus gaskets, coolant and oil
SYMPTOMSOil, and sometimes coolant, pooling in the valley under the intake and running down the back of the engine. Pickup Truck + SUV Talk lists cracked Pentastar oil-filter housings and cooler adapters as a known JL issue.
THE FIXThe job follows the JK procedure. Remove the engine cover and intake plumbing, the upper and lower intake manifolds, and the cooler's sensors and bolts. Clean the valley, fit the new housing with new seals, then refill the oil and coolant and check for leaks. Torque the oil filter cap to 18 ft-lb; overtightening is a suspected contributor to cracking.

Fix 2.0T coolant hose leaks
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 1–2 hoursSHOP COST · A Mopar hose and coolant
SYMPTOMSA dropping coolant level, a sweet smell, or crusty residue around the turbo coolant lines or the coolant bottle hoses on a 2.0 turbo. Pickup Truck + SUV Talk lists a TSB for turbo coolant return line leaks.
THE FIXPressure-test the cold system to find the leak. Drain enough coolant to get below the leak, replace the hose and its clips, refill with OAT coolant and bleed the loop. Remember the 2.0T has a separate intercooler loop. Check the dealer history too, since the TSB repair may already have been done.

Replace a front hub and bearing
DIFFICULTY · 2 / 5TIME · ≈ 1–2 hours per sideSHOP COST · A hub assembly per side
SYMPTOMSA speed-related growl, play when you rock the wheel top to bottom, or an ABS fault. Worn bearings are also on every death-wobble checklist.
THE FIXLift the corner, remove the wheel, caliper and rotor, unplug the ABS sensor, remove the axle nut and the hub-to-knuckle bolts, and pull the hub. Clean the knuckle bore, fit the new hub, and torque to the factory specification.

DIFFICULTY · 4 / 5TIME · ≈ A day for both sidesSHOP COST · Ball-joint kit plus an alignment
SYMPTOMSClunks, wander, uneven tire wear, or movement when you rock a lifted wheel top to bottom. It's more common on lifted trucks with heavy tires.
THE FIXRemove the hub, axle shaft and knuckle, press out the old upper and lower joints with a JL-specific adapter set, press in the new ones square, and reassemble. Torque everything to the factory specs and finish with an alignment.

Asked on every forum

Wrangler JL questions, answered

What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

On a Rubicon, 35s. Driving Line ran 35x12.50R17s on a stock-height JL Rubicon with JKS bump-stop spacers and the stock flares. They cleared on level ground but hit the inner fenders when flexed, and the spacers cost about 2 inches of up-travel. On a Sport or Sahara, the realistic answer is a 33 with a wheel change; 35s on those trims usually need bump stops, less backspacing and often a 2–2.5-inch lift. Driving Line's test truck was a four-door, but the two-door uses the same axles and flares.

SOURCE · DRIVING LINE · PERFORMANCE PLUS
3.6 V6 or 2.0 turbo?

The 2.0T makes more torque (295 against 260 lb-ft), and the EPA rated the 2018 two-door 2.0T at 23/25 mpg against 18/23 for the 3.6 automatic (17/25 manual). It's automatic-only, has extra cooling loops and needs spark plugs every 30,000 miles. The Pentastar is the one with a manual option and a long, well-understood service history.

SOURCE · EPA (FUELECONOMY.GOV) · WAY OF LIFE
How much can a two-door JL tow?

Quadratec lists 2,000 lb for every two-door trim, against 3,500 lb for the four-door.

SOURCE · QUADRATEC
What's the payload?

Quadratec's 2020–2021 specs list 1,000 lb for the two-door. Check the door-jamb sticker on the truck in front of you.

SOURCE · QUADRATEC
Which axles do I have?

Sport and Sahara: Dana M186 front and M200 rear, with an M220 rear if the limited slip was ordered. Rubicon: wider M210 front and M220 rear with electric lockers. Way of Life notes the Sahara always has the 186 front.

SOURCE · WAY OF LIFE
Is death wobble still a thing on the JL?

Yes. Any solid front axle can do it. FCA offered early JL owners a stronger steering damper in 2019, and later fixes addressed the track-bar welds and the steering box. A worn or loose track bar, ball joints or tie-rod ends are still the usual cause, and Quadratec warns that a new damper alone only hides it.

SOURCE · QUADRATEC · EXTREMETERRAIN
What oil does the JL Pentastar take?

Five quarts of 0W-20 meeting MS-6395, with the Mopar MO-349 cartridge filter. That's a quart less and a thinner grade than the JK, so don't copy JK numbers. The 2.0T takes 5 quarts of full-synthetic 5W-30 rated API SN PLUS.

SOURCE · QUADRATEC · OWNER'S MANUAL SPEC
What changed with the 2024 refresh?

A 12.3-inch Uconnect 5 screen on every model, first- and second-row side-curtain airbags, a new grille, an antenna built into the windshield frame, an available 8,000-lb Warn winch, power seats, a Dana 44 HD full-float rear axle on the Rubicon, a better-equipped Willys, and a new Rubicon X with 35s.

SOURCE · HAGERTY · THE DRIVE
Which years should I avoid?

2018, mainly. SlashGear counted 11 recalls and more than 1,000 owner complaints for the 2018 Wrangler, most about wandering at highway speed, and says steering complaints carried on, less often, for two more years. NHTSA's 2018 list includes track-bar bracket welds (18V-675, also 2019), the intermediate steering shaft (18V-343) and rear frame-rail corrosion (18V-231), and manual trucks from 2018 on are covered by the clutch recalls (20V-124, 21V-028, 23V-116). Quadratec says Jeep later moved from aluminum to cast-iron steering boxes. No later year is a write-off: buy any JL with its recalls and steering fixes documented.

SOURCE · SLASHGEAR · NHTSA · QUADRATEC
Is the JL safe?

It's better equipped than any earlier Wrangler: side airbags and stronger B-pillars from launch, and side-curtain airbags from 2024. But Euro NCAP gave it 1 star in 2018, and the IIHS rated the four-door Marginal after it tipped over in the small-overlap test.

SOURCE · WIKIPEDIA · HAGERTY
