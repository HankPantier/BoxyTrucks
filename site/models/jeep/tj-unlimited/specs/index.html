Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing has been done. Even a low-mile LJ is now 20 years old, so rubber, coolant and gear oil have aged regardless of mileage. On 2005–06 trucks, put the OPDA at the top of the list. On every LJ, put the frame inspection there too, before you spend a cent on mods.
