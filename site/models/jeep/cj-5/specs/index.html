Fluids, capacities & service intervals

A CJ-5 is at least 40 years old and has probably had several owners, each with their own idea of maintenance. Change every fluid, grease every fitting and sort the ignition before you judge how it runs. Specs vary by era and engine; the capacities below are from ExtremeTerrain's CJ chart, which mostly covers AMC-era drivetrains. Check anything for a Willys or Kaiser truck against its manual.
