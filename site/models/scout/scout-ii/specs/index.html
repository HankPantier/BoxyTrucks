Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing has been done. Scout IIs are simple and almost everything is still available, but they're also 45-plus years old. Start by finding your line ticket, which tells you exactly which engine, axles, ratio and transmission you have. The IH V8s are flat-tappet engines, so choose oil with enough zinc.
