Fluids, capacities & service intervals

Assume nothing has been done unless there are receipts, especially on an import that sat before it crossed an ocean. Specs here are from AvtoVAZ's own owner's manuals (2008 and 2018 editions) and the 2013 service book for the injected 1.7 21214. Capacities for a 1.6 VAZ-2121 or a carbureted 21213 may differ slightly, so check your engine. One more Niva quirk: grease the propshafts and check the front hub bearings far more often than you're used to.
