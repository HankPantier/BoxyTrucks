For getting unstuck

The kit every trail truck should carry and most don't.

  • Recovery traction boardsWedge them under the tires in sand, mud or snow and drive out. The single most-used piece of recovery gear.
    See options on Amazon ↗
  • Soft shackles and a kinetic recovery ropeLighter and safer than steel shackles and a tow strap. Check the rope's rating against the truck's weight.
    See options on Amazon ↗
  • Hitch receiver shackle mountTurns the tow hitch into a proper rated recovery point. Never recover from a tow ball.
    See options on Amazon ↗
  • Tree saver strap and glovesProtects the tree and the winch line. Cheap, and almost nobody buys one for themselves.
    See options on Amazon ↗
  • Folding shovelFor digging out an axle or a buried tire before you reach for the winch.
    See options on Amazon ↗

For airing down (and back up)

Dropping tire pressure is the cheapest capability upgrade there is. Getting it back up is the hard part.

  • Portable 12V air compressorAirs four big tires back up before the pavement. Get one rated for continuous duty.
    See options on Amazon ↗
  • Automatic tire deflatorsScrew on, set the pressure once, and all four stop at the same number.
    See options on Amazon ↗
  • Digital tire pressure gaugeAccurate at low pressures, where cheap stick gauges guess.
    See options on Amazon ↗
  • Tire plug kitFixes a trail puncture in ten minutes. Stocking-stuffer price.
    See options on Amazon ↗

For the garage

These trucks reward owners who do their own work. Our repair guides list the tools for every job.

  • Torque wrenchWheel nuts, axle and suspension bolts all have a torque spec. Guessing is how studs snap.
    See options on Amazon ↗
  • Rechargeable work lightHooks or sticks anywhere under the truck. You'll use it every weekend.
    See options on Amazon ↗
  • Rolling creeperYour back will thank you on the first oil change.
    See options on Amazon ↗
  • Rust-prevention undercoatingOld ladder frames rot from the inside. A yearly coat of a wax or lanolin spray is cheap insurance.
    See options on Amazon ↗
