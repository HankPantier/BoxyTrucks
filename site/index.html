About the site

Questions, answered

ABOUTWhat is BoxyTrucks?

An independent guide to the square-shouldered 4x4s people actually take off-road: where each generation came from, how it's built, what to check before you buy, the repairs owners end up doing, and the suppliers, builders, forums and channels worth knowing. Think Wikipedia meets an overland magazine, written by enthusiasts for hands-on owners.

BROWSE THE MODELS →
COVERAGEWhich trucks do you cover?

Right now: 4Runner, Chevrolet / GMC, Dodge Ramcharger, Ford Bronco, G-Class, Grenadier, Hummer, International Scout, Isuzu Trooper, Jeep, Lada Niva, Land Cruiser, Land Rover, Lexus LX & GX, Mercedes Unimog, Mitsubishi Delica, Mitsubishi Pajero, Nissan Patrol, Pinzgauer, Range Rover, Suzuki, with a full guide for every generation. More are on the list at the bottom of the models page; if yours isn't there yet, tell us.

SEE EVERY GENERATION →
SOURCESWhere does the information come from?

Owner's and factory service manuals, specialist shops, parts suppliers, long-running owner forums and reputable press. Every guide lists its sources at the bottom. Specs vary by year, market and engine, so always confirm fluids, torque figures and part numbers for your VIN before you start.

READ THE DISCLAIMER →
PARTSHow do I find the right parts for my truck?

Nearly every repair, and every item in each guide's “After you buy it” service list, links to the parts you need at suppliers owners trust for that truck, usually with an Amazon search alongside. (Recall checks and a few dealer-only jobs have no parts links.) Use the part numbers, check fitment against your VIN, and when in doubt call the supplier.
