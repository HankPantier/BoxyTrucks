Asked on every forum

The big questions, answered

MERCEDES-BENZ G 500What’s the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

A 275/70R18 — roughly a 33-inch tire — on the factory 18-inch wheels. Owners on Expedition Portal report it fits an unlifted G 500; one saw rubbing only on aftermarket ET63-offset wheels. A 285/65R18 can rub slightly at full left lock. Re-code the tire size with a Mercedes diagnostic tool so the speedometer and ESP stay accurate.

TOYOTA LAND CRUISER 80 SERIESWhat's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

The safe answer from IH8MUD is 285/75R16, about 33 inches, which fits a stock 80 without drama. Owners also report 305/70R16 and 295/75R16 fitting, though wheel width and offset matter as much as the tire size. 35s 'fit, but rub like mad' on a stock truck. Christo Slee's advice is that 35s work with a 2-inch Old Man Emu lift, but you'll probably rub at full flex unless you lower the bump stops. Stock size is 275/70R16.

LEXUS GX 470What's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

The stock size is 265/65R17. On a stock or nearly stock GX 470, the safe consensus is about 31.5 inches. Owners report 265/70R17 working with little or no rubbing (one reports a slight rub at full lock in reverse), and 255/75R17 with zero rubbing. Going to 33 inches (285/70R17, or the narrower 255/80R17) generally means a 2–3 in lift and some liner trimming.

MORE LEXUS GX 470 QUESTIONS →
TOYOTA 4RUNNER 3RD GENWhat's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

The Toyota-4Runner.org consensus is 265/75R16 (roughly a 31.6-inch tire) on stock suspension. Some owners see rubbing at full compression off-road with wide, aggressive tread or wheel spacers; if you want zero drama, 265/70R16 or 265/70R17 are the conservative picks. Anything bigger is lift territory.

MORE TOYOTA 4RUNNER 3RD GEN QUESTIONS →
LAND ROVER 90 / 110 / DEFENDERWhat's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

235/85R16 is the classic factory size on 110s and the 'sweet spot' most owners recommend; NAS D90s came on 265/75R16. 255/85R16 (about 33") is the popular upgrade and generally fits at stock height, but owners report rubbing on the front radius arms at full lock (fix by winding out the steering stops), against the 45 L auxiliary tank on 110s, and close to the spring brackets at full compression on 90s.

MORE LAND ROVER 90 / 110 / DEFENDER QUESTIONS →
LAND ROVER RANGE ROVER CLASSICWhat's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

The usual forum answer is 245/75R16 (about 30.5 inches) on stock coils, with the steering stops adjusted to stop rubbing at full lock. Some owners run 265/75R16 with slight chassis rub fixed by the stops. 235/85R16 generally needs a lift or some trimming on these 100-inch trucks.

MORE LAND ROVER RANGE ROVER CLASSIC QUESTIONS →
INEOS GRENADIER STATION WAGONWhat's the biggest tire I can fit without a lift?

From the factory the Grenadier rides on LT265/70R17 or LT255/70R18 (about 32 in.). INEOS Forum owners report 285/70R17 fits with no problem. 285/75R17 (about 34 in.) is the popular sweet spot: owners report no rub on stock suspension, but you'll probably need a couple of washers under the spare carrier to clear the small rear door. Owners have also run 295/70R17, 255/85R17 and 35x11.5R17 (the 35s on aftermarket 17x8 +40 mm wheels) without a lift. Treat 35s as the outer limit.
