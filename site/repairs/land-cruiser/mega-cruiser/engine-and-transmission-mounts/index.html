Engine and transmission mounts

DIFFICULTY 2 / 5TIME Half a dayCOST Parts plus labor

Symptoms

Vibration or clunking on take-off. One BaT truck had both its motor mounts and front mounts replaced.

The fix, step by step

  1. Support the engine, unbolt and replace the mounts, then check the exhaust and driveshaft clearances.

Before you start

Rated for a competent home mechanic with a decent tool kit. Confirm torque figures, fluids and procedures against the factory service manual for your VIN, support the truck on stands rather than a jack, and work at your own risk (disclaimer).
