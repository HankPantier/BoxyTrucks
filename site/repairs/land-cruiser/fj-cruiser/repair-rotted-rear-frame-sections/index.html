Repair rotted rear frame sections

DIFFICULTY 5 / 5TIME ≈ A weekend or more, with weldingCOST Rust Buster lists its rear rail kit at about $180 per side; welding labor extra

Symptoms

Flaking, swollen or holed frame rails, rear control-arm brackets and crossmembers, often hidden under fresh undercoating on salt-belt trucks.

The fix, step by step

  1. Probe the rear frame, the lower-link brackets, the crossmembers and the coil-spring buckets with a hammer and pick.
  2. Wire-brush and treat surface rust; anything rotted through has to be cut out and replaced.
  3. Rust Buster makes FJ-specific caps for the last 28.75 inches of the rails and for the center rail section, plus crossmembers, coil buckets and body mounts shared with the 4Runner.
  4. All of them need welding.
  5. One IH8MUD owner replaced his rear lower-link frame brackets with Rust Buster pieces and added weld-on reinforcement.

Tools & part numbers

  • MIG welder
  • grinder and cutoff wheels
  • jack stands
  • rust converter
  • Rust Buster RB8432 (rear rail), RB8433 (center rail) and RB8427 (rear control-arm crossmember) as needed.
