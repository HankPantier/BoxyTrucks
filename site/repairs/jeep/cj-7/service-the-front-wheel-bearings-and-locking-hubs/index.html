Repair guides/Jeep CJ-7/Service the front wheel bearings and locking hubs
DIY repair · Jeep CJ-7

Service the front wheel bearings and locking hubs

DIFFICULTY 2 / 5TIME ≈ 2–3 hours for both sidesCOST Bearings, races and seals [price not sourced]

Symptoms

Play at the top and bottom of the tire, growling that changes when you turn, grease flung inside the rim, or hubs that won't engage. The stock five-bolt Warn hubs on 1981–86 CJs are weak, and Quadra-Trac trucks have drive plates rather than hubs.

Before you start

  • 1976–86 CJs with the Dana 30 have serviceable tapered front wheel bearings held by an inner adjusting nut, a tab washer and an outer locknut. Newer Jeeps mostly use sealed hub units instead.
  • You need a spindle-nut socket (2-1/16 in). Older sockets have side slots; later ones use four prongs. Medium snap-ring pliers come off the hub.
  • Seals come in two sizes. The 2.313-in (2-5/16) spindle seal fits 1977–86 CJ-7s and 1977–83 CJ-5s; measure the old seal if you're not sure what you have.
  • Inside the locking hub, too much grease is a common reason it won't engage. Owners lube the hub internals lightly with oil instead.
  • If the six spindle bolts come out, the needle bearing inside the spindle is worth replacing while you're there.

The fix, step by step

  1. Remove the hub, the outer nut, lock washer and inner nut, then pull the rotor and catch the outer bearing.
  2. Clean everything, replace bearings and races as pairs, and press the new races in square.
  3. Seals come in two sizes (2 1/8 in and 2 5/16 in), so measure the old one first.
  4. Pack the bearings fully and tighten the inner nut to 50 ft-lb while turning the rotor to seat them, as the manuals quoted in the Off-Road.com thread say.
  5. Then back it off (one owner backs off about 1/6 turn), fit the lock washer and torque the outer locknut to 50 ft-lb and bend the lock-washer lip over it (1981 Jeep factory service manual; the inner nut is set the same way there, 50 ft-lb then back off 1/6 turn), and check end play: the manuals quoted there specify 0.001–0.005 in with a dial indicator.
  6. If the six spindle bolts come out, replace the needle bearing inside the spindle too.

Common mistakes

  • Packing the locking hub full of grease, which stops it engaging.
  • Putting thread locker on the spindle nuts; Loctite only goes on the spindle bolts.
  • Reusing a spindle locknut that drags or feels like it's stripping as it goes on.
  • Mixing up inner and outer races and bearings instead of keeping each matched pair together.
  • Leaving rust or scoring on the spindle, which ruins the new seal.

What it costs

Bearing and seal kits are cheap. A front hub seal for 1977–86 CJ-7s lists at about $5 at Somar Motor, and complete bearing-and-seal kits from Crown and Yukon run in the tens of dollars per side at Quadratec. A spindle-nut socket is about $10. Expect a full weekend per side on a seized, neglected axle.
