Carter BBD rough idle (258)

DIFFICULTY 2 / 5TIME ≈ 1–2 hoursCOST A can of carb cleaner

Symptoms

Stumbling or stalling at idle and poor economy, but fine at higher rpm. Offroaders.com says the cause is almost always clogged idle tubes. It also says post-1981 feedback carbs with a stepper motor are 'a bit more troublesome.'

The fix, step by step

  1. With the engine running and choke open, look for fuel dripping from the idle nozzles.
  2. Tag the hoses, then remove the air cleaner, choke plate and rod, and the venturi cluster (two screws, two small gaskets).
  3. Check that the idle pickup tubes are seated, clear the tubes and nozzles with fine wire and carb cleaner without losing the check ball, and reassemble.
  4. Tighten the carb body screws.
  5. Drilling the idle tubes to 0.032 in reduces clogging.
  6. If it still idles badly, check for vacuum and manifold leaks.

Tools & part numbers

  • 3/16 in socket
  • small screwdrivers
  • carb cleaner
  • fine wire
  • 0.032 in drill bit
