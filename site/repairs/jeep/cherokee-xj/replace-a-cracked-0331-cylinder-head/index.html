Replace a cracked 0331 cylinder head (2000–01)

DIFFICULTY 4 / 5TIME ≈ A weekendCOST Parts plus labor

Symptoms

Unexplained coolant loss, brown or dirty coolant, milky residue under the oil cap, and often a recent overheat. Wikipedia says the crack sits between cylinders 3 and 4 and often shows as a tan line with the valve cover off.

The fix, step by step

  1. Confirm with a cooling-system pressure test and by looking through the oil filler (no 'TUPY' mark means an original 0331).
  2. Drain the coolant and remove the intake and exhaust manifolds, the coil rail, the valve cover and the rocker assemblies, keeping pushrods in order.
  3. Remove the head bolts and lift the head off.
  4. Change the oil and filter, then flush the oil again after the first short run to get coolant out.
  5. Fit a revised or aftermarket head; never another 0331, and confirm the casting with the seller before you buy (Wikipedia notes even 'TUPY' heads occasionally crack).
  6. Use a new gasket and head bolts, torque in sequence to the service-manual spec, then refill and bleed the cooling system.
  7. Fix the cooling problem that probably caused the crack.

Tools & part numbers

  • Torque wrench
  • head bolt sequence and spec from FSM confirm in manual
  • gasket scraper
  • pushrod organizer
  • coolant pressure tester

Rated for a competent home mechanic with a decent tool kit. Confirm torque figures, fluids and procedures against the factory service manual for your VIN, support the truck on stands rather than a jack, and work at your own risk (disclaimer).
