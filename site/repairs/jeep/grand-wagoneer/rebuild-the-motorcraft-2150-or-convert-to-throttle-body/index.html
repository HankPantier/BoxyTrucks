Rebuild the Motorcraft 2150 or convert to throttle-body injection

DIFFICULTY 2 / 5 (rebuild) · 3 / 5 (TBI)TIME ≈ An afternoon (rebuild) · A weekend (TBI)COST TBI conversion about $2,000 in parts plus up to $1,000 labor (Hagerty)

Symptoms

Hesitation, stumbling or stalling cold or hot, hard hot starts, fuel smell, poor idle. Hagerty's specialist blames the carburetor for about 40% of owner headaches.

The fix, step by step

  1. Rule out vacuum leaks and ignition faults first: check every vacuum line and the distributor pickup and module.
  2. To rebuild, remove the carb, strip it on a clean bench, replace the base gasket, power valve and internal gaskets from a 2150 kit, set the float and choke pull-off to the kit's specs, and refit on a new base gasket and insulator.
  3. Converting to TBI means a throttle body with its own fuel pump, return line, sensors and harness.
  4. Most owners who keep the truck do it eventually, and it's the core of the Wagonmaster recipe.

Tools & part numbers

  • Carb rebuild kit with specs
  • gasket scraper
  • carb cleaner
  • vacuum gauge
  • timing light
  • for TBI: fuel-line tools, wiring tools, laptop or handheld tuner per kit
