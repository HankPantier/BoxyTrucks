DIY repair · International Scout 80 / 800

Springs, shackles and bushings (fix the sag and the ride)

DIFFICULTY 3 / 5TIME A weekendCOST Kit plus labor

Symptoms

A tired stance, clunks from worn spring eyes and shackles, and the choppy ride of forward-shackle front springs.

The fix, step by step

  1. Replace the springs, shackles, bushings, U-bolts and shocks as a set.
  2. IH Parts America says its CPT system for 1961–71 Scouts nets 1 to 3 inches of lift, about 2 inches on most trucks.
  3. It includes heavy-duty springs, 4-inch shackles, 1/2-inch U-bolts, 2-degree caster shims, Bilstein shocks and an extended rear brake hose.
  4. You may need to notch around the front driveshaft and transmission crossmember and enlarge the U-bolt plate holes.
  5. For a better ride, CPT's reverse-shackle kit moves the front shackle.
  6. It needs welding and drilling through the frame, and a longer-travel front driveshaft is recommended.

Tools & part numbers

  • Jacks and stands
  • penetrating oil and heat for seized bolts
  • torque wrench
  • drill (and welder for a reverse-shackle kit).
