DIY repair · Land Rover Discovery Series II

SLS air springs, or convert to coils

DIFFICULTY 2 / 5TIME ≈ 2–3 hoursCOST A pair of air springs, or a coil conversion kit

Symptoms

The rear sags overnight or when parked, then pumps back up; the compressor runs for long periods. Ignored leaks burn out the compressor.

The fix, step by step

  1. Chock the front wheels, support the chassis by the rear radius-arm mounts and the axle on jacks, and remove the wheels.
  2. Open the compressor box on the chassis rail and slowly crack the two outer air unions (not the middle one) to deflate.
  3. Pop the retaining clips, collapse the spring, undo its air union, rotate it 90 degrees and lift it out.
  4. Replace both sides.
  5. To convert instead, fit Discovery 2 (not Discovery 1) spring seats, coils and shocks, and have the SLS switched off with a diagnostic tool.

Tools & part numbers

  • Trolley jack, axle stands, 10 mm spanner, spring compressor for coils
  • air springs and clips, or a conversion kit
