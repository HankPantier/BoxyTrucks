DIY repair · Land Rover Range Rover Classic

Front radius arm bushes (chassis end)

DIFFICULTY 2 / 5TIME VariesCOST Terrafirma poly kit about $66

Symptoms

Vague or wandering steering, clunks over bumps, and cracked or perished rubber where the radius arms meet the chassis.

The fix, step by step

  1. Support the chassis on stands, take the weight off the front axle, unbolt the radius arm at the chassis mount and swap in the new bushes.

Tools & part numbers

  • Jack and axle stands
  • large sockets
  • pry bar
  • Terrafirma TF1003 (replaces NRC4514; also fits Defender and Discovery 1)

Rated for a competent home mechanic with a decent tool kit.
