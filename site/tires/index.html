Toyota Land Cruiser · Land Cruiser 80 Series · 1990–1997

Biggest tire at stock height

285/75R16 (≈33 in)

IH8MUD's safe answer. Owners also report 305/70R16 and 295/75R16 fitting, depending on wheel width and offset; 35s rub badly on a stock truck.
