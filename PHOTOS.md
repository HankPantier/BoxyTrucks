# Hank's own photos

Hank drops his own photos into `photos/` in the BoxyTrucks folder on his Mac (`/Users/webhank/LocalSites/BoxyTrucks/photos`, reachable through the device bridge). Started Nov 5, 2026. Hank said to use them wherever they fit and to adjust them as needed.

## Process for new photos
1. List the folder (device_list_dir) and compare against the log below. Stage only new files.
2. Look at each one. Blur any readable licence plate. Never use a photo of a wreck or breakdown.
3. Export for the site: strip EXIF (removes GPS), convert to sRGB, longest side 2400px, JPEG quality ~84, saved as `site/img/<family>/hank-<truck>-<what>.jpg`. The build makes the responsive variants.
4. Credit: `Hank · BoxyTrucks`, source `BoxyTrucks`, caption starting "HANK'S …". The model-page rule is off-road only, but Hank's own trucks on gravel, trail or woods settings are fine.
5. Where they go, best first: the weekly "From Hank's garage" post; the hero of that truck's guide (replacing a street or stand-in photo); the owner-note figure (`OWNED_PHOTO` in build.py); the About page; Field Notes about that truck.
6. Log each file below.
