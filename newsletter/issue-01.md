## New on the site: the three questions every buyer asks

Before you buy a boxy 4x4 you want to know three things: which years to skip, what breaks, and how big a tire fits. Every generation we cover now has a page for each one.

- **Years to avoid.** Every model year with a verdict, what changed and which recalls to check. Example: on the [5th Gen 4Runner](https://boxytrucks.com/models/4runner/5th-gen/years/) there's no bad engine year, but confirm the Takata airbag (2010–16) and fuel pump (2014–15, 2018–19) recalls are closed.
- **Common problems.** The things owners and specialists flag most, with the repair and the cost. Example: on an [80 Series](https://boxytrucks.com/models/land-cruiser/80-series/problems/), pull the front knuckle fill plug; runny grease means the inner axle seals went a long time ago.
- **Biggest tire.** What fits at stock height and at each common lift. Example: a [5th Gen 4Runner](https://boxytrucks.com/models/4runner/5th-gen/tires/) takes a true 32 in tire stock; a 285/70R17 may need a body-mount chop.

Shopping and not sure what you want? The new [Boxy SUVs list](https://boxytrucks.com/best/boxy-suvs/) puts every boxy 4x4 you can buy in one table, new, used and classic, with prices and which ones still have a solid front axle.

## From my garage: the 2007 G500

My G500's biggest issue so far has been the classic one: the oil filter housing / oil cooler gasket leak. It's a simple, inexpensive fix that just takes time. [This video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2MI3UQ5MWOw) is the walkthrough I'd use.

The lockers are my current project. Most likely the system needs bleeding or the lines need replacing. My 80 Series had a locker problem too, a leaking vacuum seal. Both trucks had the same root cause: lockers that weren't used often enough. So here's my tip of the month: **engage your lockers every six months or so**, on almost any boxy truck.

[Every G500 problem worth checking →](https://boxytrucks.com/models/g-class/g500/problems/)

## Recall worth knowing: 2024–27 Land Cruiser fuel tube

Toyota has a recall on 2024–2027 Land Cruisers (the 250) for a fuel-tube connector that can disconnect and leak (NHTSA 26V673000, Toyota 26TA23). If you own one, or you're shopping a used one, check the VIN at [nhtsa.gov/recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) or in the Toyota app. [More on the 250 →](https://boxytrucks.com/models/land-cruiser/250-series/)
