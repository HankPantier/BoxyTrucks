How the card works

Your card sits in the sidebar of the pages for the trucks you cover, above the table of contents, with your name, a line about what you do best, and a link straight to your store. It is labelled “Sponsor” so readers know what it is, and it links with the proper rel="sponsored" attribute so it’s clean with Google. Month to month, cancel any time.

What sponsorship doesn’t buy

Sponsors never change what a guide says. Best years, the problems to check for and the repair steps come from owners, specialists and the factory, with sources listed on every page. We keep linking every reputable supplier in the parts lists, sponsor or not, and we don’t remove a competitor’s links. If a sponsor’s product disappoints in an install guide, we say so. That independence is why readers trust the recommendation, and it’s what you’re paying to stand next to.
