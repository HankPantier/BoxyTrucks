Who it reaches

Owners and serious shoppers in the middle of a decision: which year to buy, what breaks, what tire fits a stock truck, and where to buy the part. Repair and tire pages are where readers click through to suppliers.

Ways to work together

  • Product in use. Your part or gear fitted and shown on a real truck in a Short or Reel, with a link in the description and a pinned comment. Clearly labelled as a paid partnership.
  • Sponsored fitment or buying video. A tire, wheel or gear brand attached to a "biggest tire on a stock…" or "5 things to check" video for the trucks your product fits.
  • Site sponsorship. A sponsor card on the guides and repair pages for the trucks you cover. See packages and prices.

Sponsors never change what a guide says, and every paid post is disclosed. See what sponsorship doesn't buy.
