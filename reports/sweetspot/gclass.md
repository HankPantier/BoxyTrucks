# Sweet spot / Beware audit: group "gclass"

Pages: g-class (4x4-squared, 500ge, europa, g500, g55-amg, g550, g63-6x6, g63-amg, second-gen, w460, w461, w463), grenadier (quartermaster, station-wagon), hummer (h1).

Rule I applied: `buy` only where a buyer's guide, a specialist or owner consensus actually recommends those years. `avoid` only for documented problems. A recall that has a free remedy is a "check the VIN" note, not Beware. Flags that were set only because "something changed that year" are now `note`.

## Key sources (all fetched)
- Handsome Motors (G specialist dealer), G-Wagon buyer's guide: https://handsomemotors.com/g-wagon-buyers-guide. It says "Early trucks (02-04) suffer from electrical gremlins which include seat motor/control issues, window regulator/motor issues, and door lock actuators. These issues improved every year and by 05-06 were mostly eliminated. Transfer case motor/actuators were also an issue." It also says "07-08 did receive the M113 5.0 V8 mated to the 7 Speed", and "for ultimate reliability the 2002-2015 will reign supreme HOWEVER [2016–18] is still a reliable option".
- CarGurus (Chris Teague, Feb 2026), G-Class buying guide: https://cargurus.com/Cars/articles/mercedes-benz-g-class-buying-guide. It says "2002 through 2005 versions seem to generate the most complaints, mostly concerning electrical issues", that "2009 and newer versions tend to have fewer problems", and that "the very last 1999-2000 G500s are considered very reliable".
- Expedition Portal threads:
  - "reliability question": https://forum.expeditionportal.com/goto/post?id=1629041. Members say 2002–03 have "a long list of issues", "04 on up should be fine", and that 2005 is the best year because it's the last five-speed with fewer electrical issues.
  - "Starting a G Wagon Search": https://forum.expeditionportal.com/goto/post?id=1482523. Many people advise avoiding 2002–03 for electrics; Mercedes changed some electronics partway through 2003.
  - "Buying a 04 G 500 w/ 150k Miles": https://forum.expeditionportal.com/goto/post?id=2156701. An owner calls the electrical fault "a common problem on early MBUSA G500".
  - "Considering a G wagen": https://forum.expeditionportal.com/goto/post?id=1325041. One member recommends 2004–05 for fewer electrical issues; another prefers a 2000 for its manual transfer-case lever.
  - "WTB G-Wagen 2005+": https://forum.expeditionportal.com/goto/post?id=1892293. An owner says pre-2002 window regulators are "far more robust".
- Flash Off-Road (H1 specialist), Buying a Used Hummer: http://www.flashoffroad.com/features/buyingTrucks/buyingUsed.html. Also its cracked-block page: http://www.flashoffroad.com/Diesel/No8Cyl/CrackedBlock.htm
- Autotrader (Feb 2026), used Hummer guide: https://www.autotrader.com/car-shopping/buying-used-hummer-everything-you-need-know-238962
- Hagerty (Jul 2021), H1 values: https://hagerty.com/media/?p=158872
- 2026 Grenadier reviews:
  - Cars.com (May 2026): https://cars.com/articles/the-updated-2026-ineos-grenadier-eliminates-deal-breakers-for-your-everyday-expeditions-review-522961/
  - GearJunkie: https://gearjunkie.com/motors/2026-ineos-grenadier-review
  - Parkers: https://www.parkers.co.uk/ineos/grenadier/review/
- Checked but not used: CarComplaints' G550 page lists 2017 as the "worst" year, but that rests on only 4 complaints, so it's no basis for a Beware flag. Reddit r/AMG G63 thread: no consensus. Euro Premium Parts M157 guide: no year-specific advice. REREV years-to-avoid: an aggregator that agrees on 2002–04 bad / 2005–06 good; not cited.

## g-class/g500 (the known bad case): FIXED
| Year | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2001–02 | **buy** | note | Wrong. This was the ESP/4ETS update and the MBUSA launch. 2002 is the worst year for electrics, not a sweet spot. |
| 2002–04 | (none) | **avoid** (new) | Electrical gremlins: seat motors, window regulators, door-lock actuators, transfer-case actuator (Handsome Motors; Expedition Portal; CarGurus) |
| 2005–08 | (none) | **buy** (new) | Handsome Motors: faults mostly gone by 2005–06. Text explains five-speed 722.6 (2005–06) vs seven-speed 7G-TRONIC (2007–08, plus HID and camera). |

- New FAQ "Which years are the best to buy?", placed right after the five-speed/seven-speed FAQ and pointing to it. It covers the 2002–04 electrics, the mid-2003 electronics change, CarGurus' harsher view (2002–05 complaints, and that it prefers the 2009-on G 550), the five-speed vs seven-speed choice within 2005–08 (one owner prefers the 722.6 because it's easier to service), and the Europa pre-2002 alternative.
- New buying check "Work every window, lock and seat".
- The "Is the G 500 reliable?" FAQ now mentions the 2002–04 electrics.
- Generations-table note fixed from "switched … during 2007" to "for MY2007 (June 2006 production)", so it matches the timeline and the gearbox FAQ.
- Sources added: Handsome Motors, CarGurus, and four Expedition Portal threads.
