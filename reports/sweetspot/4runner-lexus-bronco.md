# Sweet spot / Beware audit: 4runner-lexus-bronco

Group pages: 4runner (1st–6th gen), lexus (gx460, gx470, gx550, lx450, lx470, lx570, lx600), bronco (bronco-ii, early-bronco, full-size, sixth-gen).

I checked every `buy` and `avoid` flag against buyer's guides, forum consensus and NHTSA. Where the sources didn't agree on a year, I removed the `buy` flag instead of guessing. Every source below was fetched for this audit and has been added to that page's `sources` list (`{"name","url"}`). Any FAQ marked as new or rewritten is now consistent with the flags.

Key: **buy** = Sweet spot, **avoid** = Beware. Changed flags are in bold.

## Toyota 4Runner

### 1st gen (1984–89)
| Year | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1984½ | buy | **note** |
| 1985 | buy | buy (text reworded with the reason) |
| 1988–89 (V6) | — | **avoid (new entry)** |

- **1985:** this is the last solid-axle year and the first with the 22R-E. Hagerty says "purists still search out the early trucks". On Expedition Portal, one member rates carbureted '84s (and some '85s) as less desirable.

- **New V6 entry:** Hagerty quotes Logan Calkins saying most enthusiasts avoid the six and choose the four. The 3VZ-E head-gasket history also starts here.
- **New FAQ:** "Which years are the best to buy?"

### 4th gen (2003–09)
| Year | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 2003–05 | avoid | avoid (text reworded and re-sourced) |
| **2005** | buy | **note** |
| 2006 | buy | buy (reason added) |
| **2008** | buy | **note** |
| **2009** | buy | **note** |

- **1GR-FE head gaskets:**
  - Autotrader: "Head gasket failures on the 2003-2005 V6 4Runners are reported more than average".
  - Expedition Portal (Martinjmpr): the problems "were all in the 2003 - 2004 model years".
  - Wikipedia dates the revised gasket to late in the 2006 model year.
  - The old entry said "late in the 2006 model year" without giving the reason for the 2003–05 range. The avoid flag now covers 2003–05 and says what each source found.
- **2UZ V8 vs 1GR V6:**
  - HotCars: "the V8 facelift models from 2006 are better", and it calls out 2004–05.
  - Jalopnik says the 2UZ-FE has a reputation as "indestructible", with VVT-i from 2005.
  - **2006** is the sweet spot because it's the first V8 facelift year and the year the V6 gasket was revised.
- **2008 and 2009:** they were flagged for airbags and the Trail Edition, and no source recommends them, so both are now notes.
- **2005:** it's inside Autotrader's V6 head-gasket window, so it's now a note.
- **Buying check rewritten:** "V6 coolant loss".
- **FAQ rewritten:** "Which years should I look for?"
- Sources: [Autotrader](https://www.autotrader.com/car-shopping/buying-a-used-toyota-4runner-everything-you-need-to-know), [Expedition Portal: 4th gen buyer's guide thread](https://forum.expeditionportal.com/goto/post?id=1957098), [HotCars](https://www.hotcars.com/buying-4th-gen-toyota-4runner-what-to-know/), [Jalopnik: V8 4Runner](https://www.jalopnik.com/2184054/toyota-4runner-v8-desirable/), [Wikipedia: Toyota 4Runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_4Runner) (already listed)

| Year | Before | After |
|---|---|---|

| **2008** | buy | **note** |

## Ford Bronco

### Bronco II (1984–90)
| Year | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **1986** | note | **avoid** |
| **1987** | note | **avoid** |
| **1988** | buy | **note** |
| 1989 | buy | buy (reason added) |
| 1990 | buy | buy (reason added) |

- The Ranger Station:
  - 2.9 heads "were known to crack on the 1986-1988 models". They were redesigned for 1989 (the 89TM castings), "largely eliminating this specific failure".
  - The A4LD's "most problematic years" are 1985–87, and the builder's guide says "stay away from the 1985-1987 A4LD".
  - 1989-on A4LDs are "noticeably better".
- **1988 was wrong:** it still has the crack-prone heads.
- **New FAQ:** "Which years are the best to buy?"
- Sources: [Ranger Station: builder's guide](https://www.therangerstation.com/bronco-ii-corral/ford-bronco-ii-builders-guide/), [Ranger Station: 2.9L V6](https://www.therangerstation.com/tech/ford-ranger-2-9l-v-6-engines/), [Ranger Station: A4LD](https://www.therangerstation.com/tech/ford-ranger-a4ld-automatic-transmission/)
