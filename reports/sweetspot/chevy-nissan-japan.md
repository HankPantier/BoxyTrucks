Summary: 19 flags changed. Most of the removed `buy` flags marked "something new arrived this year" (a V6, a facelift, a new engine) and had no buyer source behind them. Only flags with a buyer, specialist, forum or complaint-data source behind them were kept. No timeline entries were added. Six "Which years…" FAQs were added and four were rewritten.

Sources I couldn't use directly: ck5.com, hagerty.com, carsguide.com.au, jdmbuysell.com and whatcar.com block curl with the BoxyTrucksBot UA (Cloudflare/CloudFront 403). I read them through WebFetch after a search surfaced the URL. I didn't use What Car?.

### y60 (GQ) — TD42 vs RD28
| Year | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1992 | buy | note |
| 1993 | avoid | **note** (reworded) |

- Reason: no source picks a GQ year. The consensus is about the engine. 4X4 Australia says the TD42 was the tourers' favourite for "legendary reliability", and the RD28T had "shortcomings" (less torque, lighter gearbox). NissanPatrol.com.au members say the TD42 fetches a premium, with the TB42 next and the RD28 "under powered but reliable". Wikipedia says the RD28T and TB42 suffer head-gasket trouble when driven hard. Westside Auto (a Perth dealer) calls the RD28T underpowered for towing. On the August 1993 lightening, Wikipedia says it weakened the TD42, but NissanPatrol.com.au owners are unsure ("stronger ones (I think)" against "black tops are still mighty strong"). There are no documented failures of stock black-tops, so it's a note, not Beware. The Series II (1992) has no buyer source.
- Page: added the FAQ "Which years are the best to buy?" (choose by engine: the best-kept TD42 manual).
- Sources: 4X4 Australia buyers guide (https://www.4x4australia.com.au/reviews/nissan-patrol-history), Westside Auto (https://www.westsideauto.com.au/news/nissanpatrol-used-car-buyers-guide), NissanPatrol.com.au t-9310 (https://www.nissanpatrol.com.au/forums/archive/index.php/t-9310.html), t-23081 (https://www.nissanpatrol.com.au/forums/archive/index.php/t-23081.html), Wikipedia Nissan Patrol / TD engine (already listed).

### y61 (GU) — ZD30 early vs common rail
| Year | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1999 | buy | buy (reason added: CarsGuide calls the TD42 turbo-diesel "the pick of the Patrol engines from this era") |
| 2000 | avoid | avoid (reworded: early ZD30, 2000–2003 per Westside) |
| 2003 | buy | buy (reworded: 4X4 Australia on TD42Ti reliability; Westside names the 2005–07 TD42T ST/ST-L "the sweet spot"; dropped the unsourced "most sought-after") |
| 2007 | note | note (reworded: no clean ZD30 cut-off) |

- ZD30 finding: there's no agreed fix year. Westside Auto says 2000–2003 engines had the "grenading" reputation and that the 2007-on common-rail was "improved". CarsGuide (David Morley) was still answering piston-failure questions on 2008 and 2009 ZD30s in 2022 and 2025. One NissanPatrol.com.au member says the early CRDs "have a few little issues". 4X4 Australia lists the 2004 ZD30 upgrades, and Wikipedia credits the GU IV refresh with fixing the VGT trouble. So the avoid stays on the 2000 introduction, and 2007 stays a note.
- Page: rewrote the FAQ "Is the ZD30 really that bad, and which years were fixed?" and added "Which years are the best to buy?" (TD42 years; wary of 2000–03 ZD30s; CarSwitch's 2008–10 Gulf petrol pick).
- Sources: Westside Auto, CarSwitch (https://carswitch.com/newsroom/nissan-patrol-used-car-buyers-guide-uae-2026-best-model-years-price-ranges-desert-ready-checks/), CarsGuide Patrol problems Q&A (https://www.carsguide.com.au/nissan/patrol/problems), CarsGuide ZD30 guide (https://carsguide.com.au/adventure/advice/zd30-patrol-your-guide-to-the-nissan-patrol-zd30-engine-81186), 4X4 Australia buyers guide, NissanPatrol.com.au t-35371 (https://www.nissanpatrol.com.au/forums/archive/index.php/t-35371.html).

## Mitsubishi Pajero

### pajero-gen1
- 1988: buy → note. JDM Buy Sell's L040 guide makes no best-year claim, and nothing else recommends years. Its source entry was already listed.

### pajero-gen2
| Year | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1993 | buy | note |
| 1998 | note | **avoid** (NADI driver airbag) |

- Reason: JDM Buy Sell's V20 guide has no best-year claim, and owners argue about engines (the 3.5 is interference with no belt clearance; the 4M40 has a chain but some head trouble). NHTSA 20V035000 covers 1998–2000 US Monteros: NADI inflators can absorb moisture and rupture or under-inflate, and the remedy is a new inflator. In Australia, Mitsubishi is buying back NL Pajeros built 31 May 1996 – 30 Sep 2000 because no replacement exists (Unsealed 4X4).
- Page: added the FAQ "Which years are the best to buy?".
- Sources: NHTSA recall API 20V035000 (https://api.nhtsa.gov/recalls/campaignNumber?campaignNumber=20V035000), Unsealed 4X4 NL buy-back (https://unsealed4x4.com.au/1996-2000-mitsubishi-pajero-nl-in-new-takata-airbag-recall-mitsubishi-offers-to-buy-back-vehicles/), JDM Buy Sell V20 (https://www.jdmbuysell.com/learn/mitsubishi/pajero/v20/).

## Isuzu Trooper

### trooper-1
- 1988 and 1989: buy → note. No buyer source recommends years. NHTSA complaint files for 1988–89 include cracked heads (by my count 2 and 1 complaints).
- Page: reworded the buying check "Know which engine you're getting". The 2.6 and V6 were called "the ones to want" and are now "the stronger engines", with the head-crack caveat.
- Sources: NHTSA complaint API (1988–89 already listed).

### trooper-2 — 3.5 oil consumption
| Year | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1996 | buy | note |
| 1998–2000 | avoid | avoid (reworded with data) |

- Reason: I counted NHTSA complaints whose text mentions oil use or seizure, by model year: 1998: 6/42, **1999: 38/81**, 2000: 15/49, 2001: 3/29, 2002: 2/12, and 0–2 a year for 1992–97. CarComplaints rates 2000 the worst year, mainly for transmission failure. Nothing documents a fix for 2001–02, and their lower counts may just reflect lower sales, so the avoid wasn't extended or cut. On Overland Bound, one owner says the 1992–97 3.2 "never had an oil consumption issue". That's one owner, so it isn't enough for a 1996 buy.
- Page: added the FAQ "Which years are the best to buy?".
- Sources: Overland Bound (https://overlandbound.com/forums/threads/isuzu-trooper-owners.35168/post-506173), CarComplaints (https://www.carcomplaints.com/Isuzu/Trooper/), NHTSA complaints 2001 and 2002 (api.nhtsa.gov complaintsByVehicle), Edmunds forum (https://forums.edmunds.com/discussion/comment/446006).
