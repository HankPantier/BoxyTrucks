# Sweet spot / Beware audit — Land Cruiser group

Scope: 16 pages under `data/extra/land-cruiser/` (timelines) and `data/pages/land-cruiser/` (the 80 Series page is `data/land-cruiser.json` → `page`). Only `timeline` flags/changes, year FAQs, one 80 Series story sentence and `sources` were touched.

Legend: `buy` = Sweet spot, `avoid` = Beware.

## 80 Series (flagship)
| Entry | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1993 (FZJ80 arrives) | buy | **buy**: reworded to say the 1993–97 FZJ80 run is the one owners recommend and the 1995–97 changes are minor |
| 1997 (final US year) | buy | note |

- Why: IH8MUD owners consistently tell buyers to get a 1993–97 FZJ80 (1FZ-FE 212 hp vs the 3F-E's 155 hp, optional factory front/rear lockers, full-float rear with discs on ABS trucks). One member calls the 3F-E "a relic". In the '94 vs '95–97 thread, owners say "the big change came in 93". The 1995–97 changes are airbags, the new dash, OBD-II and the A343F, "not much else". No source singles out 1997 (or 1994) as better. The 1FZ-FE head gasket affects every FZJ80 year, so it isn't a reason to flag one year.
- Page fixes: the story sentence "enthusiasts treat 1994 as a sweet spot" was unsourced and contradicted the flags. I replaced it with the sourced "big change came in 1993" point. The FAQ "FJ80 or FZJ80 — which should I buy?" is now "Which years are the best to buy?", with sourced reasoning that also drops the unsourced "Collectors like 1994" line.
- Sources added: IH8MUD "FJ80 vs FZJ80" (https://forum.ih8mud.com/threads/fj80-vs-fzj80.1293684); IH8MUD "'94 vs '95-'97: Details?" (https://forum.ih8mud.com/threads/94-vs-95-97-details.91296); Unsealed 4X4 80 Series buying guide (https://unsealed4x4.com.au/how-to-buy-an-80-series-landcruiser-the-greatest-4x4-of-all-time/).

## 100 Series
| Entry | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1998–1999 rear locker | buy | note (reworded: off-roaders want it, but it comes with the weak front diff) |
| 1998–1999 two-pinion front diff | avoid | avoid (unchanged) |
| 2000 four-pinion diff | buy | note |
| **2003–2007 (new)** | — | **buy**: A750F five-speed, no-contact throttle sensor, VVT-i on 2006–07 |

- **Wrong flags:** 1998–99 was flagged both Sweet spot and Beware. 2000 was a Sweet spot only because "something changed that year".
- Why: IH8MUD's new-owner buying thread lists 2003–07 for the five-speed, and several owners there rate the gearbox a bigger step than the VVT-i's extra 40 hp. It also notes many reports of transmission trouble on 2001–02. On Expedition Portal one owner says "03-05 are THE years" and warns about the cost of the 2006–07 air-injection system. Caravan World and Wikipedia/Slee document the 1998–99 front-diff failures.
- FAQ "Which years should I buy?" is now "Which years are the best to buy?", rewritten to match.
- Sources added: https://forum.ih8mud.com/threads/new-100-owner-just-adding-to-my-stable.503870/ ; https://forum.expeditionportal.com/goto/post?id=1147765 ; https://caravanworld.com.au/blogs/tow-vehicle-reviews/buying-used-toyota-landcruiser-100-series

## 200 Series
| Entry | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 2013 first facelift | note | **buy** (2013–15) |
| **2015–16 (new)** | — | **avoid**: diesel markets only, DPF arrives on the 1VD-FTV, with a Toyota DPF recall on 2015–16 |
| 2016 second facelift | buy | note |

- **Wrong flag:** 2016 (eight-speed) was a Sweet spot, but no source recommends it over earlier trucks. One IH8MUD owner says the eight-speed didn't improve fuel economy much, and CarGurus gives no year preference.
