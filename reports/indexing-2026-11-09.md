# Search Console page indexing, Nov 9, 2026

- **Indexed: 784 pages. Not indexed: 619.** Impressions are climbing (about 1.5K/day at the right of the chart).
- **Discovered – currently not indexed (590):** Google knows about these pages but hasn't crawled them yet. That's normal for a young site with about 1,400 URLs, and it clears as links and authority grow.
- **Crawled – currently not indexed (21, validation failed Nov 6):** Google crawled these pages and chose not to index them. They're mostly the per-truck sub-pages: tires, years, problems, specs, plus a few repair guides, including the five pages requested on Nov 7. Likely cause: each sub-page repeats content that is already on the generation guide. On the 80 Series, 12 of 22 sentences on /tires/ and 30 of 59 on /problems/ also appear word for word on the 8,400-word guide page, so Google treats the sub-page as a copy.
- **Server error (5xx) (1):** /models/g-class/w463/tires/ was a one-off; it returns 200 now.
- **Noindex (1):** /search/, on purpose.
- **Alternate page with proper canonical (3):** /tires/?t=… tool URLs that point to the real tires pages, as intended.
- **Page with redirect (3):** old URL forms redirecting to the canonical ones, as intended.

## Changes made
- The indexing queue now puts the 145 generation guide and compare pages first. Indexing requests for sub-pages that copy the guide don't stick.

## Recommended (needs Hank's OK: structural)
Make each sub-page the only home for its topic. On the guide page, replace the full tires, problems and years sections with a short summary (2–3 lines plus the key numbers) and a link to the sub-page, so the guide stops repeating them. Then give each sub-page something the guide doesn't have: owner fitment reports and wheel offsets on tires, the repair cost table on problems, the full year-by-year table on years. When that ships, revalidate "Crawled – currently not indexed".
