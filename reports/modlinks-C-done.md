## Replacements, removals and other notes
- **Expedition Motor Company, RESTORATION entries (g500 flagship, 500ge, europa, g550)**: EMC restores only its own ex-military 250GD Wolfs, and its FAQ says "Can you restore my G-Wagen? No can do." All four were replaced with Alliance Motorworks (https://alliancemotorworks.com/pages/services), a G-Wagon repair, service and custom-build shop in Loveland CO that runs Nothing But G's. I found no W463 shop with a page that explicitly says "restoration", so the `why` on these pages ("sympathetic restorations") is a slightly stronger claim than this link supports. It's worth a look.
- **EMC on w461 (RESTOMOD WOLFS)**: kept, and pointed at their Wolf builder configurator.
- **ARB (defender-90-110, LOCKERS & DIFFS)**: replaced. arb.com.au has no Land Rover vehicle pages. Lucky8's Defender lockers collection lists the ARB Air Locker for Land Rover plus Detroit and Ashcroft units.
- **Richards Chassis**: richardschassis.co.uk returns 403 to every request, so it couldn't be checked. On **series**, I kept the Richards brand through Paddock Spares' Richards Chassis page, which lists a Series 3 galvanised chassis. On **defender-90-110**, I replaced it with LR Direct's Defender chassis & outriggers listing, which includes a 110 galvanised chassis. LR Direct also has the Richards Defender 90 chassis as DA2056 (https://www.lrdirect.com/da2056-chassis-2-5d-td-200tdi) if you'd rather keep the brand.
- **GwagenParts.com (500ge, W463 PARTS)**: replaced with Four By Four Club W463 parts (300GE/G320/G500). GwagenParts has nothing for 1990s W463s.
- **General Tire (defender-l663, ALL-TERRAIN TYRES)**: removed. No General Tire page names the Defender, and BFGoodrich still covers the category. The `why` still mentions the AT3. If you'd rather keep a link, the GB AT3 product page is https://www.generaltire-tyres.com/gb/en/suv-4x4/tyres/grabber-at3/ (it names Discovery, not Defender).
- **Safety Devices**: 
  - *defender-90-110*: safetydevices.com's own Defender pages list only racks, bumpers and Frontier bars. Its roll cages are now sold through dealers, so I linked the Paddock Spares Safety Devices page, which lists 20+ Defender 90/110 cages. I counted this as a tightened link because the supplier is unchanged.
  - *series*: Safety Devices has no Land Rover Series I–III pages (its "Series 1/2/3" pages are Discovery generations). I linked "Request A Roll Cage" (https://safetydevices.com/request-a-roll-cage/) and added "Series cages are built to order." to the `why`.
- **Front Runner**: frontrunneroutfitters.com now redirects to dometic.com, so all links use Dometic's Front Runner pages:
  - W460, W461 and pre-2018 W463 pages: the Slimline II G-Class rack (1979–2017, spec says 1990–2018).
  - second-gen (W463A): the 2018–current rack.
  - g63-6x6 and lr3: the vehicle listings, which cover racks and ladders.
  - The pre-2018 G ladder is a separate page: https://www.dometic.com/en-us/product/mercedes-gelandewagen-g-class-ladder-lamg003 (it doesn't fit 2018+).
- **Tom Wood's (p38)**: tomwoods.com returns 403. I linked Lucky8's P38 driveshafts collection, which lists Tom Wood's TWDC-P38F and P38R double-cardan shafts.
- **Four By Four Club (w461)**: the site has no W461-specific index. I linked the W460/W461 electrical page, which names the W461 Wolf parts (24 V-era lights and so on). The W460 parts index (https://www.fourbyfourclub.com/W460Parts.htm) is broader but doesn't name the W461.
- **Compomotive (lr4)**: the PD1880 has been superseded by the PD1881, whose page lists Discovery 3/4 fitment. I renamed the entry.
- **Brabus (g63-6x6)**: Brabus no longer has a product page for the 700 6×6. I linked the 2010s history page, which describes the BRABUS 700 6×6 on the W463 G 63 6×6.
- **BFGoodrich (defender-l663)**: linked the Defender 110 fitment page (2020+ model years). A Defender 90 page also exists: https://www.bfgoodrichtires.com/auto/manufacturers/land-rover/defender-90.
- **Terrafirma**: links use the site's own filter (`?filter=25` Discovery 1, `26` Range Rover Classic, `27` P38, `29` Discovery 3), and I checked that the filtering happens on the server. For **LR3**, Terrafirma has no deep parts coverage, so I linked its LR3 suspension bush kits (14 items).
- **Pre-existing empty `who` lists (not on my list, left alone)**: WHEELS & TIRES in data/g-class.json, 4x4-squared, 500ge, europa, g55-amg and g550, plus PORTAL CONVERSIONS in 4x4-squared. These may be another agent's work in progress, but they break the "never leave a category with an empty `who`" rule.
