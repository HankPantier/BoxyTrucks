## What "viable" takes ($1,700/month)
A rough model for a niche automotive guide site:

| Source | Needs | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliate (parts, tires, gear) | ~40,000 visits at roughly $10–15 earned per 1,000 visits | $400–600 |
| Sponsors (Family $250, founding $187.50) | 4–5 shops, which means 1,000+ visits/mo to their family's pages | $750–1,250 |
| Newsletter sponsor or install guides | 2,000+ subscribers, or one $400 install guide a month | $200–400 |

That's roughly **40,000 visits a month and 2,000 subscribers**. New sites usually take 6–12 months to get there through search, so the plan is built to compound.

## The three levers, in order
1. **Search authority (biggest lever).** Google won't index or rank most of our 1,393 pages until other sites link to us. Every real link moves the whole site up.
   - Link outreach follow-ups (Nov 6).
   - **Inspector listings:** we list about 30 pre-purchase inspection shops and networks for free. Telling each one "you're listed" invites a link or share back. These are easy, earned links.
   - Get quoted as an expert: sign up at Qwoted and Featured (Hank) and answer "best used 4x4" journalist requests.
   - Forum and Reddit participation (weekly helper from Nov 9).
   - Free tools earn links on their own: the tire fitment tool and "Which 4x4?". Mention them where people ask.
2. **Indexing and click-through.** Daily batches of 10 indexing requests, meta descriptions that show the verdict, and homepage links into every best-years page (done Nov 3: the "Years to avoid, truck by truck" block).
3. **Owned audience.** Newsletter signups on the best-years pages (done Nov 2). Send Dispatch No. 01. Cross-post daily to 5 platforms. Use Hank's own photos and repairs as the content nobody else has.
