# Owner-photo outreach

These pages still use street, show or dealer photos because no free-to-use off-road photo of that generation exists. The creators below are already featured on the page, so they're the warmest asks. Send the message from your own Instagram or YouTube account (DM or the channel's business email).

## The message

```
Hi [name],

I run BoxyTrucks.com, a free, sourced guide to boxy 4x4s for owners who do their own work. Your [channel/account] is featured on our [MODEL] page (https://boxytrucks.com/models/[path]/) because it's one of the best [MODEL] resources out there.

Right now that page only has street or show photos, because we only use images we have permission for. Would you be willing to let us use one or two of your off-road shots of your [MODEL]? We'd credit you by name with a link back to your [channel/account] right under the photo, and you can ask us to take it down at any time.

If yes, just reply with the photo(s) or the post links you're happy for us to use, and the credit line you'd like.

Thanks either way, and keep the content coming.
Hank
BoxyTrucks.com
```

When someone says yes: forward me their reply and the photos. I'll add them with the credit line and keep their written permission on file (the email or DM counts).
