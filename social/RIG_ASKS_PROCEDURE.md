# Daily "Show us your rig" asks on Instagram

Hank, Nov 6, 2026: find nice-looking boxy-truck posts on Instagram and ask the owners to submit at boxytrucks.com/submit.
Asks can be comments on their posts (preferred: public, and they get seen) or DMs. **Every ask waits for Hank's approval**, then
Claude posts it from @boxytrucks.

## Daily run (3:11 pm ET)
1. Confirm the active Instagram account is **@boxytrucks** (stop if not; never switch).
2. Find 5 candidates from hashtags (#fj40, #80series, #fj60, #gwagon, #w463, #defender110, #landroverseries, #earlybronco,
   #internationalscout, #4runner, #lx470, #jeepcj, #nissanpatrol, #ineosgrenadier, #rangeroverclassic), rotating families.
   - Owners, not dealers or shops. Good photos of a clean or well-built truck. A post in the last ~6 weeks. Roughly 500–20K followers.
   - Never anyone already in the outreach tracker (doc ids `ig-<handle>`), never wrecks or crash content.
3. Look at the post and caption, then draft a short comment that names something specific about their truck, then:
   "We feature readers' rigs on boxytrucks.com and would love to show yours, credited to you. If you're up for it: boxytrucks.com/submit".
   No invented facts; only what their profile or caption shows.
4. Send Hank the 5 drafts (SendUserMessage) and wait for approval. Post only the approved ones.
5. Verify each posted on instagram.com/your_activity/interactions/comments. Stop for the day on any "Try Again Later" or restriction.
6. Log each in the outreach tracker as `ig-<handle>` (kind `rig-ask`, status Sent, followup_due +7 days). One ask per owner, no follow-ups.
7. When a submission arrives (email from "BoxyTrucks Rigs"), mark that row Replied and tell Hank.
