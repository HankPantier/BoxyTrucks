## Notes for future cross-posting
- Limit: 280 characters. Every link counts as 23 characters whatever its length. X also turns bare domains like "BoxyTrucks.com" into links, so those count as 23 too. Emoji count as 2.
- Instagram captions need cutting down by about two thirds. Keep the hook, two or three key facts, the link and the photo credit. Use 0–2 hashtags, not the IG hashtag block.
- Images: our 1080x1350 (4:5) IG images upload fine. The timeline shows them in full on desktop, though some views crop them. A safer single-image size for X is 1600x900 (16:9) or 1080x1080. Max 5 MB for JPG/PNG, and up to 4 images per post.
- Composer tip for automation: upload the image with file_upload, then click into the text box at its on-screen position before typing. A stale element ref from before the upload can drop the typed text. Don't use ctrl+a to clear the editor, because it desyncs X's editor. Reload the compose page instead.
- After posting, new accounts see an "Unlock more on X" info modal. It's safe to close with the X.
