**YouTube's title-pattern summary (61 videos, 60.1M views):**
1. Question hooks that start a debate or make people curious ("Who thought this design was a good idea?").
2. One specific engineering detail or obscure feature, not a general overview ("The engine in this Ford truck has 7 main bearings…").
3. Price tags or extreme contrast ("Cost-cutting on a $100,000 S-Class", "$25K Slate Truck").

**What it means for us:**
- Our Shorts titles are spec labels ("#20 Land Cruiser Prado 90 tire size: the biggest tire with no lift (265/75R16)"). Not one outlier is titled like that.
- Most outliers have a person on camera or real footage. Ours are photo slideshows with an AI voice.
- Our data fits patterns 1 and 3 well: the years-to-avoid verdicts, the complaint counts and now the market report's prices.
- The spec-comparison Short with a question title (>100x) is basically our spec stack. We stopped posting stacks on Instagram because they were stills, but as a Short with a "which one would you buy?" title they fit what's working.
