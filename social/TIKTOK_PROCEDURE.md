# TikTok (@boxytrucks), on every Reel day

Hank's rule (Nov 2, 2026): every day we post anywhere else, we also post to Pinterest, YouTube and TikTok. TikTok is video-only, so it gets each Reel on its Instagram date. On single and carousel days, post the next Reel that isn't on TikTok yet; once all are up, skip the day.

1. Open https://www.tiktok.com/profile and confirm the URL is **@boxytrucks**. If it's anything else, stop and tell Hank. Never switch accounts.
2. Open https://www.tiktok.com/tiktokstudio/upload, `find` the file input and `file_upload` the item's `video`. Every MP4 must be under 10 MB (the browser-upload limit); `make_reel.js` caps the bitrate.
3. If an "automatic content checks" box appears, click Cancel; that's a settings change for Hank to make. Close any feature tips.
4. **Description:** click the editor, select all and delete the filename, then type the Instagram caption without its hashtag block. Type 3–4 hashtags one at a time, each followed by a space. Check the end of the text for any leftover filename and delete it.
5. **Who can see:** Everyone. Leave the AI-generated label off (real photos). Click Post.
6. In TikTok Studio → Posts, a new post shows "Content under review" with **Only me**. Once the review clears, set privacy to **Everyone** from the dropdown, and confirm it shows Everyone.
7. Get the `/video/<id>` URL from https://www.tiktok.com/@boxytrucks, then set `tt_status: "posted"`, `tt_posted` and `tt_url` on the queue item. Commit and push.

## Deal and promotional posts (Nov 7, 2026, optimization plan item 3)
The Eurowise reader-deal post (Nov 4) is public but got 0 views in three days, while every other post got 30–260: TikTok holds back
promotional posts. Rules from now on:
- Keep codes and "% off" out of TikTok posts. Make the post useful first (a buying check, a fit, a fix) and mention a deal only in a comment.
- If a post promotes a brand or a code, turn on **Content disclosure** (More options → "Disclose post content" → Your brand / Branded content) before posting, as TikTok's rules require.
- The Eurowise post stays up (deleting is Hank's call).
